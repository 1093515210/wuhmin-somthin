# Spy versus Spy versus Spy the CCP's security and espionage apparatus in the absence of central authority/諜戰重重：中央失序下中共安全與情報機構

  ## Executive Summary/執行摘要
When united and at peace, China has almost always had emperors or emperor-like leaders who strongly grasped authority and competently handled politics and the challenges of national leadership. They were traditionally considered blessed from above with a Mandate of Heaven bestowed upon them because they worked in the interests of the people and maintained stability and peace. At other times, Chinese governments have struggled to maintain central authority, most recently during the Warlord Period (1916–28) and the first two years of the Cultural Revolution decade (1966–76).
<P>	當中國處於統一與和平狀態時，幾乎歷代皆由掌握強大權威、能勝任政治與國家領導挑戰的皇帝或類皇帝式領導人統治。傳統上，這些領導人被視為獲得「天命」的眷顧，因為他們施行於人民利益並維持了社會的穩定與和平。而在其他時期，中國政府則常面臨難以維持中央權威的困境，最近的例子包括軍閥時期（1916–28）以及文化大革命初期（1966–76）的前兩年。<P>
If the central authority of the ruling CCP deteriorates in the near future, the precedents of these recent periods may influence how personnel in the party-state’s internal security and espionage apparatus respond. These precedents will especially influence those based away from Beijing in China’s provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions (hereafter, “provinces and municipalities”). At these local levels, the party-state has organized its nonmilitary coercive power in provincial and municipal state security bureaus (SSBs) and public security bureaus (PSBs) that focus, respectively, on foreign threats including espionage and on law enforcement.
<P>	若執政的中共中央權威在不久的將來發生衰退，上述時期的歷史先例可能會影響黨國體制內部的安全與情報機構人員之應對方式。這些先例將特別影響那些駐紮於北京以外，分布在中國各省、直轄市與自治區（以下簡稱「省市」）的人員。在地方層級，黨國體制將其非軍事強制權力組織於省、市級的「國家安全局」（SSB）與「公安局」（PSB）之中，前者分別專注於包含間諜活動在內的外部威脅，後者則專注於執法。<P>
Besides being subordinate to their ministries in Beijing, these bureaus also answer to their local CCP committee, the ultimate authority in their area. Understanding the place of provincial and municipal SSBs and PSBs in the party-state can help policymakers envision how these organs of state security might react to a decline of central authority and how to think about US policy options in response.
<P>	除了隸屬於北京的部委之外，這些局處亦須向其地方中共委員會負責，而地方委員會即是該區域的最高權威。理解省、市級國安局與公安局在黨國體制中的地位，有助於決策者預判這些安全機構在中央權威衰落時可能做出的反應，並思考美國應對的政策選項。<P>
Policy proposals at the end of this chapter are as follows:
<P>	本章末尾提出的政策建議如下：<P>
•Avoid the appearance of imperialist interference. Prepare to assist localities in China only if they request it.
<P>	避免呈現帝國主義干涉之姿態。 僅在地方政府提出請求時，才準備提供協助。<P>
•Prepare ahead for a possible decline in central authority by building up information on key provincial and municipal personalities, including psychological evaluations.
<P>	預先為中央權威可能衰落的情況做準備。 建立關鍵省市要員的資訊檔案，包括心理評估。<P>
•Study, in advance, the actual effect of the CCP’s massive surveillance programs on people in selected provinces and municipalities. Publicize the results on Chinese language news and media platforms.
<P>	預先研究中共大規模監控計畫對特定省市人民的實際影響。 並將研究結果發布於中文新聞與媒體平台。<P>
•Publicize, in advance, the story of how Germany, Portugal, and the Baltic states neutralized their secret police organs and opened their files for public inspection.
<P>	預先宣傳德國、葡萄牙及波羅的海國家如何中立化其秘密警察機構，並公開其檔案供大眾查閱的故事。<P>
Prepare programs to assist Chinese provinces and municipalities to neutralize secret police and embrace transparency post-PRC.
<P>	準備相關計畫，以協助中國省市在中華人民共和國解體後，能中立化秘密警察並擁抱透明化。<P>

  ## Nothing Lasts Forever: How the Nature of Chinese Society Affects Policy Options/永恆不存在：中國社會本質如何影響政策選項
Chinese society has widely embraced the Mandate of Heaven concept (天命, Tiān mìng, hereafter “the Mandate”). Understanding it can help the United States and its allies predict how a collapse of central authority could affect workers at the local MSS and MPS bureaus, that is, at the SSBs and PSBs.
<P>	中國社會廣泛接受「天命」（Tiān mìng）的概念。理解此概念，有助於美國及其盟友預測中央權威的崩潰將如何影響地方國安局（MSS）與公安部（MPS）分支機構（即各省市的國安局與公安局）的工作人員。<P>
The Mandate is worth examining from the perspective of a popular Chinese source to ponder how people there might actually see it. Baidu (baike.baidu.com) is China’s most popular search engine and also carries a Wikipedia-like reference website that the population carefully edits and widely reads. Should people in China begin wondering whether the CCP still possesses enough legitimacy to rule, Baidu will be a first stop for many seeking to refresh their understanding of the Mandate, which retains credibility despite its antiquity.
<P>	從中國本土流行來源的角度來檢視「天命」，有助於揣摩當地人的實際看法。百度（baike.baidu.com）是中國最受歡迎的搜尋引擎，亦擁有類似維基百科的參考網站，由民眾謹慎編輯並廣泛閱讀。若中國人民開始質疑中共是否仍具備足夠的統治合法性，百度將成為許多人尋求重新理解「天命」的首選站點，儘管該概念古老，卻仍保有其公信力。<P>
Selected for brevity below are portions of Baidu’s explanation of the Mandate, translated as “the will of heaven” from the Chinese. Note that the idea applies to the lives and fates of ordinary people as well as to those of the emperor:
<P>	以下節錄百度對「天命」的解釋（譯自中文），請注意此概念不僅適用於皇帝，亦適用於平民的生與死[具体内容请访问百度词条] (https://baike.baidu.com/item/%E5%A4%A9%E5%91%BD/4747?svcp_stk=1_9rrEbRLN5_3jXv7T0ZwSeFTFfXaXMZKDhOqtw9Sw4EvMcb1pczaI7YdYcAzvFq9Cmex94z6P6xSc2KvSsXyux_NQwwqRqTo-2pPvd9lx8EP9l8mgOdp1Th0DAleQJ4VXPt3kOb55SZ9PVI5fC54rzWtoDSbTXeTF4A9MbD9yO7A_QG--IdrR-Z-f5DIFNbYx) ：<P>
The will of heaven; also refers to the destiny of people under the sovereignty of heaven. . . . The Records of the Five Emperors: “Then Emperor Yao grew old and ordered Shun to take charge of the administration of the son of heaven in order to observe the will of heaven.”
<P>	天命；亦指受天支配之人的命運……《五帝傳》：「堯帝老，命舜司天子之政，以觀天命。」<P>
Xu Chi, The Night of Carnival: “As if the sun must come out from the east and go down from the west, the peasants believe that their miserable life is the fate of heaven and the law of nature.”
<P>	徐遲，《狂歡之夜》：「猶如日出於東、落於西，農民相信其悲慘生活乃天命與自然法則。」<P>
Another important part of the idea, also from the same Baidu entry, is that a decline in the emperor’s authority is not necessarily a sign that he has lost Tiān mìng: 
<P>	該概念的另一個重要面向（同樣出自百度條目）在於：皇帝權威的衰退並不必然代表其已喪失天命：<P>
In ancient times, the power of the ruler was divinely authorized, and the ruler claimed to be commanded by heaven, which was called the Mandate of Heaven. Zuo Zhuan (左传-宣公三年): “Although Zhou’s virtue has declined, the Mandate of Heaven has not changed, and the weight of the tripod has not been questioned.”
<P>	古代君主之權力由神授，君主聲稱受命於天，即稱為天命。《左傳·宣公三年》：「雖周德衰，天命不改，鼎重不疑。」<P>
Baidu’s definitions are almost certainly subject to the scrutiny of the company’s CCP committee and therefore of the party itself, so inclusion of that last passage is not surprising. It gives the party an extension of sorts to maintain a claim to the Mandate even if the population perceives a decline in Beijing’s authority. However, the regime would probably face increasing pressure against maintaining a claim to the Mandate if central authority significantly weakens. The right to govern in this concept is not perpetual, unlike the European idea of the divine right of kings, which allowed rule despite immorality or incompetence.
<P>	百度的定義幾乎肯定受到該公司中共委員會（進而受黨本身）的審查，因此包含最後這段話並不令人意外。這為黨提供了一種延伸性的辯護，使其即便在民眾感知到北京權威衰落時，仍能維持對天命的宣稱。然而，若中央權威顯著削弱，政權在維持天命宣稱上將面臨日益增長的壓力。在此概念中，統治權並非永恆，這與歐洲「君權神授」的概念不同——後者允許君主即便在不道德或無能的情況下仍能繼續統治。<P>
The first ruler of the Zhou Dynasty (1046–256 BC) conceived the Mandate to justify overthrowing the tyrannical and destructive ruler of the previous Shang regime. The Zhou Dynasty leaders claimed the Shang had lost authority because their last emperor was no longer working for the benefit of the people. God would withdraw authority from a tyrannical emperor, and the Chinese people had the duty to resist such a ruler.
<P>	周朝（西元前 1046–256 年）的開國君主提出「天命」概念，旨在為推翻前朝商朝那暴虐且具破壞性的君主提供正當性。周朝天子聲稱商朝已喪失權威，因為其末代皇帝不再為人民謀利。天意會從暴君手中收回權力，而华夏人民有義務反抗此類統治者。<P>
If the popular perception that the regime in Beijing was no longer working for the people’s benefit accompanied a sudden decline in central authority, some local authorities, particularly those who are armed, might see it as their duty to resist the regime or might do so for opportunistic reasons. Some might fail while others succeed.
<P>	若「北京政權已不再為人民謀利」的民間觀感，伴隨著中央權威的突然衰落，部分地方當局（特別是武裝力量）可能會將其視為反抗政權的義務，或是出於投機動機而採取行動。有些人可能會失敗，而有些人則會成功。<P>
This is where the provincial and municipal SSBs and PSBs become relevant, as would the military region-based units of the PLA and its subordinate force, the PAP. An accessible example of how a previous Chinese government declined and fell, and how the coercive forces responded, is the end of the Qing Dynasty (1644–1911).
<P>	這正是省、市級國安局與公安局，以及基於軍區的解放軍部隊及其下屬武警（PAP）介入的關鍵點。一個關於前中國政府如何衰落與垮台，以及強制力量如何應對的典型案例，便是清朝（1644–1911）的終結。<P>

  ## The Challenge of Central Control/中央控制的挑戰
The decline of the Qing was different than it might be for the PRC, but provides an example that would come to the minds of many in China if central authority should decline. Before the declaration of the Republic of China on January 1, 1912, foreign aggression plagued the Qing Dynasty for over 70 years, beginning with the First Opium War (1839–42). During the Qing’s last three decades ending in 1911, Beijing pursued modernization schemes that were only partly successful and failed to transform the economy, reform the military, or curb rampant corruption.  
<P>	清国的衰落與中華人民共和國可能發生的情況有所不同，但若中央權威衰落，這將是許多中國人腦中浮現的範例。在 1912 年 1 月 1 日中華民國成立之前，清国遭受了長達 70 多年的外國侵略，始於第一次鴉片戰爭（1839–42）。在清国結束前的最後三十年裡，京师推行的現代化方案僅取得部分成功，未能轉型經濟、改革軍隊或遏制猖獗的腐敗。<P>
When the Qing finally fell, Chinese elites—social, intellectual, bureaucratic, or military—were divided in their visions of how to save China from chaos, recover lost territory, and regain national sovereignty. Regional differences in language and culture may have contributed to that division, less perhaps among intellectuals and the Chinese Nationalist Party than among the armed and more dangerous military men left over from the Qing Dynasty’s army. Due in part to this lack of unity, the Warlord Period (1916–28) followed, splitting the country into armed satrapies loyal only to their local leader.
<P>	當清国最終垮台時，中國的精英階層——無論是社會、知識、官僚或軍事精英——對於如何救中國於水火、收復失地及重獲國家主權，持有不同的願景。語言與文化的地域差異可能加劇了這種分裂，這種分裂在武裝力量與更具危險性的清軍殘餘軍官之間，可能比在知識分子或中國國民黨人之間更為明顯。由於缺乏統一，隨之而來的是軍閥時期（1916–1928），將國家分裂為僅效忠於地方首領的武裝割據勢力。<P>
By contrast, the CCP regime has been far more successful in resisting foreign opponents, modernizing its armed forces, and building perhaps the strongest economy in Chinese history. It has cultivated elites of different kinds at different stages of its short history. At each juncture, these various elites, from the worker-peasant-soldier ideal in the 1950s to the more expert cadres and businesspeople of today, have populated leadership roles and strengthened the CCP’s grip on society. They have also been the targets of numerous purges, leading to widespread cynicism about politics as a vocation. However, central control has always been challenging even to the CCP, including under Mao Zedong himself, who complained of “mountaintops,” cliques within the party whose loyalty centered on senior communists other than Mao.
<P>	相比之下，中共政權在抵禦外敵、軍隊現代化以及建立中國歷史上最強大的經濟體方面，取得了遠為成功的成就。它在短暫的歷史各階段中，培養了不同類型的精英。從 1950 年代的工農兵理想，到今日更具專業素養的幹部與企業家，這些精英填補了領導職位並強化了中共對社會的掌控。他們同時也是多次政治清洗的目標，導致民眾對從事政治工作普遍持憤世嫉俗的態度。然而，中央控制對中共而言始終是一項挑戰，即便是在毛澤東時期，他也曾抱怨黨內存在「山頭」——即忠誠對象為毛澤東以外高層共產黨員的派系。<P>
China’s large population, geographic size, and cultural diversity still contribute to this difficulty. Pervasive corruption persists and harms the application of central authority in local areas. Xi Jinping’s government has dented but not eliminated corruption in part because the CCP is the sole national authority but houses much corrupt activity. There is no institution independent of the party to solve waste, fraud, and abuse.
<P>	中國龐大的人口、廣大的地理面積與文化多樣性，仍是造成此困難的原因。普遍存在的腐敗持續損害著中央權威在地方的執行力。習近平政府雖削弱但未根除腐敗，部分原因是中共雖為唯一的國家權威，卻同時容納了大量的腐敗活動。缺乏獨立於黨之外的機構來解決浪費、欺詐與濫用問題。<P>
From afar, the CCP regime seems large and in charge, hardly at risk of a sudden downfall and decline into another warlord era. But Beijing’s policies risk sowing popular dissatisfaction even as they boost the hard edges of authoritarian control. Beijing has subjected nongovernmental and non-party civil society or ganizations to attack for over a decade. CCP supremo Xi Jinping relentlessly pursues total personal control, saying that the party, with him as its core should lead everything. The MPS, under Xi’s direction, has instituted a massive monitoring system that average people now realize can track their every move, establishing a surveillance baseline that is far higher than at the start of Xi’s tenure as CCP general secretary. Even compared to Mao’s time, with its pervasive neighborhood committees and other methods of personal control, the surveillance of today is extreme and all-encompassing.
<P>	從遠處看，中共政權顯得龐大且掌控全局，似乎不太可能面臨突然垮台並陷入另一個軍閥時代。然而，北京的政策在強化威權控制的同時，也面臨著播種民怨的風險。十多年來，北京持續打壓非政府與非黨派的公民社會組織。中共最高領導人習近平不懈追求絕對的個人控制，主張黨（以他為核心）應領導一切。在習近平的指導下，公安部建立了一個龐大的監控系統，普通民眾現在意識到其一舉一動皆被追蹤，這建立了一個遠高於習近平擔任總書記初期時的監控基準。即便與毛澤东時代遍布的鄰里委員會等個人控制手段相比，今日的監控程度亦是極端且無孔不入的。<P>
As a result of these policies and technological advances, the provinces and large municipalities have far more coercive power over their own populations than before Xi’s ascendance. Because the localities are ultimately responsible for the stability of their local areas, they wield their power somewhat independently, accountable to the center mostly for maintaining “stability.” If central power suddenly declined, some local authorities and their SSBs and PSBs might perceive an opportunity to go their own way or make another choice that does not involve staying loyal to the center.
<P>	由於這些政策與技術進步，各省與大型直轄市對其自身人口擁有的強制權力，遠比習近平掌權前更強大。由於地方政府最終需對其區域的穩定負責，他們行使權力時具有一定的獨立性，對中央的責任主要僅限於維持「穩定」。若中央權力突然衰落，部分地方當局及其國安局與公安局，可能會視之為自行其是，或做出不效忠中央的選擇之機會。<P>
Provincial and municipal SSBs and PSBs alone are not auto matically dominant in their areas because military units have superior firepower. But the nature of their missions makes them capable of, and practiced at, quick, violent action. That ability could become crucial even in the face of superior PLA and PAP forces if, for example, the local CCP committee, alongside the SSB and the PSB, decided to decapitate the regional military command and attempt to take control amid paralysis in Beijing. Anticipating how such local scenarios would end requires a thorough understanding of the players and their motivations. That in turn requires the sort of continual study and monitoring recommended in the final part of this chapter.
<P>	省、市級國安局與公安局本身並不必然在該區域佔據主導地位，因為軍事部隊擁有更強大的火力。但其任務性質使他們具備且習慣於進行快速、暴力的行動。如果地方中共委員會與國安局、公安局共同決定「斬首」區域軍事指揮部，並試圖在北京陷入癱瘓時奪取控制權，這種能力即便面對優勢的解放軍與武警部隊也可能變得至關重要。預判此類地方情境的走向，需要對參與者及其動機有透徹的理解，而這正需要本章末尾所建議的持續研究與監測。<P>

  ## Caveat Emperor/警惕皇帝：權威的脆弱性
Recall that China emerged from thousands of years of rule by an emperor only a bit more than one hundred years ago. The tradition of an unchallenged ruler has, at least to a degree, continued. In 1945, Mao Zedong was criticized for setting himself up as the founding emperor of a future non-dynastic regime after the publication of his poem “Snow” in a leftist newspaper in the Nationalist Chinese wartime capital, Chongqing. In his poem, Mao showed himself to be anything but modest:
<P>	請記住，中國僅在一百多年前才從長達數千年的皇帝統治中走出來。這種「不受挑戰的統治者」傳統，至少在某種程度上一直延續至今。1945年，毛澤東在國民黨抗戰時期臨時首都重慶的一份左翼報紙上發表詩作《沁園春·雪》後，遭到批評將自己塑造成未來非王朝體制的「開國皇帝」。在他的詩中，毛澤東表現得一點也不謙虛：<P>
<P>	北國風光，千里冰封，萬里雪飄。<P>
<P>	望長城內外，惟余莽莽；大河上下，頓失滔滔。(余 通：餘)<P>
<P>	山舞銀蛇，原馳蠟象，欲與天公試比高。(原馳 原作：原驅)<P>
<P>	須晴日，看紅裝素裹，分外妖嬈。(紅裝 一作：銀裝)<P>
This land so rich in beauty Has made countless heroes bow in homage
<P>	江山如此多嬌，引無數英雄競折腰。（英文版从这里开始）<P>
But alas! Qin Shihuang and Han Wudi Lacked literary grace; The founding emperors of the Tang and Song Had little poetry in their souls;
<P>	惜秦皇漢武，略輸文采；唐宗宋祖，稍遜風騷。<P>
And Genghis Khan, Proud Son of Heaven for a Day, Could only shoot eagles, bow outstretched,
<P>	一代天驕，成吉思汗，只識彎弓射大雕。<P>
All are past and gone! For truly great men Look to this age alone.
<P>	俱往矣，數風流人物，還看今朝。<P>
Casting the party leader as a new emperor was a tactic of Mao but also of his opponents.183 After the victory Mao took this idea further, gradually building the infamous personality cult that enabled his dominance of the CCP and powered his effort to sweep parts of the party aside when he chose to reshape it during the Cultural Revolution.
<P>	將黨魁塑造成新皇帝，既是毛澤東的戰術，也是其對手的手段。在取得勝利後，毛澤東將此概念進一步推動，逐漸建立起惡名昭彰的個人崇拜，這使他得以掌控中共，並在文化大革命期間重塑黨組織時，有能力將黨內異己掃除。<P>
Simon Leys, whose critical assessment of Mao in the 1960s was prescient, wrote that Mao turned out to be quite apt at being an emperor with updated characteristics: “More than two thousand years of imperial tradition have created in the collective consciousness the constant need for a unique, supreme, quasi-mystical head; the shaky and brief Republican interlude did not succeed in providing any convincing substitute for this, and Mao knew shrewdly how to manipulate this traditional legacy to his own advantage.”
<P>	西蒙·萊斯（Simon Leys）在 1960 年代對毛澤東進行了具有預見性的批判性評估，他寫道，毛澤東證明自己非常適合作為一名具備現代特徵的皇帝：「兩千多年的帝國傳統，在集體意識中創造了對獨特、至高無上且具準神秘色彩之領導人的持續需求；共和國時期那動盪且短暫的插曲，未能提供任何令人信服的替代方案，而毛澤東精明地知道如何操弄這份傳統遺產來為自己謀利。」<P>
Mao’s success in casting himself as a founding emperor pro-longed that tradition. The resilient place in Chinese political culture for an emperor-like figure explains why Xi Jinping built a personality cult, proclaimed himself the party’s core, and promulgated his thought as a national reference after he accumulated enough power and jailed enough opponents through his relentless anti-corruption campaigns to discourage opposition.
<P>	毛澤東成功將自己塑造成開國皇帝，延長了這項傳統。在中國政治文化中，這種「類皇帝」形象具有強大的韌性，這解釋了為何習近平在累積足夠權力，並透過其持續不斷的反腐運動監禁了足夠多的對手以遏制反對聲音後，會建立個人崇拜、宣稱自己是「黨的核心」，並將其思想定為國家指導思想。<P>
However, Xi lacks one crucial ingredient: the prestige of his two most important predecessors, Mao Zedong and Deng Xiaoping. Mao was truly the indispensable man of the Chinese Communist Revolution. His decisions saved the Red Army from annihilation during the early 1930s and the Long March (1934–35).
<P>	然而，習近平缺乏一個關鍵要素：其兩位最重要的前輩——毛澤東與鄧小平所擁有的「威望」（prestige）。毛澤東是中國共產黨革命中真正不可或缺的人物。他在 1930 年代初期的決策使紅軍免於覆滅，並領導了長征（1934–35）。<P>
He preserved the party’s fighting strength during the anti-Japanese War (1937–45), overcame steep odds to win the Chinese Civil War (1946–49), and fought the Americans to a standstill in Korea (1950–53). He proved time and again that he held the keys to success, at least until the Great Famine and the Cultural Revolution. Even then, belief in Mao’s superior insight prevailed until his death.
<P>	他在抗日戰爭（1937–45）期間保存了黨的戰鬥力，在艱難困苦中贏得了國共內戰（1946–49），並在韓戰（1950–53）中與美國人僵持不下。他一次又一次地證明自己掌握著成功的鑰匙，至少在經歷大饑荒與文化大革命之前是如此。即便在那之後，人們對毛澤東卓越洞察力的信仰仍持續到他逝世為止。<P>
Only Mao was Mao. He commanded both awe and fear. Lin Biao’s son, who plotted to depose Mao, said that “the Chairman commands such high prestige that he need only utter one sentence to remove anybody he chooses.”186 General Luo Ruiqing for example, the former PLA chief and ex-minister of public security whom Mao jailed, made a point of traveling to Tiananmen Square to bow in honor of the chairman after his death in 1976.
<P>	唯有毛澤東是唯一的毛澤東。他同時令人敬畏與恐懼。企圖廢黜毛澤東的林彪之子曾說：「主席的威望如此之高，他只需說一句話，就能除掉他想除掉的任何人。」例如，前解放軍總參謀長、前公安部長羅瑞卿，在毛澤東於 1976 年逝世後，特意前往天安門廣場向主席鞠躬致敬。<P>
Deng Xiaoping’s prestige stemmed from his role as a CCP leader and PLA commissar during the revolution, particularly in the closing year of the Civil War and in the subjugation of Tibet the following year. Afterward, Mao considered him a rare and indispensable administrative talent for the party-state. Though Mao removed Deng from power in 1966 at the start of the Cultural Revolution, he thought of Deng as one of the most able in the party, saying in 1975 that Deng was a “good hand whether the subject was politics, military affairs, or literature.”188 In the post-Mao years, Deng commanded such prestige that final decisions on major issues went through him even though he held no formal offices.
<P>	鄧小平的威望源於他在革命時期作為中共領導人與解放軍政委的角色，特別是在內戰末期以及次年對西藏的統治。此後，毛澤東視他為黨國體制中罕見且不可或缺的行政人才。儘管毛在 1966 年文化大革命開始時撤職鄧，但他仍認為鄧是黨內最有能力的人之一，並在 1975 年表示，無論是政治、軍務還是文學，鄧都是個「好手」。在毛時代之後，鄧的威望極高，即便他並未擔任正式官職，重大議題的最終決策仍須經過他。<P>
Xi Jinping has accumulated many offices and functions, and all major decisions go through him. He commands fear, but with neither revolutionary prestige nor wartime achievements, does he command awe? To be fair, Xi has had no experience in war—yet.
<P>	習近平累積了許多官職與職能，所有重大決策皆由他定奪。他令人恐懼，但既缺乏革命威望，也缺乏戰時成就，他能否贏得敬畏？公平地說，習近平目前還沒有戰爭經驗——至少尚未如此。<P>
But his seeming adherence to Mao’s playbook for dominance in the CCP does not change his lack of the prestige Mao commanded. As the father of Communist China, the chairman could make huge blunders but maintain deference among the elite. The question becomes, How many blunders is too many for Xi?
<P>	然而，他表面上遵循毛澤東在中共內部的權力運作模式，卻無法改變他缺乏毛澤東式威望的事實。作為共產中國之父，主席可以犯下巨大的錯誤卻仍能維持精英階層的順從。問題在於：對習近平而言，犯多少錯誤才算太多？<P>

  ## Xi Jinping, the Failing Emperor?/習近平，失勢的皇帝？
The idea that Xi Jinping might be losing his grip on the Mandate grew more sensitive in 2023 after the party awarded him a third term with no subsequent limits. It seemed they would allow the leader to maintain lifetime rule over China like an emperor of old or like Chairman Mao did in recent times. As economic pressures including unemployment mounted from the direct and indirect effects of the COVID-19 lockdown and other setbacks, negative reactions on Chinese social media indicated a decline in the party’s “already imperiled mandate of heaven.”
<P>	隨著黨在 2023 年授予習近平第三個任期且不再設限，關於習近平可能正在失去「天命」掌控權的觀點變得更加敏感。這似乎意味著他們允許領導人像古代皇帝或近期的毛主席一樣，對中國實施終身統治。隨著因 COVID-19 封控及其他挫折帶來的失業等經濟壓力日益增加，中國社群媒體上的負面反應顯示，黨那「已處於危險中的天命」正在衰落。<P>
The regime is so sensitive about this idea that they habitually overreact to any suggestion that Xi’s legitimacy is slipping—just as they constantly overreact to even the smallest sign of opposition at home or abroad. This sensibility was apparent in October 2023 when the CCP required bookstores to withdraw a reprinted 2016 book and censored online discussion of it. The book’s new title was The Chongzhen Emperor: Diligent Ruler of a Failed Dynasty  （崇祯: 勤政的亡国君）。
<P>	該政權對此觀點極其敏感，對於任何暗示習近平合法性正在流失的跡象，他們都會慣性地過度反應——正如他們對國內外任何微小的反對信號都採取過度反應一樣。這種敏感性在 2023 年 10 月顯露無遺，當時中共要求書店下架一本 2016 年出版的重印書，並審查網路上的相關討論。該書的新書名為《崇禎：勤政的亡國君》。<P>
Chongzhen was the last emperor of the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644) who hung himself on the eve of Beijing’s fall to the forces of the next dynasty, the Qing. Chongzhen had famously purged senior officials and mismanaged his kingdom, a history that carries an inevitable comparison to Xi Jinping’s policies since his rise to power.191 Weibo censored discussion of the book online as well as searches for it, and the PRC mainland press did not cover the issue, though the Chinese language press outside the mainland wrote about it.192 One problem seems to have been the new blurb on the cover. As the semi-independent Hong Kong newspaper Ming Pao described it, the book “was previously published by the Forbidden City Press in 2016 under the title ‘Past Stories of Chongzhen.’ It has been marketed in the mainland for many years and was not banned there. In the new edition (2023), the cover introduction says: ‘You make mistakes one after another, and the more diligent you are, the more your country will be destroyed!’”
<P>	崇禎是明朝（1368–1644）的末代皇帝，他在北京被下一朝清軍攻破的前夕自殺。崇禎因清洗高層官員與治理不善而聞名，這段歷史不可避免地讓人聯想到習近平自掌權以來的政策。微博對該書的討論與搜尋進行了審查，中國大陸媒體也未對此進行報導，儘管大陸以外的中文媒體均有相關報導。其中一個問題似乎出在封面的新文案上。正如準獨立的香港媒體《明報》所述，該書「先前於 2016 年由故宮出版社以《崇禎往事》為名出版，已在內地銷售多年，並未被禁。但在新版（2023年）中，封面序言寫道：『你接連不斷地犯錯，且越是勤政，國家就毀滅得越快！』」<P>

### At the Grassroots: The Security Services in the Provinces and Their Officers/基層層級：省級安全部門及其官員
To briefly review, the MSS and MPS are organized across China such that most personnel in each agency are based in the prov inces and municipalities under SSBs and PSBs. This follows the pattern of CCP control from the center, establishing party committees from the provinces downward to the local level, each answerable to the next one up. The local CCP committee is the ultimate authority in its area, and all government departments, including the SSBs and PSBs, are subordinate to it. This means SSBs and PSBs report upward in two directions: to their local CCP committee and to their own ministries.
<P>	簡要回顧，國安局（MSS）與公安部（MPS）在中國的組織架構是：各機構的大多數人員都駐紮在省、市級的國安局（SSB）與公安局（PSB）中。這遵循了黨從中央到地方的控制模式，建立從省級向下延伸至基層的黨委，每一級都向上一級負責。地方黨委是該區域的最高權威，所有政府部門（包括國安局與公安局）皆隸屬於其之下。這意味著國安局與公安局向兩個方向匯報：一是向其地方黨委，二是向其所屬的部委。<P>
This arrangement is meant to advance the rule of the center and, at the same time, maximize the reach of the party’s counterespionage and law enforcement apparatus. However, the differences in SSB and PSB missions indicate differences in how their local-level leaders and officers might respond to a decline of central authority.
<P>	這種安排旨在強化中央統治，同時將黨的反間諜與執法機構的觸角極大化。然而，國安局與公安局任務性質的不同，也顯示出其地方領導人與官員在面對中央權威衰落時，可能會有不同的反應。<P>
PSBs in the provinces and municipalities have a national police function—investigating the sorts of ordinary crime any local law enforcement authority would handle, along with investigating and apprehending dissidents, unauthorized religious adherents, and terrorists. Though the MPS has deployed special operations units on occasional anti-terror missions outside China—for example, to the Golden Triangle region where Burma, Thailand, and Laos meet—provincial and municipal PSBs normally focus on their own jurisdictions, not on cases or events abroad.
<P>	省、市級的公安局具有全國性的警察職能——調查任何地方執法機關會處理的普通犯罪，以及調查與逮捕異議人士、未經授權的宗教信徒與恐怖分子。儘管公安部偶爾會派遣特種行動部隊前往中國境外執行反恐任務（例如在緬、泰、寮交界的金三角地區），但省、市級公安局通常專注於其管轄範圍內，而非海外案件。<P>
By contrast, some SSBs in the provinces and municipalities, owing to the nature of their mission, focus both on counterespionage in their localities and on selected intelligence operations abroad. For example, the Jiangsu provincial SSB was the workplace of Xu Yanjun—the Chinese intelligence officer arrested in Belgium in 2018, extradited to the US, and convicted in 2022 of attempting to steal jet engine technology from General Electric. Xu’s work at the Jiangsu SSB appears to have focused on stealing technology from the aerospace industry.194 The same SSB likely has other officers doing counterintelligence work, though they would not need Xu’s competent level of English. The Shanghai SSB, which recruited Glen Duffie Shriver and tried to insert him into the CIA, may have focused on collection against US government targets.【195】 It also has agents pursuing counterespionage work against perceived foreign enemies in China’s largest city.
<P>	相比之下，部分省、市級國安局由於任務性質，既專注於當地的反間諜工作，也專注於選定的海外情報行動。例如，江蘇省國安局是徐延軍（Xu Yanjun）的工作單位——他在 2018 年於比利時被捕，被引渡至美國，並於 2022 年因企圖竊取奇異公司（General Electric）噴射引擎技術而被定罪。徐在江蘇國安局的工作似乎集中於竊取航空航太產業的技術。該局可能還有其他官員從事反情報工作，儘管他們不需要像徐那樣精通英語。上海國安局曾招募格倫·達菲·施里弗（Glen Duffie Shriver）並試圖將其滲透進 CIA，其重點可能在於針對美國政府目標的情報蒐集。該局亦有特務在中國最大的城市針對被視為外國敵人的人員進行反間諜工作。<P>
Because of differences in their missions, SSB personnel in the localities may be subject to more attention from their ministry in Beijing and stronger supervision than MPS gives to local PSBs. However, this requires further study to define, particularly from province to province.
<P>	由於任務差異，地方國安局人員受北京部委的關注與監督程度，可能比公安部對地方公安局的監督更強。然而，這仍需進一步研究來界定，特別是各省之間可能存在差異。<P>

  ## The Good, the Bad, the Red, and the Expert/優點、缺點、紅色與專業性
Professionalism, training, expertise, and political reliability seem to vary widely among Ministries of State Security and Public Security officers. It is hard to obtain more than anecdotal evidence about the level of competence and, for lack of a better term, the loyalty of these officers, but here I can at least examine a range of my own encounters and those of people I interviewed for this chapter.
<P>	國安局與公安局官員在專業化、訓練、專業知識與政治可靠性方面似乎存在巨大差異。要獲取關於這些官員能力與（暫且稱之為）忠誠度的資訊，除了軼聞外很困難，但在此我可以檢視我個人及受訪者的經驗。<P>
One academic account about the MSS and accounts from former intelligence professionals I interviewed, who have had contact with MSS officers, indicate that these officers are perhaps the closest thing to “true believers” in the CCP that one can find inside the system. They at least pretend to be, and many probably are, in near-total agreement with the CCP’s mission to restore China’s greatness and foster a socialist future.
<P>	一位學者的紀錄以及我訪談過的幾位曾與國安局官員接觸的前情報專業人士指出，這些官員或許是體制內最接近中共「真信徒」的人。他們至少在表面上表現得如此，且許多人可能在精神上完全認同中共恢復中國偉大與促進社會主義未來的使命。<P>
Nigel Inkster is the former director of operations and intelligence at MI6 (UK Secret Intelligence Service) and the senior advisor on cybersecurity and China at the International Institute for Strategic Studies, a London think tank. He put it this way when Cindy Yu interviewed him on her podcast China Whispers:
<P>	前英國秘密情報局（MI6）行動與情報主管、現任倫敦智庫國際戰略研究所（IISS）網絡安全與中國事務資深顧問奈傑爾·英克斯特（Nigel Inkster）在接受 Cindy Yu 的訪談時如此表示：<P>
The first thing to say is that the Ministry of State Security, [though] described as a ministry, is first and foremost an organ of the party. Its fundamental mission is to preserve and protect the security of the party. And Ministry of State Security cadre officers are sworn to be absolutely loyal to the party . . . and that is in many ways the primary qualification for being accepted into the Ministry of State Security.
<P>	首先要說明的是，國安局（MSS）雖然被稱為一個部，但它首要是一個黨的機構。其根本使命是維護與保護黨的安全。國安局的幹部官員宣誓絕對效忠於黨……在許多方面，這也是進入國安局的首要資格。<P>
This is a valuable insight into the MSS mindset, at least of those officers who operate abroad and at the headquarters in Beijing. Of the seven former and then-current MSS officers I have knowingly met (six in China, one in Washington), all stayed on message during meetings. None spoke English except for a young analyst from the MSS analysis organ, the China Institutes for Contemporary International Relations in Beijing.
<P>	這對於理解國安局的心態——至少是那些在海外運作或在北京總部的官員——是非常寶貴的見解。在我刻意接觸過的七名前任或現任國安局官員中（六名在中國，一名在華盛頓），所有人在會議中都嚴格遵循官方口徑。除了來自北京中國現代國際關係研究院（CICIR）的一名年輕分析師會說英語外，無人說英語。<P>
In the 1990s, one MSS officer, admitting his affiliation, claimed to be a representative of a newly founded MSS-sponsored company. He sought to “cooperate” with American technology firms, offering “valuable connections to government clients.” He spoke no English and was clumsy in his approach, but others are suave and smooth operators with good foreign language skills, like MSS former vice minister Yu Fang, aka Yu Enguang, whom the Australian scholar Alex Joske profiled in his 2022 book Spies and Lies. Writing about Yu’s activities in the early 1980s, Joske relates:
<P>	1990 年代，一名國安局官員承認其身份，聲稱自己是某家新成立的、由國安局贊助的公司代表。他試圖與美國科技公司「合作」，提供「與政府客戶的寶貴聯繫」。他不會說英語，作風笨拙，但也有人是精明幹練、外語流利的專業操作者，例如前國安局副部長于方（Yu Fang，又名于恩光），澳洲學者 Alex Joske 在其 2022 年的著作《間諜與謊言》（Spies and Lies）中對其進行了剖析。Joske 在描述于方於 1980 年代初的活動時提到：<P>
[Yu] was a witty and memorable character, skilled at interacting with targets and adept at English—something that stands out in all accounts. While posted to America undercover as a Xinhua journalist, he charmed a Washington Post reporter with his commentary on a Cantonese meal. . . . He’s been trained well, the ability to introduce Chinese cuisine to foreigners was specifically drilled into Chinese spies during their English language courses.
<P>	[于方] 是一個機智且令人難忘的人物，擅長與目標互動且精通英語——這在所有紀錄中都是顯著特點。他在美國以新華社記者身份進行臥底工作時，曾以對粵菜的評論令一名《華盛頓郵報》記者著迷……他受過良好的訓練，向外國人介紹中國菜是中國間諜在英語課程中被特別訓練的技能。<P>
That was the 1980s–2000s (Yu died in 2013). More recently, the CCP posted another smooth operator to the United States, Christine Fang Fang (no relation to Yu Fang). As an attractive young woman who enrolled in California State University, East Bay in Hayward, California, she spent lots of time on extracurricular activities. While working on the campaigns of up-and-coming Democratic Party politicians, she developed sexual relationships with at least two of them. However, there were holes in her tradecraft: the FBI became alert to Fang when they observed her meeting an MSS officer based at the PRC consulate in San Francisco. In mid-2015, when the bureau began warning her contacts of these activities, Fang fled the country.
<P>	那是 1980 至 2000 年代的事（于於 2013 年逝世）。近期，中共又派駐了另一名老練的操作者前往美國，即方芳（Christine Fang，與于方無親緣關係）。作為一名在加州東灣州立大學註冊的迷人年輕女性，她在校外活動上投入大量時間。在協助一些民主黨新秀政治家競選期間，她與至少兩名政治家發展了性關係。然而，她的情報作業技術存在漏洞：FBI 在觀察到她與一名駐舊金山領事館的國安局官員會面後，對其產生了警覺。2015 年中，當聯邦調查局開始警告其接觸對象時，方便逃離了美國。<P>
Nigel Inkster commented on the uneven nature of MSS officer skillsets in a separate interview, when he said about Chinese State Security officers:
<P>	奈傑爾·英克斯特在另一次訪談中評論了國安局官員技能的不均衡性，他對中國安全官員表示：<P>
We don’t see a predictable MO [modus operandi]. The Russians, for example, would always carefully kit out the agents that they ran, but the Chinese don’t do that; they are more hit and miss. That seems to include training of their own officers. The lack of standards and lack of care seem, at least in part, to indicate a disrespect for the US. A PSB contact in China with experience dealing with his local SSB once opined that there was a clear cultural difference between PSB and SSB officers: “We can be rough and rude, but State Security acts threatening and dangerous. . . . Chinese people prefer to avoid us, especially State Security. They just want to live their lives and keep their heads down.”【201】 I picked up a hint about that “threatening and dangerous” aspect of State Security officer behavior when they summoned me “for tea” in a northeast Chinese city in the 2000s. Their phone monitors had picked up my conversation with another American who was alarmed about a security threat. There was no rough stuff, just an unnecessarily menacing atmosphere. 
<P>	我們看不到可預測的作案手法（MO）。例如俄羅斯人總是會仔細裝備他們運作的特務，但中國人不會這樣做；他們的表現起伏不定。這似乎也包括對自身官員的訓練。缺乏標準與缺乏細緻度，至少在某種程度上顯示出對美國的不尊重。一位在中國有處理地方國安局經驗的公安部聯絡人曾評論說，公安局與國安局官員之間存在明顯的文化差異：「我們可能粗魯無禮，但國安局的行為顯得具威脅性且危險……中國人寧願避開我們，尤其是國安局。他們只想過自己的生活，低調行事。」我在 2000 年代於中國東北某城市被「請去喝茶」時，也察覺到了國安局官員那種「威脅且危險」的特質。他們的電話監聽截獲了我與另一名對安全威脅感到不安的美國人的對話。當時沒有任何粗暴行為，僅僅是那種不必要的威嚇氛圍。<P>
Public Security officers seem decidedly more concerned with their image, if not perfectly so. The New Yorker correspondent Jiayang Zha wrote that they could be “at once harsh and accommodating, insidious and absurd.” They temporarily detained, or “vacationed” (bei lüyou), her brother Jianguo in 2008 because of his political record; they did not jail him but gently removed him from his home during the visit of a foreign dignitary to prevent him from engaging in political activity that could embarrass the regime:
<P>	公安局官員似乎顯然更在意自己的形象，即便並非完美。紐約客雜誌記者趙嘉揚（Jiayang Zha）寫道，他們可以「既嚴厲又周到，既陰險又荒謬」。2008 年，他們因政治背景暫時拘留或「帶其度假」（bei lüyou）她的哥哥建國；他們沒有將其入獄，而是在外國政要訪問期間，溫和地將他從家中帶離，以防止他從事可能令政權尷尬的政治活動：<P>
On the road, the three policemen assigned to Jianguo would look after him as though they were his assistants: they bought sightseeing tickets, checked in and out of hotels, helped with his luggage, took snapshots of him at scenic spots. They fussed over him at meals, heaping meats and vegetables onto his plate, ladling up additional bowls of soup for him. Sometimes they booked a trip through an agency and ended up travelling for days with a group of real tourists. The all-male quartet aroused curiosity and inspired innocent guesses about their relationships. “So, are you father and sons?” “Colleagues?” And, pointing at Jianguo: “Is he your boss?”
<P>	在路上，指派給建國的三名警察照顧他的樣子就像他的助手一樣：他們買景點門票、辦理飯店入住、幫忙提行李，還在景點為他拍照。用餐時他們對他極盡照顧，把肉和蔬菜堆在他的盤子裡，還為他盛好幾碗湯。有時他們會透過旅行社訂行程，最後跟著一群真正的遊客旅行好幾天。這組全男性的四人組引起了好奇心，並引發了對他們關係的純真猜測。「所以，你們是父子嗎？」「同事嗎？」然後指著建國問：「他是你的老闆嗎？」<P>
It seems likely that SSB and PSB officers are different people depending on the nature of their jobs: more sophisticated and urbane abroad, tougher at home catching spies, rough-edged as police dealing with dissidents. But this is the CCP, a party that sees society as full of enemies within. A significant cultural attribute that may be common to all state security and public security officers at every level is fear: “Unending existential fear about enemies within; fear of being caught between CCP general secretary Xi Jinping’s never-ending anticorruption drive and a culture that still fosters graft, as well as fear of being insufficiently loyal to Xi’s ‘thought’ and to his status as the CCP’s ‘core.’”
<P>	看起來，國安局與公安局官員因職務性質不同而展現出不同的特質：在海外更為老練與儒雅，在國內抓間諜時更為強悍，而在處理異議人士時則顯得粗糙。但這畢竟是中共，一個將社會視為充滿內奸的政黨。所有層級的安全與公安官員可能共有的重大文化特徵是「恐懼」：「對內奸永無止境的生存恐懼；害怕夾在習近平永無止境的反腐運動與仍滋生貪腐的文化之間；以及害怕對習的『思想』與其作為中共「核心」地位的忠誠度不足。」<P>
  
## Provincial Instability/省级不确定性

In the meantime, SSB and PSB officers operate in society at large. By all accounts, things are not going well in some parts of China; the organs of state security strive to control mounting unrest. 

與此同時，國家安全部（SSB）與公安部（PSB）官員在整個社會中運作。據各方說法，中國部分地區的情況並不樂觀；國家安全機構正努力控制日益增長的動盪。

They may continue to succeed as long as party and state functionaries at the working level remain loyal and conscientiously strive to carry out Beijing’s policies in spirit and letter. But it is hard to tell how well they actually follow directives from the center without sufficient inside information beyond accounts emerging from various purges and public criticisms of errant officials. Chinese mainland politics is such a closed process that measuring the strength of the CCP’s grip in each province, municipality, and autonomous region requires detailed study, an analysis that the US should prioritize if it has not already.

只要基層的黨政官員仍保持忠誠，並盡責地在精神與條文上貫徹北京的政策，國家安全機構可能仍能持續取得成功。然而，若缺乏足夠的內部情報，僅憑各類清洗行動或對失職官員的公開批評所呈現的資訊，實難判斷他們究竟在多大程度上確實執行了中央的指令。中國大陸的政治運作過程極度封閉，若要衡量中共在各省、直轄市及自治區的控制力，必須進行詳盡的研究；若美國尚未將此項分析列為優先事項，則應當儘速著手。

Specific incidents can be revealing—like the 2011 and 2016 uprisings in Wukan, Guangzhou Province.203 Even then, the center’s level of control remains obscure. The CCP generally does not allow Chinese news media—especially now—to exploit data leaks and other unofficial information to dig into angles like emails between the center and local authorities and relevant official documents.

特定事件可能具備啟示作用——例如 2011 年與 2016 年廣東省烏坎發生的起義。【203】即便如此，中央的控制程度依然模糊不清。中共通常不允許中國新聞媒體——尤其是在目前這種情況下——利用數據洩漏或其他非官方資訊，去挖掘如中央與地方當局之間的電子郵件或相關官方文件等面向。

The MPS called the Wukan uprisings “mass incidents,” which it defined as any kind of planned or impromptu gathering formed because of “internal contradictions.” These include mass public speeches, physical conflicts, airing of grievances, and other forms of group behavior that may disrupt social stability. They can involve only a dozen or so people or be huge, such as the violent demonstrations in Weng’an County, Guizhou Province, when over 10,000 people protested the death of a schoolgirl, overturning police cars and setting fire to the local PSB. Such major incidents dwarf the COVID-19 lockdown protests across China in November 2022, when hundreds of people took to the streets in a number of locations.

公安部將烏坎起義稱為「群體性事件」，並將其定義為因「內部矛盾」而形成的任何形式之計劃性或即興集會。這包括群眾集會演說、肢體衝突、訴苦陳情，以及其他可能破壞社會穩定的群體行為。此類事件規模可僅有十餘人，亦可規模龐大，例如貴州省翁安縣發生的暴力示威，當時逾萬人因抗議一名女學生之死，翻覆警車並縱火焚燒當地公安局。此類重大事件之規模，遠非 2022 年 11 月中國各地因防疫封控所引發的抗議活動可比，後者僅有數百人走上街頭。
According to MPS figures, these incidents grouped together increased tenfold over the period 1993–2005, from 8,700 to 87,000. The last time the MPS released these figures was 2006. Foreign analysts estimated the number at 180,000 in 2010 based on a separate statement that the nationwide figure had doubled from 2006 to 2010. Unfortunately, no breakdown by province has been available.

根據公安部數據，此類事件總數在 1993 年至 2005 年間增長了十倍，從 8,700 件增加到 87,000 件。公安部最後一次發布此類數據是在 2006 年。外國分析人士根據另一份聲明（稱全國數據在 2006 年至 2010 年間翻倍）估計，2010 年之事件數達 18 萬件。遺憾的是，目前並無按省份分類的詳細數據可供參考。

The number of incidents nationwide may not have declined since 2010, because if it had, the MPS might have resumed issuing those statistics. The CCP’s massive surveillance may have reduced mass incidents, but it is impossible to know more without further study. Surveillance did not seem to stop people from hitting the streets in the COVID-19 lockdown protests, though the police later rounded many up for questioning and punishment. 

全國範圍內的事件數量自 2010 年以來可能並未下降，因為若真有下降，公安部或許早已恢復發布這些統計數據。中共的大規模監控或許減少了群體性事件，但在未經進一步研究之前，無法得知更多詳情。監控似乎並未能阻止人們在 COVID-19 封控期間走上街頭，儘管警方隨後將許多人抓捕歸案以進行審問與懲處。

## Policy Proposals: Before the Deluge/政策建議：洪流之前
This chapter has illustrated the heavy burden the CCP places on SSB and PSB officers in the provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions. With uneven training and uncertain skillsets, these officers must defend a regime that has ever more oppressively surveilled its population. The CCP subjects officers to continual anti-corruption campaigns and other movements and expects them to regularly express loyalty in ways that have begun to resemble the political campaigns of the Mao era.

本章說明了中共對各省、直轄市及自治區之 SSB 與 PSB 官員所施加的沉重負擔。在培訓不均且技能水平不確定的情況下，這些官員必須捍衛一個對其國民進行日益高壓監控的政權。中共讓官員持續參與反腐運動及其他政治運動，並要求他們定期表達忠誠，其方式已開始與毛澤東時代的政治運動趨於相似。

Meanwhile, many ordinary people not involved in state security or public security matters, particularly if they are not CCP members, live fairly normal lives. They prefer to avoid involvement in such affairs, possibly engendering an uncertain level of social isolation for officers.

與此同時，許多未參與國家安全或公安事務的普通民眾（特別是若其非中共黨員），過著相當正常的生活。他們傾向於避免捲入此類事務，這可能導致官員面臨一定程度且難以預測的社會孤立。

Anticipating future instability or a decline of central power in China involves preparing to engage SSB and PSB officials outside Beijing in ways that evoke positive rather than negative reactions. To prepare for a destabilized scenario, the US should consider the following measures.

預判中國未來的動盪或中央權力的衰落，意味著必須準備好以能引發正面而非負面反應的方式，與北京以外的 SSB 與 PSB 官員進行接觸。為了應對政權動盪之情境，美國應考慮採取以下措施。

### Build Relationships with SSB and PSB Officials/建立與 SSB 及 PSB 官員之關係

American diplomatic posts should improve contacts with provincial and lower-level officials from the security ministries and from the PLA in military regions. They should pursue profiles of key persons if they have not already. Ironically, Christine Fang Fang’s effort to profile up-and-coming American politicians for the CCP is an interesting counterpoint, minus the practice of seduction.

美國外交機構應加強與安全部及軍區解放軍（PLA）之省級及基層官員的接觸。若尚未進行，應著手建立關鍵人物之背景檔案。諷刺的是，方芳（Christine Fang）曾為中共蒐集美國政壇新秀背景資料之舉，可作為一個有趣的對照——僅少了「誘惑」這一手段。

US diplomats should consider creating psychological profiles of individuals who might decide, in a crisis scenario, to ignore orders from Beijing and begin making independent decisions.The US should initiate a program to interview exiles and others who have had contacts with MPS/PSB and MSS/SSB officers at the provincial level and below to build a more comprehensive picture of key personalities and of the cultural changes that may be taking place in the security organs at localities.

美國外交官應考慮為那些在危機情境下，可能決定無視北京指令並開始進行獨立決策之個人，建立心理剖析。美國應啟動一項計畫，訪談流亡者及其他曾與省級及以下層級之公安部/公安局（MPS/PSB）與國家安全部/國家安全局（MSS/SSB）官員有接觸之人，藉此勾勒出更全面的關鍵人物圖像，並掌握地方安全機構可能正在發生的文化變遷。

### Measure and Covertly Encourage Disloyalty to the CCP/衡量並隱密地鼓勵對中共之不忠誠

Washington should study and define the actual effects of the CCP’s enhanced surveillance programs on local populations. Some people welcome such surveillance, feeling that only criminals and swindlers have anything to fear. But others do not, and their feelings may also differ about other issues, such as Xi Jinping’s drive to mandate the study of his thought. Valuable insights about the bewilderment and frustration building up in Chinese society are available in an October 2023 essay by Evan Osnos.

華盛頓應研究並界定中共強化之監控計畫對於地方民眾產生的實際影響。部分民眾對此類監控表示歡迎，認為唯有犯罪者與詐騙犯才需要恐懼。但其他人並不認同，且他們對於其他議題（如習近平強制推行「習近平思想」學習）的感受亦可能有所不同。Evan Osnos 於 2023 年 10 月發表的一篇文章，對中國社會中日益積累的困惑與挫折感提供了極具價值的洞察。

The US also needs to focus analytic efforts to determine which provinces and municipalities are likely and not likely to stay loyal to the center in a crisis of central authority.

此外，美國亦需集中分析力量，以判定在中央權威遭遇危機時，哪些省份與直轄市可能（或不可能）維持對中央之忠誠。

The West could publicize in Chinese language media the story of how Germany neutralized the East German secret police (Stasi) and opened their files for public inspection. The result was to demystify the Stasi’s capabilities and reach, exposing how the Stasi had recruited enormous numbers of East and West Germans to spy on behalf of the communist government. Other examples of post-authoritarian exposure of secret police activities occurred in Portugal and the Baltic states, where, as in Berlin, specialized libraries open to the public offer local citizens and foreign researchers access to these annals of oppression.

西方國家可透過中文媒體，宣傳德國如何使東德秘密警察（史塔西，Stasi）失去作用，並將其檔案開放供公眾查閱之故事。此舉之結果是去除了史塔西能力與影響範圍的神祕感，並揭露其如何招募大量東西德民眾為共產政府從事間諜活動。其他在威權政體瓦解後揭露秘密警察活動之案例，亦見於葡萄牙及波羅的海國家；在這些國家，如同柏林一般，設有對公眾開放之專門圖書館，讓當地公民及外國研究人員得以查閱這些壓迫之史冊。

## Policy Proposals: After the Deluge/政策建议：洪流之后

As described above, the governments of unified Germany, Portugal, and the Baltic states pursued programs that provide lessons on handling a defunct secret police organization and personnel. These examples may be useful in a post-PRC China, and the United States should make plans to help the Chinese people and their new leaders learn the following:

如前所述，統一後的德國、葡萄牙及波羅的海國家所推行的計畫，為如何處理已失效之秘密警察組織及其人員提供了寶貴教訓。這些案例對於「後中華人民共和國時代」的中國可能極具參考價值，美國應制定計畫，協助中國人民及其新任領導層學習以下內容：

•How democratic outcomes can emerge from the downfall of authoritarian governments

•民主成果如何從威權政府的垮台中產生

•How Berlin punished lawbreakers while avoiding victor’s justice

•柏林如何在懲處違法者之同時，避免落入「勝者正義」（victor's justice）的窠臼

•How Germany worked to dispel myths and legends about the Stasi secret police by opening secret archives for public examination

•德國如何透過開放秘密檔案供公眾查閱，藉此消除有關史塔西（Stasi）秘密警察的神祕化與傳說

In the end, these measures helped advance the newly freed nations along the road to democracy, an outcome that Washington should prioritize for China. If these nations are willing, they could, with American help, introduce post-PRC provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions to similar programs through exchanges and educational visits. If possible, the governments of or nongovernmental organizations in Germany, Portugal, and the Baltic states should lead the efforts.

最終，這些措施協助了新獲得自由的國家邁向民主之路；華盛頓應將此作為中國的首要目標。若上述國家願意，在美國的協助下，可透過交流與教育訪問，向「後中華人民共和國時代」的各省、直轄市及自治區介紹類似的計畫。若情況允許，應由德國、葡萄牙及波羅的海國家的政府或非政府組織來主導這些工作。

The US previously promoted exchanges with the PRC court system to explore juvenile justice solutions and other specialized matters. Such exchanges focused on police reform and should also consider counterespionage systems.

美國先前曾促進與中國法院系統的交流，以探討青少年司法解決方案及其他專業領域。此類交流應著重於警察改革，且亦應將反間諜體系納入考量。

Washington may decide to encourage democratic outcomes, post-PRC, in China’s provinces, municipalities, and autonomous regions. In doing so, it should carefully consider Chinese sensibilities about the legacy of modern Chinese nationalism, avoiding the appearance of interfering in the country’s affairs. Few things can unite the majority Han Chinese population more quickly than actions they perceive as foreign interference, which can instantly prompt emotional reactions recalling the troubled decades before the 1949 CCP victory. Beijing’s propagandists have successfully labeled that time the Century of Humiliation, and the US should avoid becoming connected with that idea.

華盛頓可能會決定在「後中華人民共和國時代」的中國各省、直轄市及自治區中，鼓勵民主成果之產出。在執行此舉時，應審慎考量中國對於現代民族主義遺產之敏感性，避免呈現出干涉該國內政之態勢。幾乎沒有什麼比「被視為外國干預」的行為更能迅速團結大多數漢族人口，因為這會立即誘發令人聯想起 1949 年中共獲勝前那段動盪年代的情緒反應。北京的宣傳者已成功將該時期定性為「百年國恥」，美國應避免與此觀念產生關聯。
