# How to initiate a truth and reconciliation process in China（如何在中国启动真相与和解进程）
## Choose Forgiveness/選擇寬恕
<P>	In 1995, South Africa established the Truth and Reconciliation Commission, pioneering a new transitional model from authoritarianism to democracy. Its key purposes were to investigate and publicly disclose the crimes of past oppressors while granting amnesty for these crimes and achieving reconciliation.
<P>	在1995年，南非成立了真相與和解委員會，開創了一種由威權主義向民主轉型的嶄新模式。其主要目的是調查並公開披露過去壓迫者的罪行，同時給予這些罪行特赦，實現和解。
<P>	For China, only through the investigation and public disclosure of past crimes can the country put an end to past evils and bid farewell to the era of oppression. Without forgiveness and reconciliation, a peaceful transition to democracy will be difficult to achieve.
<P>	對中國而言，只有通過調查並公開披露過去的罪行，才能結束過去的邪惡，告別壓迫的時代。沒有寬恕與和解，和平轉型為民主將變得困難。
<P>	Why is forgiveness important? Because after the fall of the CCP, the Chinese people should aim to change the system of evil, not to punish specific individuals. To smoothly end that system of evil, they should be willing to forgo punishing the leaders who committed wrongdoing under that system. When authoritarian rulers lay down their arms and accept a peaceful transition, this act itself is a form of redemption. Even in violent revolutions (such as the Xinhai Revolution in 1911) when one side lays down its weapons, is willing to engage in talks, and accepts its opponent, the other side can forgive past actions.
<P>	為什麼寬恕很重要？因為在中共垮台後，中國人民應該以改變邪惡體制為目標，而非懲罰特定個人。為了順利結束那個邪惡體系，他們應該願意放棄懲罰在該體系下犯錯的領導人。當威權統治者放下武器、接受和平轉型時，這本身就是一種救贖。即使在暴力革命（如1911年的辛亥革命）中，當一方放下武器、願意談判並接受對方時，另一方也能原諒過去的行為。
<P>	If the parties to a violent revolution can do this, surely China’s people can do it in the case of a peaceful transition to democracy. Forgiveness leads to reconciliation, and reconciliation paves the way for the future.
<P>	若暴力革命之各方能如此作為，則中國人民於和平轉型為民主之際，亦當能如是。寬恕能促成和解；和解乃未來之路。
<P>	The future democratic transition in China should also involve a truth and reconciliation process.
<P>	中國未來之民主轉型，亦應包含真相與和解之程序。

  ##  Organize Groups for Action/組織行動團體
<P>	The new Chinese government should establish a truth and reconciliation commission as soon as possible. This commission can be an independent body under a parliament or cabinet, with a president or prime minister proposing it and parliament approving it. The president or prime minister can nominate a director and a deputy director of the commission, and prominent leaders from the government, parliament, or the judiciary can appoint these officials, after which parliament can approve them. The members of the truth and reconciliation commission can include government officials, parliamentarians, activists, scholars and experts in relevant fields, representatives of political victims or their families (such as the Tiananmen Mothers, pro-democracy groups, Falun Gong, and representatives of Tibetans, Uyghurs, Mongolians, and others). They may also include former government leaders. Furthermore, parliament should pass legislation and regulations regarding the truth and reconciliation process.
<P>	新政府應盡早成立真相與和解委員會。此委員會可作為國會或內閣下獨立機構，由總統或總理提議，經國會批准。總統或總理得提名委員會主席與副主席，並由政府、國會或司法界之重要人物任命，然後由國會核准。委員會成員可包括政府官員、議員、活動家、學者及相關專家，亦可有政治受害者或其家屬代表（如六四母親、民主運動團體、法輪功、藏族、維吾爾族、蒙古族等代表）。亦可邀請前任政府領導人加入。此外，國會應通過有關真相與和解之法律規範
<P>	Before the government establishes the commission, civil society can form organizations such as truth and reconciliation promotion committees, to carry out various related tasks. In fact, civil society has already done a significant amount of work. For example, the Tiananmen Mothers have been collecting lists of those who died in the June Fourth Incident for the past three decades. Activists have published several comprehensive narratives in the public domain about the truth of the June Fourth Incident, along with concrete suggestions for seeking justice for it. Chinese and overseas writers, journalists, scholars, and other individuals have written extensively about various major political movements in Chinese history. Overseas Chinese scholars have established extensive databases on the CCP’s various political movements, and so on.
<P>	委員會成立前，民間社會亦可組織如真相與和解推動委員會等組織，從事相關工作。事實上，民間已做出相當多努力。例如，六四母親持續三十多年搜集遇難者名單，活動家亦公開多份關於六四事件真相之全面敘述，並提出追求正義之具體建議。華人與海外作家、記者、學者與其他人士，亦已廣泛撰寫中國歷史上各大政治運動之相關內容。海外華人學者建立了豐富之 CCP 政治運動資料庫，等等。

  ## Specific Actions/具體行動
<P>	In general, the truth and reconciliation process involves several actions. Of course, some of these activities can and should commence before the establishment of the commission.
<P>	真相與和解之進程，通常涵蓋若干措施。當然，部分活動可於委員會成立前即行動。

  ### Promote Freedom/促進自由
<P>	Promote genuine freedom of speech, as well as freedom of the press, assembly, and association.
<P>	推動真正之言論自由，以及新聞、集會與結社之自由。

  ###  Release Prisoners of Conscience /釋放良心犯
<P>	Release all prisoners of conscience, including those whom the CCP has arrested and imprisoned for their speech, publications, assembly, or association.
<P>	釋放所有良心犯，包括中共因言論、出版、集會或結社而拘捕之人士。
<P>	This point may seem straightforward, but in reality it is quite complex. Since the era of Jiang Zemin, CCP authorities have adopted a strategy of “depoliticizing political issues” when dealing with dissidents. They have changed the previous “counter-revolutionary crime” into “crimes endangering national security.” In recent years, “crimes endangering national security”274 has become the primary charge authorities use to persecute dissidents. Furthermore, the CCP has broadened the scope of the “starting quarrels and provoking trouble” charge, using it to arbitrarily arrest dissidents. In addition, when cracking down on dissidents, CCP authorities no longer cite political reasons for prosecution but charge them with economic or other criminal offenses. In power struggles within the top echelons, the government also frequently levies economic or other criminal charges against those the party seeks to purge.
<P>	此論點似似直截，實際卻頗為複雜。自江澤民時代起，中共當局於對待異見者時，已採取「去政治化政治議題」之策略。彼等將昔日之「反革命罪」改稱為「危害國家安全罪」。近年以來，「危害國家安全罪」已成為當局鎮壓異見者之首要控罪。再者，中共亦拓展「煽動滋事」之範圍，以任意拘捕異見者。另在鎮壓異見者時，當局不再以政治理由起訴，改以經濟或其他刑事罪名論處。於高層權力鬥爭之際，政府亦屢以經濟或其他刑事罪名，對黨欲清除之人課以罪名。
<P>	This makes the task of releasing prisoners of conscience quite complex. A new Chinese government can adopt the following approach:
<P>	此事使釋放良心犯之任務頗為複雜。新政府可採取下列辦法：
<P>	1 Release all individuals who clearly qualify as prisoners of conscience.
<P>	釋放所有明顯屬於良心犯者。
<P>	2. For those whom the CCP convicted on other charges but who expressed dissenting views, consider each case individually.
<P>	對於以他罪名判決、但曾表達異見者，宜逐案審理。
<P>	For instance, some entrepreneurs who previously had no legal trouble were arrested after they expressed significant differences in political views, which indicates that the CCP essentially imprisoned them for their speech. However, the economic charges against them are not baseless accusations; there may indeed be some substance to those charges.
<P>	舉例，有些先前無法律麻煩之企業家，因表述重大政見而被捕，顯示以言論為由之拘禁；但其經濟罪名並非毫無根據，或有成分在。因此，應逐案評估。
<P>	Nevertheless, numerous entrepreneurs face similar economic issues, but the authorities have not touched them. In such cases, the new Chinese government should treat them as prisoners of conscience and release them without any guilt.  As for their economic issues, authorities should handle them separately, like they do for other entrepreneurs in similar situations. They should address the ill-gotten gains or “original sin” of individuals in this category accordingly, which includes requiring them to return any unlawfully acquired assets.
<P>	然而，許多企業家亦遇相似經濟問題，當局卻未出手處理。在此情況，新政府宜以良心犯視之，釋放無罪者；至經濟問題，則另行處理，如同對同類企業家之處置，清理不當所得或所謂原罪，含要求歸還不法所得。
<P>	The authorities should not subject these individuals to trial or punishment. Since the entrepreneur has already been imprisoned or fined and their property confiscated on economic charges, they have paid a price, often excessively so. Therefore, there is no reason to continue their detention. Some individuals may even deserve compensation.
<P>	當局不應就此等人之事實予以審判或處刑。既然他們已因經濟罪名入獄或罰款，財產亦被沒收，付出之代價多半過重，故不宜再拘禁。若有補償之可能，亦可考慮。

  ###  Let Dissidents Return/允異見者歸國
<P>	End the blacklist and restore the right of Chinese citizens who have been living abroad due to political persecution, including for involvement in the June Fourth Incident, to freely return to China. Chinese citizens who have acquired foreign nationality and express a desire to reinstate their Chinese citizenship should regain it immediately.
<P>	終止黑名單，恢復因政治迫害而居於海外之中國公民歸國自由之權利，含因參與六四事件而流亡者。取得外國籍且欲重獲中國籍者，應立即復得中國國籍。

  ###  Rectify Miscarriages of Justice/糾正冤假錯案
<P>	Simultaneously with the release of prisoners of conscience, rectify miscarriages of justice. This includes addressing wrongful convictions and other injustices both during and after the Mao era.
<P>	在釋放良心犯之餘，亦應同時糾正冤假錯案。此範疇涉及處理毛時代及其後期之誤判與不公正事件。
<P>	After Mao’s death, CCP authorities conducted a large-scale rectification of miscarriages of justice from the Mao era, but this process had significant limitations. For example, in cases involving landlords, their labels were removed, and the CCP said they had been reformed but did not acknowledge that the government had wrongly accused them in the first place. Therefore, these injustices still require rectification. The individuals who suffered them may have passed away, but authorities need to restore their reputations for historical accuracy.
<P>	毛時代結束後，中共當局曾大規模糾正毛時代之冤假錯案，然此一過程存有重大局限。例如，在地主案中，曾將其標籤去除，中共聲稱其已改造，惟未承認政府曾誤控其人。故此類不公仍需糾正，受害者雖已逝去，政府仍應為其平反，追求史實之準確。
<P>	In addition to those from the Mao era, the CCP has created numerous wrongful convictions and miscarriages of justice in the post-Mao era. These include the June Fourth Incident, the suppression of Falun Gong, the crackdown on human rights activists, the suppression of religious and faith groups, and the human rights abuses and cultural eradication of ethnic minorities in Tibet, Xinjiang, and other regions. Furthermore, a new Chinese government should also address the ongoing suppression of dissenting activities, human rights activism, family churches, and other religious and faith communities.
<P>	除毛時代之案件外，於後毛時代，中共亦累積大量冤案與錯案，範圍涵蓋六四事件、鎮壓法輪功、人權活動者之打壓、宗教與信仰團體之迫害，及藏、維吾爾等少數民族之人權侵害與文化滅絕。此外，新一代中國政府亦應持續處理對異見活動、人權運動、家庭教會及其他宗教信仰團體之打壓問題。

  ### Admit Wrongs/承認過錯
<P>	Reveal the truth of history. Identify and acknowledge significant human rights violations that have occurred during each period since the CCP came to power. In the Mao era, these include land reform, anti-rightist campaigns, and the Cultural Revolution. In the post-Mao era, they include the June Fourth Incident, the suppression of Falun Gong, the crackdown on human rights activism, and the human rights abuses and cultural eradication of ethnic minorities, among others.
<P>	揭示歷史真相。辨識並承認自中共執政以來各時期所發生之重大人權侵犯。於毛時代，包含土地改革、反右運動與文化大革命等。於後毛時代，包含六四事件、鎮壓法輪功、人權活動打壓，以及對少數民族之人權侵害與文化滅絕等。
<P>	Regarding violations in the post-Mao era, China will also need to address the issue of live organ harvesting. On March 15,  2015, Phoenix TV interviewed Huang Jiefu（黃潔夫）, former vice minister of health and then deputy director of the Central Health Committee and chairman of the Human Organ Donation and Transplantation Committee, regarding organ transplantation in China. He stated, “You don’t know where these organs come from, and the number of transplants performed is also a secret.”
<P>	關於毛時代之後之違法行為，中國亦須面對活摘器官之問題。2015年3月15日，鳳凰衛視曾訪問前衛生部副部長、時任中央衛生委員會副主任兼器官捐贈與移植委員會主席黃潔夫，他表示：「你不知道這些器官來自何處，移植數量也是秘密。」
<P>	His statement implies that organs are not coming solely from death row prisoners and voluntary donors. It raises questions about why the current government is keeping this information confidential. The reason is simple: if the Chinese people knew the number of organ transplant surgeries performed each year and found it exceeded the number of executions and voluntary donors, it would suggest a significant number of transplanted organs are from living individuals. Huang Jiefu indirectly acknowledged the existence of large-scale live organ harvesting in China. Ordinary criminal activities cannot account for this, and it is likely a government-led operation. Therefore, a new Chinese government will need to include this crime as a severe human rights violation and investigate the truth behind it.
<P>	此話暗示，器官並非僅來自死囚或志願捐贈者，引發疑問：為何現任政府要對此保密？原因很簡單：若民眾得知每年移植手術數量已超過死刑人數與志願捐贈數量，便會懷疑大量器官來自活人。黃潔夫亦間接承認中國存在大規模活體器官摘取，普通犯罪行為難以解釋此一規模，極可能由政府主導。因此，新一代中國政府須將此列為嚴重人權侵害，徹底追查真相。
<P>	The truth and reconciliation commission should extensively collect materials, invite relevant parties to present their views, conduct various hearings, and make relevant archives public. Then it should compile a report and publish it for the world to see.
<P>	真相與和解委員會應廣泛收集資料，邀請相關人士表達意見，舉辦聽證會，公開存檔，並整理成報告，並向世界公布。

  ###  Compensate Victims/賠償受害者
<P>	Economic compensation for victims of the CCP consists of two parts: general compensation, which provides a certain amount of financial compensation to victims of political persecution, and compensation for economic deprivation. For example, during land reform, the CCP expropriated land from its owners without compensation. The descendants of these landlords should receive appropriate compensation. Those who suffered political persecution and the confiscation of their private property should also receive appropriate compensation.
<P>	一般賠償：對遭受政治迫害之受害者，給予一定額度之金錢補償。經濟剝奪賠償：就財產被沒收或經濟損失予以補償。例如在土地改革之時，地主之土地被徵收而未賠償，其後裔當得適當賠償；遭受政治迫害者及私人財產遭沒收者亦應獲得相應之賠償。

  ###  Assign Responsibility and Dispense Forgiveness/釐定責任與赦免
<P>	The new Chinese government and the Chinese people will need to determine the responsibility of oppressors and grant them forgiveness. Forgiving these individuals means choosing not to punish them or require them to admit guilt and apologize for the human rights violations they committed.278 However, it is essential to clarify their responsibility, which is also part of revealing the truth. The new government should encourage past wrongdoers to openly repent and apologize but without coercion. It should widely publicize the stories of those who offer genuine apologies to set an example and create public pressure on those who refuse to admit wrongdoing.
<P>	新政府與人民須確定壓迫者之責任，並予以赦免。赦免，意指不予懲罰，亦不要求當事人承認罪行或道歉；然須澄清其責任，乃揭示真相之一環。新政府應鼓勵過去之不法者公開悔改並道歉，但不可脅迫。宜廣泛宣傳真正道歉者之事跡，以作示範，並以此對拒不承認者施加社會壓力。此亦可促進社會道德重建。

  ### Embrace Transparency/推動透明
<P>	As part of reform, the government should open political archives. It can divide this task into two categories. One includes the highest-level secrets of the party-state. These include the true number of people who died during the Great Famine, the name of the leader who gave the order to shoot during the Tiananmen Square protests, how the CCP issued and carried out orders, and so on. This situation is different from that of the Soviet Union and Eastern Europe. Today’s CCP may be prepared for the idea of the party’s demise, and it may destroy some of the most secret archives. However, even in this case, it is essential to open the archives of the party-state’s highest levels to help the Chinese people understand the historical truth as much as possible.

作為改革環節之一，政府應開放政治檔案，並可將此任務分為兩類。其中一類包含黨國最高層級之機密，例如大饑荒期間死亡人數之真相、天安門事件下下令開槍之領導人姓名、中共發布與執行命令之機制等。此情勢與蘇聯及東歐不同：當今中共或已對政黨覆滅做好準備，恐將銷毀部分最核心之機密檔案；然即便如此，仍須開放黨國最高層級檔案，以助中國人民盡可能了解歷史真相。
<P>	The other category involves informants. Some Eastern European countries enacted lustration laws during their transitional justice processes, which required opening archives to make the activities of Communist secret police and informants transparent. Some countries advocated for some form of punishment for informants, such as prohibitions on holding public office, while others did not advocate for punishment but required openness as part of revealing historical truth. Taiwan has also had similar experiences. Consequently, people were surprised to learn that Lech Wałęsa, the leader of Poland’s Solidarity trade union whom citizens later elected president, had once been an informant. The famous Czech writer Milan Kundera was also an informer in his youth, and there were strong suspicions that Taiwan’s Democratic Progressive Party heavyweight, Joseph Wu, had engaged in informant activity as well.
<P>	另一類涉及之問題乃關於「線人」。部分東歐國家於轉型正義之進程中，曾制定「清算法」（lustration laws），要求公開檔案，以使共產黨秘密警察與線人之活動透明化。部分國家主張對線人實施某種形式之懲處，例如禁止擔任公職；亦有國家不主張懲處，而僅要求公開檔案，以作為揭示歷史真相之一環。台灣亦有類似經驗。因此，當世人得知波蘭「團結工會」領袖、後經選民推舉為總統之萊赫·華勒沙（Lech Wałęsa）曾為線人時，深感驚訝；捷克著名作家米蘭·昆德拉（Milan Kundera）亦於青年時期擔任過線人；此外，外界亦強烈懷疑台灣民進黨重量級人物吳钊燮，曾參與線人活動。
<P>	I have reservations about lustration laws and similar measures. In other countries, these laws exposed many dissidents who had worked as informants. In China, it is highly likely there are numerous informants within the dissident community because the CCP has long persecuted and threatened this group, leading some to become informants under duress. Those who never participated in dissident activities would not have faced persecution or threats from the authorities and therefore would not have been compelled to become informants. If all archives related to the secret police became public, it could lead to a paradoxical situation in which the people view the dissident community with heavy suspicion while directing none toward those who never engaged in dissident activities.
<P>	本人對肅清法（Lustration laws）及類似措施持保留態度。於他國，此類法律曾使眾多擔任線人的異議人士身分曝光。然於中國，異議人士群體內極可能存在大量線人，蓋因中共長期迫害並威脅該群體，致使部分人士於壓力下淪為線人；而未參與異議活動者，既未受當局迫害威脅，亦無被迫成為線人之虞。若秘密警察相關檔案悉數公開，恐致悖論：民眾將對異議群體深懷疑忌，卻對未曾參與異議活動者毫無戒心。
<P>	Dissident activities in China are generally peaceful and open. Informant reports are not the cause of most dissident cases. Therefore, I advocate starting from the consequences, investigating and publicly disclosing only cases in which informant reports led to severe consequences. Other cases may not require such scrutiny. Furthermore, in a highly centralized China, political persecution incidents originate from the highest levels of leadership. The focus should be on the top rather than the bottom.
<P>	中國異議活動多屬和平且公開。線人舉報並非多數異議案件之主因，故本人主張由結果著手，僅針對線人舉報導致嚴重後果之案件進行調查並公開披露，其餘案件則未必需如此嚴審。此外，於高度集權之中國，政治迫害事件皆源於最高領導層，處理重心應置於高層而非基層。
<P>	Truth and reconciliation processes require taking a case-by-case approach to some matters while maintaining a broad perspective on others. In general, authorities should consider the rehabilitation and compensation of victims with as much detail and individual application as possible. Regarding some minor perpetrators, it is more appropriate to be general than specific in clarifying their personal responsibility.
<P>	真相與和解程序於部分事務應採取個案處理方式，於其他事務則須保有宏觀視野。原則而言，當局應盡可能詳盡並依個案情況，考量受害者之平反與補償；至於部分輕微加害者，釐清其個人責任時，採取概括處理較比個別追究為宜。

  ###  Prevent Reoffense/防止再次犯罪
<P>	Propose a set of recommendations and establish mechanisms to prevent future human rights violations.
<P>	提出一系列建議並建立機制，以防止未來人權侵犯行為再次發生。

  ###  Remove Symbols of Tyranny/移除暴政象徵
<P>	In China, there are too many symbols of tyranny, and during the early stages of transition, there may be significant disputes among various factions on many issues, making it difficult to make changes all at once. It may be appropriate to remove some symbols initially. For example, the portrait of Mao Zedong on top of Tiananmen Gate and the Mao Zedong Mausoleum in Tiananmen Square are the greatest symbols of tyranny. The new Chinese government should arrange the removal of the portrait atop Tiananmen Gate and transform the mausoleum in Tiananmen Square. Removing these two symbols has major symbolic significance in itself. The authorities can address other symbols of tyranny in public places gradually.
<P>	中國暴政象徵過多，於轉型初期，各派系間針對諸多議題恐有重大分歧，難以一蹴而就。初步階段宜先移除部分象徵，例如天安門城樓上之毛澤東像，以及天安門廣場內之毛澤東紀念堂，此二者乃最顯著之暴政象徵。新任中國政府應安排移除天安門城樓肖像，並改造天安門廣場紀念堂；移除此二象徵本身即具重大意義，其餘公共場所之暴政象徵，當可逐步處理。
  
###  Honor and Remember the Victims/緬懷並紀念受害者
<P>	Take symbolic steps to restore the reputation of victims, such as building memorials, statues, and museums. In locations with symbolic significance where large-scale human rights violations occurred, such as Chang’an Street in Beijing for the Tiananmen Square protests or Jiabiangou for forced labor camps, preserve the sites or establish memorials.
<P>	採取具象徵意義的措施來恢復受害者的名譽，例如建立紀念碑、雕像與博物館。在發生過大規模侵犯人權行為且具備象徵意義的地點，例如發生天安門事件的北京長安街，或是曾有強制勞改營的夾邊溝，應予以保護遺址或建立紀念設施。

  ###  Require an Economic Reckoning/推動經濟清算
<P>	An economic reckoning is necessary to recover wealth stolen from the people, including inappropriate gains from party assets and property that the CCP elite privatized.
<P>	為了從人民身上追回被竊取的財富，需進行經濟清算，涵蓋黨資產之不當收益與中共高層私有化財產之回歸與追繳。此清算應依法施行，包含獨立審計、資產來源之追溯、沒收不法所得、以及資金或資產之回歸公眾利益與國家基金。
<P>	In democratic countries, legitimate party assets can include the following:
<P>	在民主國家，合法的黨資產可包括下列項目：
<P>	• Party dues paid by members
<P>	•成員所繳之黨費
<P>	• Donations from various sectors, including party members
<P>	•各界（含黨員）之捐贈
<P>	• Industries managed by the party
<P>	• 黨所經營之產業
<P>	However, in China under one-party rule, the CCP has treated the entire country as its own and directly regarded the national treasury as its party treasury. This has made it the wealthiest party in the world. With China’s transition, this needs to change completely.
<P>	然於一黨專政之中國，中共將整個國家視為己有，並直接將國庫視為黨庫，致使其成為全球最富黨派。隨中國轉型，此現狀必須徹底改變。
<P>	First, the CCP, or any party that has descended from it and inherited its resources, needs to completely separate from the national treasury; this is self-evident. The assets it leaves behind, particularly the portion related to party-managed industries, should also undergo necessary adjustments. Take the media industry as an example. Strictly speaking, Xinhua News Agency and China Central Television (CCTV) belong to the state, while The People’s Daily belongs to the party. The People’s Daily is a CCP asset. However, The People’s Daily has become a huge media industry in China because of the CCP’s monopoly. Therefore, this party asset needs to undergo some form of divestment and streamlining. Other party assets should undergo similar treatment.
<P>	首先，中共或任何承襲其資源之派系，均須與國庫完全分離，此乃不言自明。其留存資產，特別是涉及黨管產業之部分，亦應進行必要調整。以媒體產業為例，嚴格而言，新華社與中國中央電視台（CCTV）屬國家所有，而《人民日報》則屬黨產。雖《人民日報》乃中共資產，然因中共壟斷，已演變為中國龐大媒體產業。因此，此類黨產須進行某種形式之剝離與精簡，其他黨產亦應比照辦理。
<P>	Privatization by the elite is a unique problem in China. Countries undergoing democratic transitions can be broadly divided into two categories: traditional capitalist societies and those with a history of state ownership.
<P>	精英階層壟斷私有化乃中國特有之問題。正進行民主轉型之國家，大致可分為兩類：傳統資本主義社會，以及具備國有制歷史之社會。
<P>	Traditional capitalist societies, like South Korea and Taiwan, generally protect private property. In these places, transitioning to democracy mainly requires ending political persecution, achieving political reconciliation, and opening up democratic elections.
<P>	傳統資本主義社會（如大韓民國與中華民國台灣）普遍保障私有財產；於此類地區，民主轉型主要需終止政治迫害、實現政治和解，並開放民主選舉。
<P>	However, in countries with a history of state ownership, such as the former Soviet Bloc countries, two types of transition are necessary: a transition from authoritarianism to democracy and a transition from state ownership to private ownership. In the former Soviet Bloc countries, political reform and economic reform happened simultaneously with democratic participation. Therefore, the results of privatization had legitimacy despite some issues. Generally speaking, the people in these countries accepted the outcomes of privatization.
<P>	然於具備國有制歷史之國家（如前蘇聯集團國家），須進行兩類轉型：其一係由威權轉向民主，其二係由國有轉向私有。在前蘇聯集團國家，政治改革與經濟改革係隨民主參與同步進行，故私有化結果雖存瑕疵，仍具正當性；概言之，該等國家民眾普遍接受私有化結果。
<P>	But China is different. China is a communist country, and before reform, it was characterized by state ownership rather than traditional private ownership. Mainland China differs from South Korea or Taiwan and more closely resembles the former Soviet Bloc countries. China needs to undergo both political and economic transitions. But unlike the former Soviet Bloc, China conducted economic reform without political reform, and it carried out privatization without democratic participation. This kind of privatization inevitably became privatization by the elite, and the people did not accept its results, so it lacked legitimacy. To this day, the elites have divided among themselves the assets of the nation and its collective property. The wealth gap in China is not a result of a market economy but of elite robbery.
<P>	然中國情況迥異。中國屬共產國家，改革前以國有制而非傳統私有制為特徵。中國大陸與南韓或台灣不同，與前蘇聯集團國家較為相似，須同時經歷政治與經濟轉型。惟與前蘇聯集團不同，中國在未進行政治改革之情況下推行經濟改革，且於缺乏民主參與下進行私有化。此類私有化必然演變為精英階層之私有化，因民眾不予接受，故缺乏正當性。時至今日，精英階層仍瓜分國家資產與集體財產；中國之貧富差距並非市場經濟之果，實乃精英掠奪之所致。
<P>	Therefore, a future Chinese democratic transition should also involve an accounting of the elites’ ill-gotten gains. To accomplish this, the new government can implement a strategy of rectifying the wrongdoing without punishing the wrongdoers. As long as they return the ill-gotten gains, there is no need for personal trials or punishments. The new government can use the returned wealth for public welfare and charity.
<P>	是以，未來中國民主轉型應包含對精英非法所得之清算。為達成此目的，新政府可採取「糾正錯誤但不懲處當事人」之策略：只要歸還非法所得，則無需進行個人審判或處罰；新政府得將追回之財富用於公益與慈善事業。
<P>	Truth and reconciliation are a process, and the entire transition is a process as well. The change cannot happen all at once, so it is vital to determine priorities. In advancing the process of truth and reconciliation, the top priorities are to ensure freedom of speech, restore fundamental human rights, and release all prisoners of conscience.
<P>	真相與和解乃一過程，整體轉型亦然。變革無法一蹴而就，故確立優先順序至關重要。於推進真相與和解程序時，首要任務應為確保言論自由、恢復基本人權，並釋放所有良心犯。
