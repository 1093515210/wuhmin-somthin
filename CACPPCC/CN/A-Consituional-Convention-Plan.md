  # A-Constitutional-Convention-Plan/制宪会议方案
<p>匿名</p>

  ## The Constitutional Process/憲政程序
<P>	For China to establish a constitutional democracy in the future, it will need to abandon the existing constitution and draft a new one. In general, this process will involve the following steps:
<P>	若中國未來欲建立憲政民主，須廢止現行憲法並起草新憲。概言之，此程序包含下列步驟：
<P>	1.     The government convenes a constitutional convention.
<P>	一、政府召集制憲會議。
<P>	2.The constitutional convention drafts a new constitution.
<P>	二、制憲會議起草新憲。
<P>	3.The convention submits a draft of the new constitution to the parliament, which represents the will of the people, for deliberation and approval.
<P>	三、會議將新憲草案提交予代表民意之議會，進行審議與通過。
<P>	Responsibility for convening the constitutional convention will lie with the new government that replaces the authoritarian CCP. This new government will not be democratically elected and will therefore not have the mandate of the people, making it temporary and transitional, and one of its main responsibilities will be drafting a new constitution.
<P>	召集制憲會議之責，應由取代威權中共之新政府承擔。此新政府並非經民主選舉產生，故不具民意授權，僅具臨時過渡性質；其主要職責之一，即為起草新憲。

  ### About the Constitutional Convention/關於制憲會議
<P>	The number of participants in the constitutional convention can range from 151 to 201 people and should include individuals from the following categories:
<P>	制憲會議參與人數可介於 151 至 201 人之間，成員應涵蓋下列類別：
<P>	1-Representatives of provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities, regardless of size, which nominate an equal number of delegates
<P>	一、各省、自治區及直轄市代表，不論規模大小，均應提名等額代表。
<P>	2. Representatives of major political parties
<P>	二、主要政黨代表。
<P>	3. Representatives of various sectors (such as education, labor, business, and religious groups)
<P>	三、各界代表（如教育、勞工、工商及宗教團體）。
<P>	4. Representatives of minority groups and women
<P>	四、少數民族與女性代表。
<P>	5. Constitutional law experts and scholars
<P>	五、憲法專家與學者。
<P>	6. Civilian representatives
<P>	六、平民代表。
<P>	7. Overseas representatives
<P>	七、海外代表。
<P>	8. Representatives of the interim government
<P>	八、過渡政府代表。
<P>	A challenging question for this process is who should decide who attends the constitutional convention. For example, if a province has 10 seats in the convention, who should fill them?  How should the province select these representatives?
<P>	此程序面臨一項挑戰：應由誰決定制憲會議之成員？例如，若某省擁有十席代表，應由誰填補？該省又應如何選拔此類代表？
<P>	For example, should the old CCP cadre decide who will attend the constitutional convention? Obviously, this is not ideal. However, if the Chinese people do not let them decide, whom should they allow? If China cannot find a workable mechanism, it may have to let the old CCP cadre make decisions temporarily. Given the nature of China’s current system, when the central leadership changes, the changes will ripple down. Therefore, letting former CCP members govern briefly might be a practical option. The process of drafting a new constitution will be transitional, and abolishing all the old elements of government at once will be impossible. So in the initial phase of the transition, China may inevitably need to use some of the old elements.
<P>	例如，應由原中共幹部決定制憲會議成員否？顯然此舉並不理想。然若中國人民不讓其決定，又應交由誰？倘中國無法找到可行機制，或須暫時由原中共幹部決策。鑒於中國現行制度之本質，中央領導層更迭時，變革將由上而下傳遞；故讓前中共成員短暫執政，或屬務實選項。起草新憲乃過渡過程，欲一舉廢除所有舊有政府元素實屬不可能；是以在轉型初期，中國或難免須沿用部分舊有元素。
<P>	Regarding civilian and overseas representatives, China’s civilian population and overseas Chinese and ethnic minority communities have many talented individuals and groups who can participate. These include independent scholars, pro-democracy organizations, human rights advocates, Falun Gong practitioners, independent churches, Tibetan exile groups, Uyghur advocacy groups, and Mongolian advocacy groups. So the constitutional convention should include representatives of both the civilian population and overseas communities, and these groups should choose them through their respective mechanisms.
<P>	至於平民與海外代表，中國平民、海外華僑及少數族裔社群皆有許多人才與團體可參與，包括獨立學者、民主組織、人權倡議者、法輪功學員、獨立教會、藏人流亡團體、維吾爾倡議團體及蒙古倡議團體等。故制憲會議應納入平民與海外社群代表，並由該等團體透過各自機制選出。
<P>	After the representatives arrive, an expert committee can draft the constitution, and individuals from the interim government or those designated by the interim government may chair the convention.
<P>	代表集結後，可由專家委員會起草憲法，並由過渡政府成員或其指定人員主持會議。
<P>	The constitutional process should involve public participation, and the public should be able to express their views on the new constitution. Its progress should be largely transparent to facilitate public awareness and engagement in discussions within society. Once the convention drafts a new constitution, the representatives will submit it to the parliament for deliberation and approval.
<P>	憲政程序應納入公眾參與，使民眾得以對新憲表達見解。其進程應具高度透明度，以促進社會大眾之認知與討論參與。待會議完成新憲草案後，代表將其提交議會審議並通過。
	
  ### Electing a New National Congress/選舉新任全國代表大會
<P>	The parliament responsible for deliberating and approving the new constitution should be a true representation of the people’s will. Therefore, the existing National People’s Congress is not up to the task. The people have not genuinely elected this body, and it has long served as a rubber-stamping entity under the CCP’s oppressive rule. Therefore, it does not represent the will of the people and is not qualified to undertake the significant responsibility of reviewing and approving a new constitution. In other words, during the process of drafting a new constitution, the Chinese people will need to elect a parliament through genuine democratic elections that truly represent their will.
<P>	負責審議並通過新憲之議會，應為民意之真實代表。是以，現行之全國人民代表大會不符此項任務：該機構並非經由人民真實選舉產生，且長期於中共高壓統治下充當橡皮圖章；故其既不代表民意，亦無資格承擔審查與批准新憲之重大責任。換言之，於起草新憲之過程中，中國人民須透過真正的民主選舉，選出能真實代表其民意之議會。
<P>	How should China form this parliament, and what rules and procedures should it follow? There are two methods to consider. First, the country can follow the existing constitution. China’s constitution and electoral laws include a set of rules and procedures for electing representatives at all levels, including those for the National People’s Congress. However, the CCP has always manipulated elections and turned them into sham elections by restricting free speech. Once there is freedom of speech, sham elections immediately become real elections, and a sham parliament (People’s Congress) becomes a real one as well. Citizens elected the first real parliaments in the former Soviet Union, Eastern European countries, Mongolia, and elsewhere according to the rules and procedures of their old constitution in an open atmosphere with freedom of speech. China could also form its first real parliament using this method.
<P>	中國應如何組成議會，並應遵循何種規則與程序？此處有二法可考。其一，可沿用現行憲法。中國憲法與選舉法雖已訂定各級代表（含全國人大代表）之選舉規則與程序，然中共因限制言論自由，長期操弄選舉，使其淪為虛假形式。一旦言論自由得以實現，虛假選舉將轉為真實選舉，虛假議會（人民代表大會）亦將成為真實議會。前蘇聯、東歐國家、蒙古等地之公民，皆是在言論自由之開放氛圍下，依據舊憲法之規則與程序，選出首屆真實議會；中國亦可採此法建立首屆真實議會。
<P>	However, there are drawbacks to this approach. According to China’s old constitution and electoral laws, voters directly elect only county-level representatives, while representatives at the provincial and national levels are indirectly elected. This compromises the ability of the National People’s Congress to reflect the will of the voters. Furthermore, this method would require three consecutive elections: first for representatives of counties; then for representatives of provincial governments, municipalities, and autonomous regions; and finally for representatives of the National People’s Congress. The process would be time-consuming and costly.
<P>	然此法存在弊端。依舊憲與選舉法，選民僅能直選縣級代表，省級及國家級代表則採間接選舉；此舉削弱全國人大反映民意之能力。此外，此法須進行三次連續選舉：首為縣級代表，次為省級、直轄市及自治區代表，最後始為全國人大代表；程序繁瑣且耗費鉅資。
<P>	Second, China can propose a new and different method, allowing voters to directly elect representatives to the National People’s Congress. This would require introducing a new procedure to elect a parliament before the establishment of the new constitution and corresponding electoral laws.
<P>	其二，中國可提出全新方法，允許選民直選全國人大代表。此舉須於新憲與新選舉法確立前，先行引入一套選舉議會之新程序。
<P>	This may seem like a chicken-and-egg problem. If China uses the second method to elect a real parliament, then this real parliament cannot approve the method. The government must enact the method unilaterally, and therefore it would have only transitional and temporary significance.
<P>	此舉或有「先有雞或先有蛋」之疑慮：若中國採第二法選出真實議會，則該議會本身無法批准該選舉方法；故須由政府單方面頒布，其性質僅具過渡與臨時意義。
<P>	In summary, during the early stages of a transition—when the new government abandons certain provisions of the old constitution and old regulations due to inapplicability, and it has not yet established the new constitution and corresponding new regulations through the necessary procedures—the government will need to enact a set of temporary regulations. These should include rules for electing members of parliament (National People’s Congress representatives). This step is indispensable and unavoidable in the constitutional process.
<P>	總結而言，於轉型初期——即新政府因不適用而廢止舊憲部分條文，且尚未經必要程序建立新憲與新規章之際——政府須頒布一系列臨時規章，其中應包含議會成員（全國人大代表）之選舉規則。此步驟於憲政程序中乃不可或缺且勢必經過之環節。

  ### Key Points of the New Constitution/新憲要點
<P>	The new constitution should aim to achieve six important goals:
<P>	新憲應旨在達成下列六大目標：
<P>	1-Elevate fundamental human rights to a paramount position.
<P>	一、將基本人權提升至至高地位。
<P>	2. Establish a democratic government structure, a parliamentary system with two chambers (a senate and a house of representatives). The house of representatives represents the population proportionality, and the cabinet is formed from it. The senate mainly represents regional diversity and may also include a certain proportion of representatives from various interest groups. The cabinet obtains authorization from the parliament and is accountable to it. The head of state nominates the prime minister, and the parliament approves the appointment. The head of state is a ceremonial president.
<P>	二、建立民主政府架構，採兩院制議會制（參議院與眾議院）。眾議院依人口比例代表，內閣由其組成。參議院則主要代表地域多樣性，並可納入一定比例之各界利益團體代表。內閣受議會授權並對其負責。國家元首提名總理，並由議會批准任命；國家元首為禮儀性總統。
<P>	3. Establish a state structure (a federal system). Explicitly define local autonomy in the constitution for each province. Minority autonomous regions should have greater autonomy in language and script, among other aspects. Hong Kong and Macau should have more autonomy. Taiwan is subject to further discussion.
<P>	三、建立國家結構（聯邦制）。於憲法中明確界定各省之地方自治權；少數民族自治區於語言、文字等面向應擁有更高自治權。香港與澳門應具備更高自治權；台灣問題則有待進一步討論。
<P>	4. Establish an electoral system featuring proportional representation and ranked-choice voting.
<P>	四、建立採比例代表制與排序複選制之選舉制度。
<P>	5. Establish judicial independence.
<P>	五、確立司法獨立。
<P>	6. Nationalize the military.
<P>	六、軍隊國有化。
<P>	Due to space limitations, this text will not delve into detailed discussions of these points. Instead, the following section highlights a few important issues.
<P>	受篇幅限制，本文不就上述要點進行詳盡討論，下文將轉而重點說明若干重要議題。

  ### Implications of the Hu Huanyong Line/胡煥庸線之啟示
<P>	In 1935, geographer Hu Huanyong（胡煥庸） proposed drawing a straight line from Heihe（黑河） in Heilongjiang（黑龍江） to Tengchong（雲南騰沖） in Yunnan, dividing China into two parts: the southeast and the northwest. Only 4 percent of China’s population inhabits the northwest, which makes up about 60 percent of the country’s land area, while the southeast, covering 40 percent of the land, is home to 96 percent of the population. This line is known as the Hu Line, Heihe-Tengchong, or the Anhui-Tengchong Line.
<P>	1935年，地理學家胡煥庸提出劃定一條由黑龍江黑河至雲南騰沖之直線，將中國分為東南與西北兩部分。西北地區面積約佔全國六成，卻僅居住百分之四之人口；反之，東南地區面積僅佔四成，卻承載了百分之九十六之人口。此線即為「胡煥庸線」，亦稱「黑河—騰沖線」。
<P>	Observing the Hu Line, it becomes apparent that several ethnic minorities in China—such as the Tibetans, Uyghurs, and Mongols, who differ significantly from the Han majority—primarily reside in the northwest, while the Han and other ethnically similar groups inhabit the southeast.
<P>	觀察胡煥庸線可知，中國數個與漢族多數顯著不同之少數民族——如藏族、維吾爾族及蒙古族——主要居住於西北；漢族及其他族裔相近之族群則多棲於東南。
<P>	The Hu Line suggests that the future design of China’s institutions, in addition to recognizing equal citizenship rights, should establish a diverse system based on non-majority and noncitizen voting. Otherwise, the majority will drown out and overlook the voices of minority groups like the Tibetans, Uyghurs, and Mongols. This implies the need for ethnic regional autonomy in such areas and ensures that these ethnic groups have a greater representation in parliament than their population proportion.
<P>	胡煥庸線顯示，中國未來制度設計除保障平等公民權外，應建立基於「非多數」與「非公民」投票權之多元制度；否則，多數群體將淹沒並忽視藏、維、蒙等少數族群之聲音。此意指該等地區須實施民族區域自治，並確保其於議會中之代表比例高於其人口比例。
<P>	The Hu Line also indicates that, at this stage, the non-Han ethnic groups mentioned above are unlikely to achieve independence from China. Achieving independence without the consent or acquiescence of the majority ethnic group is virtually impossible, as the power imbalance in favor of the majority is too great. However, obtaining the majority’s consent for the secession of minority groups is not an easy task, as it requires them to cede a significant portion of territory.
<P>	胡煥庸線亦預示，現階段上述非漢族群恐難脫離中國實現獨立。於未獲多數族群同意或默許下，因多數群體之權力優勢過大，獨立幾無可能；然欲獲多數群體同意其分裂，亦非易事，因其須割讓大量領土。
<P>	The pursuit of independence by non-Han ethnic groups has a moral justification, but in the early stages of transition, the strong Han-centric views of unity are likely to prevail. The authoritarian forces, which the Chinese people have already marginalized, can easily exploit the rhetoric of safeguarding unity to suppress opposition and possibly stage a comeback. Ultimately, the pursuit of independence by minority ethnic groups may face brutal repression, and the overall democratic progress of the country could suffer a severe setback. Therefore, government officials should temporarily set aside complex issues like separatism that can easily lead to zero-sum games during the early stages of a transition. Instead, the new government should implement genuine autonomy in non-Hanethnic regions, following the Middle Way approach that the Dalai Lama advocates.
<P>	非漢族群追求獨立雖具道義正當性，然於轉型初期，強烈之漢中心主義統一觀恐占主導地位。已被人民邊緣化之威權勢力，極易利用「維護統一」之辭令來鎮壓異議，甚至捲土重來。最終，少數民族對獨立之追求或面臨殘酷鎮壓，致使國家民主進程遭受嚴重挫敗。是以，政府官員於轉型初期，應暫時擱置易引發零和遊戲之分離主義等複雜議題；相對而言，新政府應遵循達賴喇嘛所倡導之「中道」，於非漢族區域實施真正的自治。

  ### Boundaries of Existing Administrative Regions/現行行政區劃界線
<P>	The current division of administrative regions, primarily provinces, has some areas of concern and may not be entirely rational. However, attempting to redraw these boundaries hastily during the initial implementation of a constitutional democracy could lead to disputes and may have destructive consequences. Therefore, the new government should not change these boundaries while drafting the new constitution.
<P>	現行行政區劃（以省為單位）存有疑義，且未必完全合理。然於憲政民主實施初期，若急於重劃界線，恐引發爭端並導致破壞性後果；是以，新政府於起草新憲期間，不應變動此類界線。
<P>	However, the situation is different when it comes to the division of ethnic autonomous regions. In theory, for a region to become an autonomous region for a particular ethnic minority under the conditions of constitutional democracy, that ethnic minority should make up at least half of the population with voting rights. Otherwise, that minority will struggle to gain a majority of seats in parliament through elections, making ethnic autonomy difficult to achieve.
<P>	惟民族自治區之劃分則另當別論。理論上，於憲政民主條件下，一區域若欲成為特定少數民族之自治區，該民族應佔具投票權人口至少半數；否則，該民族恐難透過選舉取得議會多數席次，致使民族自治難以實現。
<P>	Understanding the demography of three major non-Han ethnic autonomous regions—Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region, Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region, and Tibet Autonomous Region—can help illuminate the issues:
<P>	藉由分析三大非漢族自治區——內蒙古、新疆及西藏之人口結構，可釐清此議題：
<P>	1-Inner Mongolia: When the government established this region in 1947, its total population was 5.617 million, with the Mongolian population accounting for only 832,000, or 14.81 percent.293 According to the 2020 census, the region’s permanent population was 24.0492 million, of which 4.2478 million were Mongolian, accounting for 17.66 percent.
<P>	一、內蒙古：1947年該區成立時，總人口為561.7萬，蒙古族僅83.2萬，占比14.81%。依2020年人口普查，該區常住人口為2404.92萬，其中蒙古族424.78萬，占比17.66%。
<P>	2. Xinjiang: According to the first national population census in 1953, Xinjiang had a total population of 4.7836 million, with the Uyghur population at 3.6076 million, representing75.42 percent. The 2020 census found Xinjiang had a total population of 25.8523 million, with 11.6243 million Uyghurs, accounting for 44.96 percent.
<P>	二、新疆：依1953年首次全國人口普查，新疆總人口為478.36萬，維吾爾族360.76萬，占比75.42%。2020年普查顯示，新疆總人口為2585.23萬，其中維吾爾族1162.43萬，占比44.96%。
<P>	3. Tibet: Tibetans have always been the absolute majority here. According to the 2020 census, the region’s permanent population was 3.6481 million, with the Tibetan population at 3.1379 million, accounting for 86.1 percent.
<P>	三、西藏：藏族向來為絕對多數。依2020年普查，該區常住人口為364.81萬，藏族313.79萬，占比86.1%。
<P>	With this in mind, Tibet’s boundary does not require revision, while Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia may benefit from boundary adjustments.
<P>	綜上所述，西藏界線無需修訂，而新疆與內蒙古則或可透過界線調整以利發展。
<P>	In Xinjiang, from a regional distribution perspective, the Uyghur population is mainly concentrated in four southern prefectures: Kashgar, Hotan, Aksu, and Kizilsu Kyrgyz Autonomous Prefecture. According to the 2020 national population census data, 74.01 percent of Uyghurs in Xinjiang reside in these southern areas, where they constitute 83.74 percent of the local population. Therefore, it may be appropriate to divide Xinjiang into Southern Xinjiang, which becomes the Uyghur Autonomous Region, and Northern Xinjiang, which is not.
<P>	於新疆之區域分布來看，維吾爾族人口主要集中於南疆四地：喀什、和田、阿克蘇及克孜勒蘇柯爾克孜自治州。依2020年全國人口普查，新疆七成四之維吾爾族人口居住於上述南疆地區，並占該地人口百分之八十三點七四。是以，將新疆劃分為維吾爾自治區之「南疆」，以及非自治之「北疆」，或屬合宜。
<P>	The situation in Inner Mongolia is more complex. In principle, it makes sense to designate areas where the Mongolian population is there majority as the Mongolian Autonomous Region, while excluding the other areas.
<P>	內蒙古情勢則較為複雜。原則上，將蒙古族人口占多數之區域劃定為蒙古自治區，並排除其他區域，理應可行。
<P>	The convention should write the adjustment of Xinjiang’s and Inner Mongolia’s boundaries into the new constitution, but first, the new government will need to propose the changes in the temporary regulations it issues. The reason is simple: if it does not propose redrawing the boundaries of these two ethnic autonomous regions in the temporary regulations, then in the first elections of the new parliament, these regions will still elect their representatives based on the existing boundaries. In the current division, Uyghurs and Mongols are both minorities, and they may not gain a majority or even any representation in elections, which goes against the original intention of establishing ethnic autonomous regions.
<P>	制憲會議應將新疆與內蒙古之界線調整載入新憲，然新政府須先行於其頒布之臨時規章中提出變更建議。其原因顯而易見：若未於臨時規章中提議重劃此兩大民族自治區界線，則新議會首屆選舉仍將依現行劃分選出代表。於現行劃分下，維吾爾族與蒙古族皆屬少數族裔，恐於選舉中無法取得多數席次甚至全然無代表，此舉實有違設立民族自治區之初衷。
<P>	In several ethnic minority regions, the proportions of different ethnic groups have changed significantly over the past few decades. This is particularly true in Xinjiang, where the proportion of Uyghur people has decreased significantly due to an influx of Han Chinese settlers. The challenge is that the new government cannot force Han Chinese who have already settled in Xinjiang to leave, and therefore it will need to implement regulations to address these situations.
<P>	近數十年來，數個少數民族地區之族群比例已大幅變動。新疆尤甚，因漢族移民湧入，致維吾爾族比例顯著下降。其挑戰在於，新政府無法強制已定居新疆之漢族遷出，故須透過實施相關規章以應對此情勢。
<P>	In the future, ethnic autonomous regions can retain a certain degree of autonomy in matters related to immigration, similar to the arrangements in Hong Kong and Macau. Non-local residents should obtain formal resident status in the region through a specified process, including the right to vote locally. Purchasing property and hiring labor should also require permits, with priority given to formal local residents to prevent marginalization of local ethnic groups in their own regions and disadvantages in employment opportunities. The assimilation of different ethnic groups should take place without violating the will of the local ethnic minorities.
<P>	未來，民族自治區可就移民相關事務保有一定自治權，類同港澳之安排。非本地居民應經特定程序取得該區正式居民身分，並包含當地投票權；購置房產與聘僱勞工亦應採取許可制，並優先保障正式本地居民，以防本地族群於其家園遭邊緣化或在就業機會上處於劣勢。不同族群間之融合，應於不違背當地少數民族意願之前提下進行。
	
  ### Relationship with Taiwan/對台關係
<P>	Due to over 70 years of separation between the two sides of the Taiwan Strait, the people of Taiwan have grown accustomed to their separate existence. Even if mainland China were to transition to a democratic constitutional system, the people of Taiwan may not immediately choose reunification. Therefore, in the early stages of establishing democratic constitutional governance on the mainland, it is advisable not to prioritize reunification but to maintain the status quo.
<P>	鑒於兩岸已隔絕逾七十載，台灣人民已習慣兩岸分立之現狀。縱使中國大陸轉型為民主憲政體制，台灣人民亦未必會立即選擇統一；是以，於大陸建立民主憲政統治之初期，建議不應將統一列為首要任務，而應維持現狀。
<P>	Currently, according to the existing constitutional system of the Republic of China (Taiwan), mainland China is also part of its territory.299 According to the Constitution of the People’s Republic of China (PRC), Taiwan is part of the PRC’s territory. Since both sides claim the same territory, they both consider it one country rather than two.
<P>	目前，依中華民國（台灣）現行憲政體制，中國大陸亦屬其領土；依中華人民共和國憲法，台灣亦屬其領土。因雙方皆主張同一領土，故兩岸皆視其為一國，而非兩國。
<P>	Additionally, Taiwan’s constitution declares that Taiwan’s territory falls under its jurisdiction, indirectly acknowledging that mainland China is not under its jurisdiction. This effectively acknowledges PRC governance of the mainland. In other words, Taiwan recognizes the existence of the PRC government.
<P>	此外，台灣憲法宣告其管轄範圍僅限於台灣領土，此舉間接承認中國大陸並不在其管轄之下，實質上亦承認了中華人民共和國對大陸之統治。換言之，台灣認可中華人民共和國政府之存在。
<P>	On the mainland side, the PRC’s effective governance has always been limited to the mainland territory and has never extended to Taiwan. However, the PRC government has not legally recognized this status quo. The new government should legally acknowledge it. It should recognize that the mainland territory is within its jurisdiction, while the territory of Taiwan is not. In other words, the new government shouldacknowledge that the territory of Taiwan falls under the jurisdiction of Taiwan’s government, not the PRC government. This is the concept of one China, two governments, or one country, two administrations, similar to the relationship between the two Koreas.
<P>	就大陸而言，中華人民共和國之實際統治範圍始終僅限於大陸領土，從未延伸至台灣；然該政府尚未於法律上承認此種現狀。新政府應依法予以承認，即確認大陸領土屬其管轄，而台灣領土則非如此；換言之，新政府應承認台灣領土受台灣政府管轄，而非中華人民共和國政府。此即「一個中國、兩個政府」或「一個國家、兩個行政體系」之概念，類同於朝韓之關係。
<P>	One China, two governments means the two governments have equal status. It implies that the government of the Republic of China can also join international organizations, such as the United Nations. Further, it means foreign governments can establish formal diplomatic relations with both the PRC government and the Taiwanese government, among other possibilities.
<P>	「一個中國、兩個政府」意指兩政府地位平等，暗示中華民國政府亦得加入聯合國等國際組織；此外，外國政府亦得與中華人民共和國政府及台灣政府分別建立正式外交關係，或具其他可能性。
<P>	One country, two administrations does not mean reunification or independence. It retains the goal and vision of reunification while allowing room for independence. Therefore, both sides may be able to accept it.
<P>	「一個國家、兩個行政體系」並不等同於統一或獨立。此概念既保留統一之目標與願景，亦為獨立留有空間，故兩岸或皆能接受。
<P>	The mainland should not rush toward reunification, and Taiwan should not rush toward independence. If Taiwan seeks to establish its own constitution and declare independence too early in the process of constitutional democracy on the mainland, it may provoke mainland nationalism. The marginalized authoritarian forces may exploit the situation to incite nationalist fervor in the name of national unity, leading to the resurgence of authoritarianism and threatening cross-strait peace. Therefore, during the early stages of constitutional democracy on the mainland, Taiwan’s government should put the issue of independence on hold.
<P>	大陸不應急於統一，台灣亦不應急於獨立。若於大陸憲政民主進程初期，台灣即尋求制定憲法並宣布獨立，恐激起大陸民族主義；已被邊緣化之威權勢力或藉此藉「國家統一」之名煽動民族主義熱潮，導致威權主義復辟，威脅兩岸和平。是以，於大陸憲政民主初期，台灣政府應暫緩處理獨立議題。
	
  ## The Chinese Communist Party in a Time of Great Change/中共之巨變
<P>	In the context of China’s future transition to democracy, it is highly likely that the CCP will cease to exist. Drawing from the historical experiences of the Soviet Union, Eastern Europe, and Mongolia, when countries under communist rule experience a significant transformation, the ruling Communist Party often disbands, undergoes a complete rebranding, or changes its name and symbols.
<P>	於中國未來轉型為民主之背景下，高度可能中共將不復存在。借鏡前蘇聯、東歐及蒙古之歷史經驗，當共產政權經歷重大轉變時，執政之共產黨常會解散、徹底改頭換面或更名換標。
<P>	There are a few instances when the party retains its name and maintains a significant presence in politics; the Russian Communist Party is one such example. However, the current Russian Communist Party is not a direct continuation of the Soviet-era CPSU but was reestablished in 1993, and many of its prominent figures were dissidents during the Soviet era. Moreover, its political agenda and stance differ significantly from those of the CPSU. For example, the contemporary Russian Communist Party advocates for private property rights, endorses multi-party democracy, and supports a market economy. Some Russian scholars argue that the current party and its predecessor are effectively two distinct political entities.
<P>	少數情況下，黨仍保留名稱並在政治中扮演重要角色，例如俄羅斯共產黨，但現行俄共並非前蘇聯共產黨（CPSU）之直接延續，乃於1993年重建，且多重要人物曾為蘇聯時代異議份子。其政治主張亦大異於前蘇聯共產黨，例如現今俄共支持私產、多黨民主及市場經濟。部分俄羅斯學者認為，當前黨與前身實屬兩個獨立之政治實體。
<P>	With this in mind, there are essentially two ways in which the CCP may vanish:
<P>	綜上所述，中共之消亡基本上有二途：
<P>	1-Dissolution: its members either form new parties or join other political parties.
<P>	一、解散：其成員另組新黨，或加入其他政黨。
<P>	2. Transformation: the party’s constitution and name change, effectively moving away from the term Communist Party.
<P>	二、轉型：變更黨章與名稱，從而脫離「共產黨」之稱謂。
<P>	The CCP’s name has garnered a negative reputation over time, making association with it unattractive to many. Furthermore, the original constitution of the CCP includes clauses that uphold authoritarianism and oppose democratic principles, which it would need to discard in the process of transformation. The CCP’s constitution also promotes a communist ideological framework even though communism as an ideology has largely become obsolete. In practical terms, the CCP has already deviated from communism, so it is highly likely that any future amendment to the party’s constitution would remove all references to communism and its guiding role. In some other communist countries, the communist parties have names like Worker’s Party, Labor Party, or Socialist Party, with no explicit mention of communism. These parties may not necessarily change their names after a shift in ideology or policy. However, the specific inclusion of communist in the CCP’s name would make a name change imperative to signal a departure from that ideology.
<P>	中共名稱隨時間累積負面聲譽，使多數人對其避之唯恐不及。此外，中共原黨章包含維護威權並反對民主原則之條款，轉型時勢必須予以廢除。即便共產主義意識形態已趨式微，中共黨章仍推崇該框架；然就實務而言，中共早已偏離共產主義，故未來黨章修訂極可能刪除所有關於共產主義及其領導地位之內容。在其他共產國家，共產黨名稱或為工人黨、勞動黨或社會黨，並未明言「共產」。此類政黨於意識形態或政策轉變後未必須更名；惟中共名稱中明確包含「共產」二字，為彰顯脫離該意識形態，更名實屬必然。
<P>	After the CCP has rebranded as well as changed its flag and ideology, different political tendencies and ideologies within the former CCP will emerge, leading to the formation of various political factions. These factions will compete for control of the party, which will likely result in a significant split. Those who fail to secure control may leave the party and join other political groups or establish new parties of their own. Some may wish to continue carrying the Communist Party banner. However, their numbers are likely to be very limited.
<P>	待中共完成改名、變更黨旗與意識形態後，原黨內不同政治傾向與意識形態將顯露，進而形成各派系。各派系將爭奪黨權，恐導致大規模分裂：未能掌握控制權者或將退出原黨，轉而加入其他政治團體或另立新黨；亦有少數人欲繼續高舉共產黨旗幟，然其人數恐極為有限。
<P>	In the context of significant change, the CCP may hold a special representative assembly to change its party constitution and name. Given the party’s large membership of 90 million, there will be a substantial exodus, causing a major split. However, many members are likely to remain. In the early stages of party politics, other political parties may struggle to consolidate, leading to a landscape of diverse political groups. The reformed former Communist Party may still be one of the largest parties, given its history, membership composition, and connections, particularly with senior military leaders. This raises concerns about its potential to become an anti-democratic force.
<P>	於重大變革之際，中共或將召開特別代表大會，以變更黨章與名稱。鑒於該黨擁有九千萬龐大黨員，屆時必有大量成員流失，引發嚴重分裂；然仍有眾多成員可能留任。於政黨政治初期，其他政黨或難以迅速整合，致使政治版圖呈現多元群體並存之態勢。考量其歷史、黨員組成及與軍方高層之關係，改革後之原共產黨仍可能為最大政黨之一，此點恐引發其演變為反民主勢力之疑慮。
	
  ## Should the Country’s Name Change？/江山可改？
<P>	Many communist countries changed their names after transitioning to democracy. For example, the People’s Republic of Bulgaria became the Republic of Bulgaria; the People’s Republic of Hungary became Hungary; the People’s Republic of Albania became Albania; and the People’s Republic of Mongolia became Mongolia.
<P>	眾多共產國家於轉型民主後皆變更國名。例如：保加利亞人民共和國更名為保加利亞共和國；匈牙利人民共和國更名為匈牙利；阿爾巴尼亞人民共和國更名為阿爾巴尼亞；蒙古人民共和國亦更名為蒙古。
<P>	It is ironic that countries with People’s in their names often do not represent the people, but removal of the word signifies a shift toward a truly people-oriented government. If the People’s Republic of China were to change its name by removing People’s, its name would become the same as the English translation of the Republic of China (Taiwan). Considering that mainland China’s transition to democracy may not necessarily coincide with reunification with Taiwan, this name change might pose a challenge. Charter 08 suggests the name Chinese Federal Republic as an alternative
<P>	諷刺的是，國名帶有「人民」二字往往不代表人民；然去除之則象徵向真正以民為本之政府轉型。若中華人民共和國透過刪除「人民」二字來更名，其英文名稱將與「中華民國（台灣）」之英譯相同。鑒於大陸民主轉型未必與台灣統一同步，此項更名或將面臨挑戰。「零八憲章」則建議以「中華聯邦共和國」作為替代方案。
