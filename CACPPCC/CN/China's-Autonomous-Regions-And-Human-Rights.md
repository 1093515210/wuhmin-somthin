# CHINA’S AUTONOMOUS REGIONS AND HUMAN RIGHTS
<P>	中国的自治区与人权——Nina Shea

  ## Executive Summary/执行摘要
<P>	In the transition period between the collapse of CCP rule and the establishment of a functioning democratic government in China, the United States will need to lead an effort to head off ethnic violence, civil wars, and violent political retribution and score settling. To put Chinese society on a stable footing for the future, US policies to advance democracy, religious freedom, freedom of speech, and other human rights will be critical. This priority is the focus of this chapter.
<P>	中共覆亡至民主政府肇建之過渡期，美國須導引各方，以遏制民族衝突、內戰迭起與政治報復之私下清算。欲使華夏社會步入長治久安之軌道，則推動民主、宗教、言論自由及基本人權之美方政策至關重要。本章即聚焦於此核心任務。
<P>	US policies should aim to maximize the possibility of a peaceful, orderly transition process and should become operational as rapidly as possible as the dissolution of the CCP regime becomes apparent. The recommendations in this chapter emphasize diplomacy and civic action rather than kinetic military action. They should foster voluntary cooperation and participation by the people of what is now China, as far as possible.
<P>	美國政策應旨在極大化和平且有序之轉型機會，並於中共政權瓦解跡象顯現時，儘速轉入實務運作。本章建議強調外交手段與公民行動，而非直接軍事行動。應盡可能激發現今中國人民之自發合作與參與。
<P>	A Chinese proverb defines a crisis as “an opportunity riding a dangerous wind.” These policies will encourage China’s various peoples to see an opportunity to create a better future amid what is certain to be great political uncertainty. Overall, they should give cause to hope for an opportunity to build a freer and more prosperous, just, and secure future for themselves, whether they are ethnic and religious minorities or religious minorities within the Han majority. This will advance American interests in an orderly transition toward a stable China at peace with its own peoples, including its minorities, and its neighbors.
<P>	中國諺語將危機定義為「危中有機」。這些政策將鼓勵中國各族群，在必然面臨巨大政治不確定性的環境中，看見創造美好未來之契機。整體而言，無論是少數民族、宗教少數群體，或是漢族主體中的宗教少數派，此舉皆應賦予其希望，使其相信有機會建立一個更自由、繁榮、公正且安全的未來。這將有助於美國實現戰略利益，推動中國走向穩定轉型，使其能與國內各族群及鄰國和平共處。
<P>	This chapter draws on historical precedents to identify successful measures and lessons learned. On the one hand, the sudden collapse of Soviet communism three and a half decades ago ended the Cold War without a shot fired. Its first stages saw the liberation of millions of captive people who formed 20 new republics in Central and Eastern Europe, which have largely gravitated to the West in what historians consider a mostly peaceful transition. As the historian of the State Department describes the collapse of the Soviet Union itself two years later, “On December 25, 1991, the Soviet hammer and sickle flag lowered for the last time over the Kremlin, thereafter replaced by the Russian tricolor. Earlier in the day, Mikhail Gorbachev resigned his post as president of the Soviet Union, leaving Boris Yeltsin as president of the newly independent Russian state.” The historian’s record observes, “People all over the world watched in amazement at this relatively peaceful transition from former Communist monolith into multiple separate nations.”
<P>	本章借鑑歷史先例，歸納成功措施與經驗教訓。一方面，三十五年前蘇聯共產主義之突然崩解，在未發一彈的情況下終結了冷戰。其初期階段解放了數百萬受壓迫人民，並於中東歐促成二十個新共和國成立；這些國家在歷史學家視為大致和平之轉型過程中，已大規模向西方靠攏。正如國務院歷史學家描述兩年後蘇聯崩解之情景：「1991 年 12 月 25 日，蘇聯錘子與鐮刀旗在克里姆林宮最後一次降下，隨後由俄羅斯三色旗取代。當日稍早，米哈伊爾·高布查科夫辭去蘇聯總統職務，由鮑里斯·葉爾欽接任新獨立俄羅斯國家之總統。」該史料紀錄觀察到：「全世界皆驚訝於這場從昔日共產主義單一體制轉變為多個獨立國家之相對和平轉型。」
<P>	On the other hand, the examples of South Sudan and Iraq provide warnings of the dangers that can occur when abrupt regime change occurs in societies with religious, tribal, and political animosities or when border disputes are unresolved.
<P>	另一方面，南蘇丹與伊拉克之案例則發出警示：在存在宗教、部落與政治仇恨，或邊界爭端未決之社會，政權更迭若過於倉促，恐將釀成巨大危機。
<P>	Implementing appropriate US policies in the context of regime change is an immensely complex operation everywhere, and even more so when it occurs within a population as vast as China’s. The undertaking will require calibrating varied policies or sets of policies to the needs of each region, locale, and demographic of that diverse country. Providing reassurances to and meeting the needs of the Uyghur Muslims, whom the CCP targeted for genocide in Xinjiang, and the repressed citizens of Hong Kong, for example, demand distinct approaches.209 And those measures will differ again from those for the majority Han population on the mainland.
<P>	在政權更迭的背景下，執行適當之美國政策，無論在何處皆屬極其複雜之行動；若發生於中國如此龐大之人口規模中，難度更甚。此項任務須根據該多元國家各區域、各地點及各族群之需求，精準校準各種政策或政策組合。例如，針對曾遭中共於新疆進行種族滅絕計畫之維吾爾穆斯林，以及受壓迫之香港公民，提供安全保障並滿足其需求，皆須採取截然不同之手段。此外，上述措施與針對大陸漢族主體人口之政策亦有所差異。
<P>	This chapter outlines a plan of action that the United States government should adopt, with the endorsement of Congress, to obtain a political consensus that can continue no matter who is in the White House. As the next section shows, even President George H.W. Bush came into office in January 1989 with policy assumptions concerning the Soviet Union that differed from those of the prior Reagan administration, in which he had just served as vice president. The fall of the Berlin Wall occurred only months after Bush took office. His questioning and review of the prior administration’s goal of defeating Soviet communism delayed the US policy response, leaving the United States inactive at a critical time. As it turned out, the Eastern and Central European transitions were almost miraculously peaceful, but the United States cannot count on events to unfold as smoothly in China. It should be prepared.
<P>	本章概述美國政府應採取之行動計畫。該計畫應經國會認可，以達成無論白宮由誰執掌皆能持續推動之政治共識。如下一節所示，即便如喬治·H·W·布希總統，於 1989 年 1 月就任時，其對蘇聯之政策假設亦與其剛擔任副總統之雷根政府有所不同。柏林圍牆倒塌發生於布希就任僅數月之後。由於布希對前任政府「擊敗蘇聯共產主義」目標之質疑與重新審視，導致美國政策反應遲緩，致使美國在關鍵時刻處於不作為狀態。儘管東歐與中歐之轉型過程近乎奇蹟般地和平，但美國絕不可寄望中國情勢亦能如此順遂發展。美國應做好萬全準備。
<P>	The plan’s eventual implementation will involve the State Department and Justice Department as well as public and private organizations and experts. The US government should form an interagency task force to coordinate this effort.
<P>	該計畫最終執行，將涉及國務院、司法部以及各類公私組織與專家。美國政府應成立跨部門專案小組，以統籌此項工作。
<P>	The plan depends on US leadership and cooperation with partners in existing international alliances that focus on China. While no single international or regional political infrastructure is available to serve this purpose, US partners could include the countries in the trilateral security pact for the Indo-Pacific region, AUKUS (a security pact that includes Australia, the UK, and the US). In addition, they could include New Zealand and Canada, which are part of the Five Eyes intelligence-sharing alliance already in place, and regional allies Taiwan, Japan, South Korea, India, and the Philippines. Some of these nations are part of the Quadrilateral Security Dialogue, or Quad, which focuses on Indo-Pacific security and which some observers are already viewing as a potential Pacific NATO.
<P>	此計畫之成敗，取決於美國之領導力，以及與現有關注中國之國際聯盟夥伴進行合作。儘管目前尚無單一國際或區域政治架構可供此用途，但美國夥伴可涵蓋印太地區三邊安全協定之成員國，以及 AUKUS（包含澳洲、英國與美國之安全協定）。此外，亦可納入現有「五眼聯盟」成員紐西蘭與加拿大，以及區域盟友台灣、日本、南韓、印度與菲律賓。其中部分國家屬於「四方安全對話」（Quad），該機制專注於印太安全，部分觀察家已將其視為潛在之「太平洋版北約」。
<P>	The specific policies for the US in this chapter fall into three broad categories to achieve the principal goal of averting social violence and promoting social stability during China’s political transition. They also largely draw from the United States’ approach to the collapse of the Soviet Union. According to the State Department account, in addition to working to prevent “nuclear catastrophe,” “amidst quick, dramatic changes across the landscape of the Soviet Union, Bush administration officials prioritized . . . the curbing of ethnic violence, and the stable transition to new political orders.”
<P>	本章所列美国之具体对策，皆围绕三大昭昭之目，以期达其要旨——即于中国政治转型之期，弭平社会暴乱，促其大局稳定。此诸般方略，多沿袭当年美国因应苏联解体之旧轨。据国务院文献所载，彼时布什政府除戮力防范“核劫”之外，“面对苏俄大地迅猛剧烈之变局，首务乃在遏制族群冲突，并力促新政体之平稳过渡。
<P>	The policy categories discussed here are as follows:
<P>	本文討論之政策範疇如下：
<P>	1. Considerations for determining the future political status of the special administrative region of Hong Kong and the five autonomous regions Guangxi, Xinjiang, Tibet, Inner Mongolia, and Ningxia. By law, the CCP allows these regions a degree of self-governance and ability to legislate, but in practice it curtails that freedom. Together, these regions represent some 118 million people and include the homelands of China’s most oppressed, and thus aggrieved, ethnic minorities.
<P>	關於釐定香港特別行政區，以及廣西、新疆、西藏、內蒙古、寧夏五大自治區未來政治地位之考量。 依據法律，中共允許該等區域享有一定程度自治權與立法能力，然實務上卻限制此等自由。此等區域合計人口約一億一千八百萬，亦為中國受壓迫最深、怨懟最重之少數民族聚居地。
<P>	2. Measures to immediately end severe human rights violations against the peoples of these hard-hit regions and to foster optimism that justice will be served without extralegal violence or retribution. These include specific measures to end the ongoing genocide against the Uyghur, Kazah, Tajik, and other Turkic and Muslim people of Xinjiang.
<P>	旨在立即終止對上述受苦地區人民嚴重人權侵害之措施，並培養其對司法正義之樂觀態度，確保其不須透過非法暴力或報復即可獲取公義。 此項措施包含終結針對新疆維吾爾、哈薩克、塔吉克及其他突厥語系與穆斯林人民持續進行之種族滅絕行為的具體手段。
<P>	3. Measures to alleviate the fears and frustrations of various groups in mainland China. Christians and Falun Gong are two large religious minorities who are mostly Han and together account for up to 200 million people. The CCP also targets human rights lawyers, student protestors, and other dissidents in China’s majority Han population with severe repression throughout China.
<P>	旨在緩解中國大陸各族群恐懼與挫敗感之措施。 基督徒與法輪功為兩大宗教少數群體，成員多屬漢族，總數高達兩億人。中共亦在全中國範圍內，對漢族主體中的人權律師、學生抗議者及其他異議人士實施嚴厲鎮壓。
<P>	These three broad concerns encompass communities and individuals who are now experiencing the most intense political suffering under the CCP. These populations are the ones most likely to give rise to unrest and retribution, if a new Chinese government ignores them, denies them justice, or leaves them without a clear and immediate path toward greater freedom.
<P>	上述三大關懷範疇，涵蓋了目前於中共統治下承受最劇烈政治苦難之社群與個人。若新中國政府忽視其需求、否定其正義，或使其失去通往更大自由之清晰且即時途徑，這些群體最有可能引發動盪與報復。
<P>	The plan’s eventual implementation will involve the State Department and Justice Department as well as public and private organizations and experts. The US government should form an interagency task force to coordinate this effort.
<P>	該計畫最終執行，將涉及國務院、司法部以及各類公私組織與專家。美國政府應成立跨部門專案小組，以統籌此項工作。
<P>	In addition, it is from these oppressed groups that the world could see massive refugee flows. Surveys of refugees escaping East Germany at the end of the Cold War are instructive: “In more than 90 percent of the refugee cases, contrary to the expectations of many Western analysts, the two most frequently mentioned reasons for leaving were (1) general political, not economic, conditions and (2) the lack of political freedom, not consumer goods.” 
<P>	此外，世界亦可能因這些受壓迫群體，目睹大規模難民潮之湧現。冷戰結束時，針對逃離東德難民之調查具備高度啟示意義：「在超過九成之難民案例中，與多數西方分析家預期相反，最常被提及之離境理由為：（一）政治環境而非經濟條件；（二）缺乏政治自由而非缺乏消費品。」
<P>	These three points are the subject of the subsequent sections.
<P>	这三点是后续各节的主题。

  ## Autonomous Regions/自治区
<P>	Under China’s law, the CCP gives a handful of geographic regions in China a degree of self-governance for administrative, legislative, and judicial purposes, though the party permits none to have a separate defense force or militia. It limits and manipulates this relative autonomy in practice, and in most cases the regions’ populations face extreme oppression and chafe under CCP rule. As the CCP regime weakens, such areas may begin to declare sovereignty, following the pattern of the dissolution of the Soviet empire in which every region with a legal claim to sovereignty acted on it within three years. They will require immediate help from the United States and its allies to resolve their future legal status and overcome their painful past.
<P>	依據中國法律，中共賦予若干地理區域行政、立法與司法層面之一定程度自治權，然黨並不允許任何地區擁有獨立之防衛力量或民兵。實務上，中共對此類相對自治權進行限縮與操弄；多數情況下，該等區域人口面臨極端壓迫，對中共統治深感怨懟。隨著中共政權衰弱，上述地區可能仿效蘇聯帝國解體之模式，開始宣示主權——在蘇聯解體過程中，凡具備主權法律主張之區域，皆於三年內採取行動。這些地區將需要美國及其盟友之即時協助，以解決其未來法律地位，並克服過往之痛苦歷史。
<P>	The five officially designated autonomous regions are Inner  Mongolia, Guangxi, Tibet, Ningxia, and Xinjiang (see map 1). In addition, Hong Kong and Macau hold the status of special administrative regions. With the end of the CCP regime, these regions, representing some 118 million people, will each face the decision of whether to remain in China with reinforced autonomy or to separate and declare themselves independent sovereign states. Some may also consider unifying with neighboring states.
<P>	五個官方指定之自治區為內蒙古、廣西、西藏、寧夏與新疆（參見地圖一）。此外，香港與澳門亦享有特別行政區地位。隨著中共政權終結，這些區域，共計約一億一千八百萬人口，將面臨抉擇：是選擇於中國境內保留強化後之自治權，抑或分離並宣布獨立為主權國家。部分區域亦可能考慮與鄰國合併。
<P>	Working with its allies in the Indo-Pacific region, the United States should be prepared to support and advise these regions in making this decision. The US and its allies should not automatically assume a province will become independent due to its autonomous status in the PRC. Instead, American support for independence should depend on the will of the people of the province, various regional factors, and conditions that Washington defines in a set of guidelines reflecting US government interests.
<P>	美國應與印太區域盟友合作，準備支持並諮詢該等區域，協助其做出此項決定。美方及其盟友不應僅因某省份於中華人民共和國體制下享有自治地位，即理所當然地預設其將走向獨立。相對地，美國對獨立之支持，應取決於該省份人民之意願、各種區域因素，以及華府於一套反映美國政府利益之指導方針中定義之條件。
<P>	The US government should adopt principles to determine which autonomous provinces aiming to separate from China merit American support. Principles that the George H.W. Bush administration adopted on the eve of the Soviet Union’s dissolution provide a solid basis. As that dissolution appeared imminent, on September 4, 1991, Secretary of State James Baker set forth five principles to guide US policy, establishing that, if prospective republics followed these principles, they could expect “cooperation and assistance from the United States.” These principles were:
<P>	美國政府應採納特定原則，以判定哪些尋求脫離中國之自治省份，值得美國支持。喬治·H·W·布希政府於蘇聯解體前夕所採納之原則，提供了堅實基礎。隨著蘇聯解體跡象顯現，1991年9月4日，國務卿詹姆斯·貝克提出五項指導美國政策之原則，確立若未來共和國遵循此等原則，即可期望「獲得美國之合作與援助」。此五項原則為：
<P>	•Self-determination consistent with democratic principles
<P>	•Recognition of existing borders
<P>	•Support for democracy and rule of law
<P>	•Preservation of human rights and rights of national minorities
<P>	•Respect for international law and obligations
<P>	·以民主原則為基礎之自決權
<P>	·承認現有邊界
<P>	·支持民主與法治
<P>	·維護人權及少數民族權利
<P>	·尊重國際法與義務
<P>	The United States should take the lead in weighing whether secession is a viable option for each autonomous region, on a case-by-case basis and in consultation with regional leaders and neighboring powers. In some cases, Washington may determine that secession is unrealistic or is not in American interests, in which case the United States should make clear its opposition.
<P>	美國應牽頭，就每個自治區域是否具備獨立可行性，進行逐案評估，並諮詢區域領導人及鄰國勢力。在某些情況下，華府可能判定獨立不切實際，或不符美國利益，此時美國應明確表達其反對立場。
<P>	The recent case of Iraqi Kurdistan serves as a cautionary example for any region that might proceed with independence in the face of international opposition. After the fall of Saddam Hussein and Baath Party rule and the defeat of the Islamic State, in which the Kurdistan Regional Government played a pivotal role, this autonomous region in northern Iraq pursued secession. Iraq’s central government and the region’s immediate neighbors—including Turkey, which threatened the region—opposed secession. Independence would also have been against US stated interests. Still, Iraqi Kurdistan held a referendum on secession in 2017 in which 93 percent of voters favored independence for the region, including disputed territories. This maneuver proved to be a setback to both Kurdistan and regional peace. A military conflict with Iraq ensued in which Kurdistan lost control of 20 percent of its territory, its leader was forced to resign, and it remained under Iraqi rule.
<P>	近期伊拉克庫德斯坦地區之案例，對任何可能面臨國際反對而尋求獨立之區域，堪稱警示。在薩達姆·海珊及其復興黨政權倒台，並擊敗伊斯蘭國（庫德斯坦地區政府在此扮演關鍵角色）後，此伊拉克北部自治區域尋求獨立。伊拉克中央政府及其鄰近國家——包括對該區域構成威脅的土耳其——均反對獨立。獨立亦有違美國聲明之利益。儘管如此，伊拉克庫德斯坦地區仍於2017年舉行獨立公投，93%之投票者支持該區域獨立，包含爭議領土。此舉 proved to be a setback to both Kurdistan and regional peace. 隨後爆發與伊拉克之軍事衝突，庫德斯坦喪失20%領土控制權，其領導人被迫辭職，該區域仍處於伊拉克統治之下。
<P>	To facilitate self-determination within China’s autonomous regions, the alliance should help draw up plans for secret balloting and a transparent vote count, and it will need to convince China and the regions’ immediate neighbors to accept the outcome. Balloting should extend to areas with large numbers of diaspora from some of these regions since Beijing’s forced assimilation policies have promoted a majority Han influx into minority ethnic autonomous regions, while many of the native population have fled, been deported, or migrated to other parts of China. Since the 1959 CCP invasion of Tibet, for example, 150,000 Tibetan Buddhists have fled to refugee camps in India along with their spiritual head the Dalai Lama, of which as many as 40 percent have reportedly migrated recently to the West. In addition, since the 2020 imposition of a draconian national security law in Hong Kong, 88,000 Hong Kongers have left for Britain, availing themselves of its special visa policy, and 300,000 are likely to immigrate there within five years, according to the UK Home Office.213 There is a precedent for extraterritorial balloting in areas where sizeable South Sudanese and other diaspora populations relocated.

<P>	Some or most of these regions might not wait for internationally arranged and supervised referendums to declare their independence.   ### Hong Kong
<P>	With a population of nearly 7.5 million, Hong Kong has a designation as a special administrative region of China.223 Its Basic Law, or constitution, guarantees it “a high degree of autonomy” (Article 2), including “executive, legislative and independent judicial power” and the right to have a “capitalist system and way of life” free of communist control, until 2047.224 Beijing agreed to this unique framework, known as the one country, two systems principle, when the UK returned Hong Kong to China in 1997. But Beijing has broken its agreement and is actively forcing Hong Kong to come under the same CCP rule it applies to mainland China. With its demonstrable passion for democratic freedom, Hong Kong will undoubtedly be at the forefront of those seeking independence from China as soon as the opportunity arises. And with its recent democratic experience, high degree of human development, and vibrant economy, it is the most likely to succeed.
<P>	香港人口近七百五十萬，被指定為中國之特別行政區。其《基本法》（或憲法）保證其享有「高度自治」（第二條），包含「行政、立法及獨立司法權」，以及直至2047年不受共產控制之「資本主義制度和生活方式」。北京於1997年英國將香港移交中國時，同意此一獨特架構，即「一國兩制」原則。然北京已違背協議，正積極迫使香港納入其適用於中國大陸之相同中共統治之下。憑藉其對民主自由顯著之熱情，一旦機會出現，香港無疑將走在尋求脫離中國獨立之最前線。憑藉其近期之民主經驗、高度之人文發展及蓬勃經濟，其最有可能成功。
<P>	Beijing repudiated its agreement on Hong Kong in 2019 when it attempted to impose an extradition law that would deny Hong Kongers their right to a fair trial under the Basic Law. The extradition rule would require them to stand trial for certain offenses in China’s courts under CCP laws. On a single day, June 16, 2019, some 2 million Hong Kongers—over a quarter of its population—marched in protest against this encroachment on their rights and freedoms.225 While this pressure caused the Beijing-controled Hong Kong government to withdraw the extradition law, in 2020, the CCP regime cracked down on dissent and effectively ended Hong Kong’s special status by imposing the National Security Law. This vaguely worded, sweeping law ensures CCP control and effectively negates Hong Kongers’ democratic rights and freedoms. Days after its enactment, the Trump administration determined that Hong Kong was no longer sufficiently autonomous to warrant the benefits that the United States–Hong Kong Policy Act of 1992 had afforded it.
<P>	北京於2019年試圖強加引渡法，剝奪港人《基本法》下之公平審判權，從而否定其對香港之協議。該引渡條例將要求其就某些罪行，依據中共法律，於中國法院接受審判。於2019年6月16日單日，約兩百萬港人——佔其人口四分之一以上——遊行抗議此等侵犯其權利與自由之行為。儘管此壓力導致北京控制下之香港政府撤回引渡法，然於2020年，中共政權卻藉由實施《國家安全法》鎮壓異議，實質終結香港之特殊地位。此項措辭含糊、涵蓋廣泛之法律，確保了中共之控制，並實質上否定了港人享有的民主權利與自由。該法頒布數日後，川普政府判定香港不再享有足夠自治權，以致無法獲得1992年《美國—香港政策法》所賦予之優惠待遇。
<P>	Since 2019, some 10,000 Hong Kongers, from nonagenarian Cardinal Joseph Zen to a 13-year-old, have been arrested for violating the National Security Law or taking a stand for democracy.226 Of these, some 2,000are currently political prisoners, while many others, including independent publisher Jimmy Lai, anxiously await the disposition of their cases, according to the documentation of the Hong Kong Democratic Council in Washington, DC.
<P>	自2019年以來，約一萬名香港人，從九旬高齡的樞機主教陳日君到一名十三歲少年，因違反《國家安全法》或為民主發聲而遭逮捕。根據華盛頓香港民主委員會之記錄，其中約兩千人現為政治犯，另有許多人，包括獨立出版人黎智英，則焦慮地等待案件之裁決。
<P>	Moreover, Hong Kong, which has served as a global financial hub that was the envy of all Asia, is now seeing its international companies leave as CCP decrees make the acquisition of basic business data a serious national security crime. This across-the-board crackdown on Hong Kong’s civil, political, and economic freedoms is likely to spur its citizens to seek the restoration of democracy there as an urgent priority.
<P>	此外，香港曾為亞洲各國所豔羨之全球金融中心，如今隨著中共頒布將獲取基本商業數據視為嚴重國家安全罪之法令，其國際企業正紛紛撤離。此種對香港公民、政治及經濟自由之全面打壓，很可能將促使香港市民將恢復當地民主作為當務之急。
<P>	Hong Kong has a proto-national constitution in its Basis Law, a multiparty system, and a legislative and judicial infrastructure that it can reinvigorate with free and fair democratic elections, which should include those in the diaspora. It has been free of the ethnic tensions and border disputes that have impeded democracy in South Sudan and the Balkans. Hong Kong’s economy will likely strengthen with the reinstatement of the rule of law and fundamental freedoms. Like China’s other autonomous regions, it lacks a military and will need a plan for its future defense, an issue that is beyond the scope of this chapter.
<P>	香港在其《基本法》中擁有具備國家憲法雛形之法律，並存在一個多黨制，以及可藉由自由公平之民主選舉（應包含海外僑民投票）重振之立法與司法基礎設施。香港免受了阻礙南蘇丹及巴爾幹地區民主化進程之族裔緊張關係與邊界爭端。隨著法治及基本自由之恢復，香港經濟將可能增強。如同中國其他自治地區，香港缺乏軍隊，且需要一份未來國防規劃，此議題已超出本章範圍。
<P>	Liberated from the National Security Law and CCP interference in its electoral system, Hong Kong would be in an excellent position to quickly stabilize and succeed as a sovereign democracy. It can base a claim for sovereignty on the Basic Law, which grants it fundamental freedoms and the right to manage its own affairs, and on Beijing’s breach of the Sino-British Joint Declaration of 1984.229 The United States and its allies should be prepared to give Hong Kong political, legal, and diplomatic support to make the transition to independence, consistent with the US guidelines.
<P>	若香港擺脫《國家安全法》及中共對其選舉體系之干預，將能處於絕佳地位，迅速穩定並成功轉型為一個主權民主國家。香港可基於《基本法》主張其主權，該法賦予其基本自由及管理自身事務之權利，亦可基於北京違反1984年中英聯合聲明之行為。美國及其盟友應準備好，依照美國指導方針，給予香港政治、法律與外交支持，以促成其獨立轉型。

  ### Xinjiang
<P>	Xinjiang Autonomous Region will undoubtedly seek independence from China at the first opportunity, as no bonds of trust with Beijing can exist given the intense repression and discrimination it has suffered under CCP rule. Several well-organized Uyghur diaspora organizations advocate for a peaceful democratic transition. They express pride in the Turkic ethnic groups’ long history on the steppe of Central Asia, their millennia-old culture, and their Muslim religion.The United States should support Xinjiang in the determination of its future status.
<P>	新疆自治區無疑將在第一時間尋求脫離中國獨立，蓋因在中共統治下所遭受之嚴酷鎮壓與歧視，使其與北京間無法建立任何信任紐帶。數個組織良好之維吾爾族僑民組織，正倡導和平民主轉型。渠等對突厥語族群在中亞草原之悠久歷史、千年文化及其伊斯蘭宗教，均表自豪。美國應支持新疆決定其未來地位。
<P>	The region spans one-sixth of China’s landmass, and some Uyghurs refer to it as East Turkestan because of its distinct cultural identity as a Turkic Muslim homeland. Nearly half of its 26 million people are Turkic Muslims; the majority are Uyghurs but there are Kazakhs, Uzbeks, and Tajiks, while 42 percent of the overall population is Han Chinese. Though underdeveloped, it has economic potential, being rich in mineral deposits and natural resources and located along ancient trade routes and the Silk Road.
<P>	該區域佔中國陸地面積六分之一，部分維吾爾人因其作為突厥穆斯林家園之獨特文化認同，稱之為東突厥斯坦。其二千六百萬人口中，近半數為突厥穆斯林；其中多數為維吾爾人，亦有哈薩克族、烏茲別克族與塔吉克族，而總人口之42%為漢族。儘管經濟欠發達，新疆蘊藏豐富礦藏與自然資源，且地處古代貿易路線與絲綢之路沿線，具備經濟潛力。
<P>	Under its forced assimilation program, the CCP has targeted the Turkic ethnic groups for genocide, as then-Secretary of State Mike Pompeo stated in a determination in 2021 that Secretary of State Antony Blinken endorsed in the successive administration. The CCP regime has taken extreme measures to diminish their demographics through forced sterilization, deportations for forced labor, and efforts to eradicate their culture by destroying mosques and traditional architecture. Since 2017, it has detained over a million Uyghurs and other minorities in Xinji- ang in camps to undergo Communist indoctrination and torture. It has also forced the mass transfer of nearly 900,000 Uyghur Muslim children from their families into government-approved boarding schools and orphanages as part of Beijing’s ruthless policy to destroy their identity and annihilate them as a distinct community.
<P>	中共透過強制同化計劃，將突厥語族群列為滅絕目標，時任國務卿蓬佩奧於2021年之聲明中如此認定，繼任國務卿布林肯亦於後續政府中予以支持。中共政權採取極端措施，透過強制絕育、驅逐進行強迫勞動，以及破壞清真寺與傳統建築以消滅其文化，來削減其人口結構。自2017年以來，新疆已拘留超過百萬維吾爾族及其他少數民族於營區，進行共產主義思想灌輸與酷刑。作為北京摧毀其身份認同、消滅其作為獨特社群之殘酷政策一部分，中共亦已強制將近九十萬維吾爾穆斯林兒童與家庭分離，送往政府核准之寄宿學校與孤兒院。
<P>	For multiple decades beginning in the 1990s, Xinjiang has seen periodic violence connected with independence movements and Islamist extremism. Beijing blamed the militant Uyghur separatist group, the East Turkestan Islamic Movement, though in 2020 the US government stated there was no credible evidence that they had been operating over the prior decade and removed them from its terror list.233 The CCP crushed all civic protests against forced assimilation in Xinjiang.
<P>	自1990年代起，數十年來，新疆經歷了與獨立運動及伊斯蘭極端主義相關之週期性暴力。北京將此歸咎於激進維吾爾分裂組織「東突厥斯坦伊斯蘭運動」，然美國政府於2020年聲明，並無可信證據顯示該組織在先前十年有所活動，並將其從恐怖組織名單中移除。中共鎮壓了新疆境內所有反對強制同化之公民抗議。
<P>	The CCP forced the region’s incorporation into China. A short-lived East Turkestan Republic ended with a takeover by the PLA in October 1949. Signifying its tenuous link, China named the region Xinjiang, meaning “New Frontier,” a name the Qing dynasty had first used after its conquest in the late nineteenth century.
<P>	中共強行將該區域併入中國。一個短暫存續之「東突厥斯坦共和國」於1949年10月隨解放軍接管而終結。為象徵其鬆散聯繫，中國將該區域命名為「新疆」，意為「新邊疆」，此名稱源於清朝於19世紀末征服後之首次使用。
<P>	Due to the CCP’s harsh treatment, the Uyghurs and other Turkic people in Xinjiang have gained sympathy and support in the United States and other Western countries. This should translate into support for their independence in the wake of a CCP regime collapse. Beijing, however, views control of Xinjiang as a strategic imperative for its Belt and Road Initiative and has justified its oppression of the Uyghurs mainly as a counterterrorism measure. Whether a post-CCP Chinese government will continue to hold this position and oppose any declaration of Xinjiang independence remains to be seen.
<P>	由於中共之殘酷對待，新疆之維吾爾族及其他突厥人民已贏得美國及其他西方國家之同情與支持。在中共政權垮台後，此應轉化為對其獨立之支持。然而，北京將新疆之控制視為其「一帶一路」倡議之戰略必要條件，並主要以反恐措施合理化其對維吾爾人之壓迫。後中共時代之中國政府是否將繼續持此立場並反對新疆獨立之任何宣示，仍有待觀察。
<P>	Hudson Institute scholar Nury Turkel writes in his book about the Uyghur genocide, No Escape, “when the Chinese people learn what is being done in their name to millions of innocent peo- ple, will they still silently watch their video clips on TikTok and refuse to speak up? It is unlikely.”234 A hint that Turkel’s answer may be right came with the spontaneous mass protests against Xi’s zero-COVID policy on the streets of dozens of Chinese mega-cities and scores of university campuses in late 2022. Those protests were a reaction to news reports that 10 Uyghur family members burned to death in a strict COVID lockdown in a Uyghur neighborhood in Xinjiang’s capital of Urumqi. That many ordinary Chinese people risked their freedom to light candles and show support for the Uyghur victims indicates a remarkable degree of empathy for a community that CCP propaganda has indoctrinated the Chinese people to hate.
<P>	哈德遜研究所學者努爾·圖爾克爾在其關於維吾爾種族滅絕之著作《無處可逃》（No Escape）中寫道：「當中國人民得知，以其名義對數百萬無辜人民所做之惡行時，他們是否仍會默默觀看TikTok上的視頻，拒絕發聲？可能性不大。」圖爾克爾之判斷可能正確之一絲跡象，來自2022年末爆發於數十個中國特大城市街頭及眾多大學校園、針對習近平「清零」政策之自發性大規模抗議。該等抗議是對新疆首府烏魯木齊一維吾爾族社區嚴格封鎖期間，十名維吾爾家庭成員因火災死亡新聞報導之回應。眾多普通中國民眾冒著自由風險，點燃蠟燭為維吾爾受害者表示支持，顯示了對一個中共宣傳鼓動中國人民去仇恨之社群，有著非凡程度之同情。
<P>	While Xinjiang would have numerous reasons for optimism as a free nation, it will have to overcome formidable challenges. These include its lack of democratic experience; ethnic divisions; resentment against its large Han minority, some of whom the CCP specifically sent there to oppress them; and a serious threat of Islamist terror and extremism spreading from neighboring Afghanistan and Pakistan. It will need strong assistance from the United States and its allies in these areas.
<P>	儘管新疆作為一個自由國家將有眾多樂觀理由，但仍需克服嚴峻挑戰。這些挑戰包括其缺乏民主經驗；族裔分裂；對其龐大漢族少數群體之怨恨（其中部分為中共特意派駐以壓迫之）；以及來自鄰國阿富汗與巴基斯坦蔓延之伊斯蘭恐怖與極端主義之嚴重威脅。在這些方面，新疆將需要美國及其盟友提供強有力之援助。
<P>	The diaspora organizations from Xinjiang in the United States and other Western nations will be a valuable resource. They and others can support referendums, elections, and democratic nation building. American policymakers should refrain from sending in as unelected rulers those who claim to be the “government in exile,” as the US government once attempted to do in Iraq. To alleviate resentment and help avert retribution, human rights reforms should receive priority attention. Such measures are discussed later in this chapter. The United States should expect Xinjiang to declare sovereign independence, and if this happens it should be prepared to support it, consistent with the US guidelines.
<P>	來自美國及其他西方國家新疆僑民組織將是寶貴資源。渠等及其他團體可支持公投、選舉及民主國家建設。美國決策者應避免如美國政府曾試圖在伊拉克所為，派遣自稱「流亡政府」者作為未經選舉產生之統治者。為緩解怨恨並協助避免報復，人權改革應獲優先關注。此類措施將於本章後文詳述。美國應預期新疆宣布主權獨立，若此事發生，應準備好支持之，符合美國指導方針。

  ### Tibet
<P>	The Autonomous Province of Tibet is home to the ethnic Tibetan Buddhist community. Ethnic Tibetan communities also live in Tibetan autonomous prefectures and counties in Sichuan, Qinghai, Yunnan, and Gansu Provinces. Small, defenseless, relatively poor, and severely oppressed, Tibet will face two choices with the collapse of the CCP regime: to pursue independent sovereignty or to remain as part of China with greater autonomy.
<P>	西藏自治區是藏傳佛教社群的家園。藏族社群亦居住於四川、青海、雲南及甘肅省之藏族自治州與縣。西藏人口稀少、無力自衛、相對貧困且遭受嚴重壓迫，在中共政權崩潰後將面臨兩個抉擇：追求獨立主權，或在更大自治權下繼續作為中國一部分。
<P>	Most of Tibet’s population of about 3.6 million people no longer speak Tibetan, and many of them are Han Chinese transplanted there under the CCP’s forced assimilation policy. The party is continuously introducing new, extreme measures in pursuit of the policy. In 2023, several United Nations experts felt compelled to call on China to stop “what appears to be a policy of forced assimilation” of Tibetans into China’s majority Han culture, citing reports that authorities had removed as many as 1 million Tibetan children from their families and placed them in state-run boarding schools to indoctrinate them against their religious and ethnic Tibetan identity. It is precisely these and other repressive policies that some Tibetan groups in exile say make imperative Tibetan self-determination and a “homeland, free from China’s occupation”—one they hope would include the autonomous prefects of ethnic Tibetans.
<P>	西藏約三百六十萬人口中，多數已不再說藏語，其中許多人是被中共強制同化政策移植至此的漢族人。該黨正持續推行新穎、極端之措施以推動此政策。2023年，數名聯合國專家被迫呼籲中國停止「顯然是強制同化藏人融入中國主流漢族文化之政策」，引用報導指當局已將多達一百萬藏族兒童從其家庭中帶走，安置於國家經營之寄宿學校，以灌輸其反對宗教與民族藏族身份認同之思想。正是這些及其他壓迫性政策，讓部分流亡藏族團體認為，藏人自決及建立一個「免於中國佔領之故土」——彼等希望此故土能包含藏族自治州——勢在必行。
<P>	The Dalai Lama has been an important voice of peace, stability, and democracy for Tibetans, and his role will be critical to the success of the region as it goes forward. After fleeing a failed uprising of the local Tibet army against China in 1959, the Dalai Lama currently lives in exile in Dharamshala, India, along with nearly 150,000 Tibetan Buddhists in exile across India and Nepal. Beijing considers him a dangerous “separatist” who aims to sever Tibet from China. The CCP treats him as an enemy, pressuring Tibetans in government positions to criticize him and pressuring both locals and foreigners, including even American universities, to shun him.
<P>	達賴喇嘛一直是西藏人民和平、穩定與民主之重要代言人，其角色對該區域未來之成功至關重要。1959年當地藏軍反抗中國之起義失敗後，達賴喇嘛與近十五萬流亡於印度及尼泊爾之藏傳佛教徒，現居於印度達蘭薩拉。北京視其為危險之「分裂份子」，意圖將西藏從中國分裂出去。中共將其視為敵人，施壓政府職位上之藏人批評渠，並迫使當地人及外國人，甚至美國大學，迴避渠。
<P>	However, the Nobel Peace Prize laureate publicly declares that he rejects independence for Tibet and instead seeks meaningful autonomy, espousing a “middle way” policy. For example, he unequivocally stated publicly in 2010 that Tibetans “are not seeking independence.” He further explained, “That’s why we [are called] middle way. We complain [about] the presence of policy in Tibet. It is actually very much damaging. . . . But [onthe] other hand, we also do not want separation from China because . . . Tibet [is a] landlocked country, materially backward. Every Tibetan want modernized Tibet, so for that reason, [we] remain within the People’s Republic of China.”
<P>	然此諾貝爾和平獎得主公開宣之，渠拒絕西藏獨立，轉而尋求實質自治，倡導「中間道路」政策。例如，渠於2010年公開明確表示，藏人「不尋求獨立」。渠進一步解釋：「這就是為何我們[被稱為]中間道路。我們抱怨[在]西藏的政策。這實際上損害甚鉅……。但[另一方面]，我們也不希望與中國分離，因為……西藏[是]一個內陸國家，物質落後。每個藏人都希望現代化西藏，因此，[我們]留在中華人民共和國境內。」
<P>	Whether the nonagenarian or his successor would continue tohold this view in the absence of the CCP remains to be seen.The Dalai Lama would likely have significant influence on the future status of Tibet, though its entire population, including the exile community, should have the opportunity to democratically determine this. As well as being the Tibetan Buddhist religious authority, the current Fourteenth Dalai Lama followed the group’s custom of assuming temporal leadership of his community until 2011, when he retired from his political office and ended the Tibetan Buddhist practice of merging the two roles. As the Dalai Lama’s website declares, he established democratic governance for the Tibetan government in exile: “On 29 May 2011 His Holiness signed the document formally transferring his temporal authority to the democratically elected leader. In so doing he formally put an end to the 368-year-old tradition of the Dalai Lamas functioning as both the spiritual and temporal head of Tibet.”
<P>	至於這位九旬長者或其繼任者，在中共缺席後是否將繼續持此觀點，仍有待觀察。達賴喇嘛很可能對西藏未來地位具有重大影響，儘管其全體人民，包括流亡社群，應有機會民主決定此一事項。現任第十四世達賴喇嘛，除了是藏傳佛教之精神權威外，亦遵循其社群之傳統，直至2011年擔任其政治職務並終止達賴喇嘛兼任精神與世俗領袖之傳統，才卸下世俗領導權。達賴喇嘛網站聲明，渠為流亡藏人政府建立了民主治理：「2011年5月29日，上師簽署正式將其世俗權力移交予民選領袖之文件。藉此，渠正式終結了達賴喇嘛兼任西藏精神與世俗領袖長達368年之傳統。」
<P>	A last, important consideration for Tibet’s prospects as an independent democratic nation is its borders. This dispute with China will require skillful American diplomacy to resolve—di- plomacy even more complex than in the German reunification. Diplomats will also need to resolve whether and how to include the autonomous Tibetan prefectures and counties within four of China’s provinces. China’s ongoing conflict on Tibet’s border with India, however, presents a significantly more difficult diplomatic challenge. Despite a recent agreement between China and India to ease border tensions, they intensified days later, in August 2023, when China issued the new edition of its standardized map showing Aksai Chin and the northeastern Indian state of Arunachal Pradesh (which China calls southern Tibet) within Chinese territory. To ensure Tibet’s stability, its border issues will require resolution.
<P>	西藏作為獨立民主國家之前景，最後一個重要考量是其邊界。此與中國之爭議將需要美國嫻熟之外交手段方能解決——此等外交難度甚至超過德國統一。外交官亦需解決是否以及如何將中國四省藏族自治州縣納入其中。然而，中國與印度之間持續之邊界衝突，構成更為艱鉅之外交挑戰。儘管中印近期就緩解邊境緊張達成協議，然於2023年8月，中國發布標準化地圖新版，將阿克賽欽及印度東北邦阿魯納恰爾邦（中國稱之為藏南）劃入中國領土，令緊張局勢數日後加劇。為確保西藏穩定，其邊界問題必須得到解決。
<P>	The case of declaring sovereignty in South Sudan, which in 2011 became the world’s newest country with a population of 10 million, offers a cautionary tale. Like Tibet, its borders were unresolved, and it suffered from internal ethnic tensions. But South Sudan went ahead with independence after enduring genocidal levels of ethno-religious repression. It gained sovereignty according to an internationally backed road map, based on the 2005 comprehensive peace agreement on Sudan that ended its two-decade war with its aggressor, Khartoum. Successfully conducting a referendum process with American and international assistance, it decisively determined its population’s desire for independence from Sudan, which in turn accepted this process.
<P>	南蘇丹於2011年宣布主權，成為當時世界最新國家，人口達一千萬，其案例可謂警世之言。如同西藏，其邊界未定，且飽受內部族裔緊張關係之苦。但南蘇丹在承受了種族滅絕等級之族裔宗教壓迫後，仍前進獨立。渠等根據2005年結束其與侵略者喀土穆長達二十年戰爭之蘇丹全面和平協議，循一項國際支持之路線圖，獲得主權。在美國及國際協助下成功舉辦了公民投票，明確決定其人口脫離蘇丹之意願，蘇丹亦接受此進程。
<P>	These positive outcomes resulted largely from the pressure and deep engagement of the United States government, which had bipartisan support in Congress, and efforts by the African Union and a committed core group of African and European states.  The alliance of “friends” of Sudan supported the referendum, ascertained balloting integrity, enabled balloting for its large refugee population around the world, and negotiated Khartoum’s acceptance of the result.
<P>	此等正面成果，主要歸因於美國政府之壓力與深入參與（此獲得國會兩黨支持），以及非洲聯盟與一群堅定之非洲及歐洲國家之努力。「蘇丹之友」聯盟支持了公民投票，確認了投票之公正性，使其遍布全球之龐大難民人口得以投票，並協商喀土穆接受了結果。
<P>	Despite the unresolved issues, American leaders across the political spectrum celebrated South Sudan’s declaration of independence in 2011. President Barack Obama asserted, “We know that southern Sudanese have claimed their sovereignty, and shown that neither their dignity nor their dream of self-determination can be denied.” Yet, within two years, South Sudan devolved into an ethnic civil war (distinct from the Islamist extremism of the original north-south conflict) and has suffered from Sudan’s violent aggression over an unresolved border dispute. Millions of South Sudanese citizens have fled the fledgling nation, escaping hunger and violence. A decade later, the United States remained South Sudan’s largest single donor of aid, as 60 percent of the devastated country faced food insecurity and many world leaders view it as a failed state.
<P>	儘管存在未決問題，美國各政治光譜之領導人均慶祝了南蘇丹於2011年之獨立宣言。總統貝拉克·奧巴馬斷言：「我們知道南蘇丹人民已宣稱其主權，並表明其尊嚴或自決之夢想均不可被剝奪。」然而，不到兩年，南蘇丹即陷入族裔內戰（不同於南北衝突初期之伊斯蘭極端主義），並因未解決之邊界爭端而遭受蘇丹之暴力侵略。數百萬南蘇丹公民逃離這個新生的國家，以躲避飢餓與暴力。十年後，美國仍是南蘇丹最大之單一援助捐助國，該飽受摧殘國家之60%面臨糧食不安全，許多世界領導人視之為一個失敗國家。
<P>	Border disputes, particularly regarding the China-India border, pose a serious impediment to Tibetan independence. With pragmatic urgency, the United States and its allies should work to facilitate the resolution of these disputes before Tibet pursues sovereignty. They should be prepared to expend even greater diplomatic effort than German reunification required.
<P>	邊界爭端，特別是關於中印邊界，對西藏獨立構成嚴重障礙。美國及其盟友應以務實之緊迫感，在西藏尋求主權之前，致力於促成這些爭端之解決。渠等應準備付出比德國統一所需更甚之外交努力。
<P>	If there is no resolution to the Tibetan border problem, the United States should weigh measures to reinforce Tibet’s autonomy instead of supporting independence. The Tibetan Policy and Support Act, which President Trump signed into US law in 2020, lays some groundwork that the United States can build on. It establishes as official US policy the right of the Tibetan Buddhist community to select and venerate its own religious leaders, including the Dalai Lama, and specifies sanctions for Chinese officials who interfere in the selection of his successor. This is critical in light of China’s ongoing measures to select the religion’s spiritual leaders, such as in the disappearance of the six-year-old Panchen Lama in 1995, three days after his recognition by the Dalai Lama. In addition, the act requires the secretary of state to seek to establish a consulate in Lhasa, which he should do as soon as events allow by appointing a consul general and a full staff.
<P>	若西藏邊界問題無法解決，美國應權衡措施，加強西藏自治，而非支持獨立。川普總統於2020年簽署成為美國法律之《西藏政策與支持法案》，為美國可據以發展之基礎奠定了一些工作。該法案將藏傳佛教社群選擇與尊奉其宗教領袖（包括達賴喇嘛）之權利，確立為美國官方政策，並具體規定對干預其繼承人選拔之中國官員施加制裁。鑑於中國持續採取選拔該宗教精神領袖之措施，如1995年達賴喇嘛確認班禪喇嘛後三天，年僅六歲之班禪喇嘛即告失蹤，此法案至關重要。此外，該法案要求國務卿尋求在拉薩設立領事館，渠應於情況允許時，任命總領事及完整班底，儘速執行此項任務。
<P>	Regarding the risk of ethnic strife and efforts to avert retribution within Tibet, the region has a valuable asset in the Dalai Lama, a spiritual leader whom people respect and recognize worldwide for his advocacy and actions to promote peaceful coexistence. Additional priority human rights reforms to alleviate these tensions are proposed at the end of this section.
<P>	關於族裔衝突之風險及為避免西藏境內報復之努力，該區域擁有達賴喇嘛這一寶貴資產。渠作為一位受全世界尊敬與認可之精神領袖，以其倡導與行動促進和平共存。本節末尾提出了額外優先之人權改革措施，以緩解此等緊張關係。
 
  ### Inner Mongolia
<P>	The Autonomous Region of Inner Mongolia will face three choices: gaining independence, remaining as an autonomous part of China, or uniting with the neighboring Republic of Mongolia.Though unification may at first seem reasonable, a closer look indicates it is not likely, and the United States should support its sovereign independence instead.
<P>	內蒙古自治區將面臨三種選擇：獲得獨立、作為中國之一自治部分繼續存在，或與鄰國蒙古共和國合併。儘管合併乍看之下或顯合理，然仔細審視則表明其可能性不大，美國應轉而支持其主權獨立。
<P>	Inner Mongolia borders the Republic of Mongolia, which is one of the world’s most sparsely populated countries with just 3.3 million people and ranks among the free nations of the world. Once part of the Mongol Empire of Genghis Khan, Mongols on both sides of the border share a common language and continue contact with cross-border trade and travel. Some 95 percent of the residents of the Republic of Mongolia are ethnically Mongol, and many of them, like many within Inner Mongolia’s ethnic Mongolian community, are Buddhist. Tibetan Buddhism arrived there in the sixteenth century, and four centuries later, reportedly a third of the Republic of Mongolia’s adult males were Buddhist monks. In 2016, the Dalai Lama discovered a boy there whom he considers the reincarnation of a high spiritual leader.
<P>	內蒙古與蒙古共和國接壤，後者為全球人口最稀疏之國家之一，僅有三百三十萬人口，並位列世界自由國家之列。蒙古人曾為成吉思汗蒙古帝國之一部分，邊界兩側之蒙古人共享共同語言，並透過跨境貿易與旅行持續聯繫。蒙古共和國約95%之居民為蒙古族，其中許多人，如同內蒙古境內眾多蒙古族社群成員，均為佛教徒。藏傳佛教於16世紀傳入，四世紀後，據報蒙古共和國成年男性中，三分之一為佛教僧侶。2016年，達賴喇嘛於當地發現一男孩，渠認為其為一位高級精神領袖之轉世。
<P>	Inner Mongolia, though, has been the target of accelerating forced assimilation measures in recent years, by which the CCP aims to abolish the Mongolian language in schools and in media. In 2020, large crowds protested these measures in several cities, and parents extracted their children from schools, stating that this was an attempt to erase their identity. “Our language is Mongolian, and our homeland is Mongolia forever! Our mother tongue is Mongolian, and we will die for our mother tongue!” students shouted during a protest. Defying Chinese government threats against those criticizing the new language policy, some Inner Mongolians sent videos and messages of their protests to the Republic of Mongolia, where others reposted them on Facebook and Twitter.
<P>	然而，近年來內蒙古已成為加速強制同化措施之目標，中共藉此旨在廢除學校與媒體中之蒙古語。2020年，大批民眾於數座城市抗議這些措施，家長將其子女從學校撤出，聲稱此為抹殺其身份認同之企圖。「我們的語言是蒙古語，我們的家園永遠是蒙古！我們的母語是蒙古語，我們將為母語而死！」學生於一次抗議中高喊。儘管中國政府對批評新語言政策者構成威脅，部分內蒙古人仍將其抗議視頻與訊息發送至蒙古共和國，並由他人轉發至Facebook與Twitter。
<P>	In addition, demographic manipulation by the CCP has had a large impact on Inner Mongolia. Any proposal to unify Inner Mongolia and the Republic of Mongolia is likely to be unacceptable to Ulaanbaatar, whose people would become an ethnic minority in their own country overnight in such an arrangement. The Chinese-speaking Han ethnic population now constitutes the overwhelming majority within Inner Mongolia’s population of 24 million, vastly outnumbering its 5 million ethnic Mongolians and the republic’s entire population of 3.3 million. The Republic of Mongolia may also fear that the sudden influx of a much larger population with no tradition of democracy would jeopardize its form of government. German reunification, by contrast, entailed the absorption of a proportionately smaller population of East Germans into West German democracy, and both were largely German ethnics.
<P>	此外，中共之人口結構操弄亦對內蒙古產生重大影響。任何關於統一內蒙古與蒙古共和國之提議，很可能為烏蘭巴托所無法接受，因在此安排下，渠等人民將一夜之間成為其本國之少數族裔。現今，漢語人口已構成內蒙古兩千四百萬人口中之絕大多數，遠超其五百萬蒙古族人口及蒙古共和國總人口三百三十萬。蒙古共和國亦可能擔憂，一個缺乏民主傳統之龐大人口突然湧入，將危及其政體。相比之下，德國統一涉及將相對較少之東德人口吸收到西德民主體制中，且雙方皆主要為德意志族裔。
<P>	That said, the Republic of Mongolia could consider some accommodation of citizenship for the Mongolians of Inner Mongolia and even for Tibetan Buddhists in Tibet, along the lines of the birthright citizenship policies of Israel for Jews and of Armenia for ethnic Armenians. Controlled immigration could benefit the republic and offer a steam valve for tensions within the native minority communities of both Inner Mongolia and Tibet. This is an idea that the United States should explore.
<P>	話雖如此，蒙古共和國可考慮對內蒙古蒙古人，乃至西藏之藏傳佛教徒，給予一定程度之公民權待遇，類似以色列對猶太人及亞美尼亞對亞美尼亞族裔實施之出生公民權政策。受控移民可惠及該共和國，並為內蒙古及西藏本土少數族群內部之緊張關係提供緩衝。此一構想，美國應予探討。
<P>	The United States should expect Inner Mongolia to declare sovereign independence, and if this happens, it should be prepared to support it, consistent with the US guidelines.
<P>	美國應預期內蒙古宣布主權獨立，若此事發生，應準備好支持之，符合美國指導方針。

  ### Guangxi/广西
<P>	Guangxi’s formal name is the Zhuang Autonomous Region of Guangxi. Its largest ethnic minority, constituting up to a third of its population of over 50 million, is the Zhuang, who have lived there for some 2,500 years, speak their own language and dialects, and practice local folk religions. For millennia, Guangxi’s Zhuang people have lived alongside the Han, who now comprise nearly two-thirds of the region’s population. Populations in its eastern and southern parts speak Cantonese together with the Zhuang language. In recent decades, Guangxi has developed light industry, forestry, and mining while continuing traditional fishing and agriculture. According to scholars, the Han population has largely assimilated the Zhuang: “Because the minorities in Guangxi possess neither a unified organization nor support by fraternal groups, their assimilation by the Chinese is far more advanced than in the other autonomous regions.”
<P>	廣西之正式名稱為廣西壯族自治區。其最大少數族裔為壯族，佔其超過五千萬人口之三分之一，渠等在此居住了約2500年，說自有語言及方言，並信奉當地民間宗教。數千年來，廣西壯族人民與漢族共同生活，漢族現佔該區域人口近三分之二。其東部與南部地區人口，除壯語外，亦說粵語。近數十年來，廣西發展了輕工業、林業及礦業，同時延續傳統漁業與農業。學者指出，漢族人口已大規模同化壯族：「由於廣西之少數民族既無統一組織，亦無兄弟群體之支持，渠等被漢人同化之程度遠超其他自治區。」
<P>	It is impossible to know the Guangxi population’s views on separating from China without a free and fair referendum. Policymakers should remember that even the most Stalinist countries of the Soviet empire at the time of its breakup chose sovereignty when they had the chance. The United States should expect Guangxi to declare sovereign independence, and if this happens, it should be prepared to support it, consistent with the US guidelines.
<P>	在自由公正之公民投票前，無法得知廣西人口對脫離中國之看法。決策者應銘記，即使在蘇聯解體之際，東歐集團中最斯大林主義之國家，亦在其有機會時選擇了主權。美國應預期廣西宣布主權獨立，若此事發生，應準備好支持之，符合美國指導方針。

  ### Ningxia/宁夏
<P>	China formed the Ningxia Hui Autonomous Region for the ethnic Hui people in 1958. The Hui are mostly Muslims who constitute 38 percent of Ningxia’s population of 7 million, while the Han are the other 62 percent. The Hui have assimilated into the Chinese culture and speak Mandarin, and 10.5 million of them are spread over several other provinces. Recent religious repression against them has resulted in an assertion of their religious identity and may spur Hui interest in independence from China.
<P>	中國於1958年為回族建立寧夏回族自治區。回族多為穆斯林，佔寧夏七百萬人口中之百分之三十八，其餘百分之六十二則為漢族。回族已融入中華文化並使用漢語，另有一千零五十萬回族散居於其他數省。近期針對其施加之宗教壓迫，已引發回族對其宗教身份之捍衛，並可能激發其脫離中國、追求獨立之意願。
<P>	Since 2018, the Hui Muslims have engaged in rare protests against the demolition of their mosques’ domes, minarets, and displays of Arabic script under the CCP’s Sinicization policy. In 2018, the international press reported that hundreds of Hui Muslims staged a sit-in to block the destruction of their newly constructed Grand Mosque in Weizhou, for which they had permits. In 2019, an NPR investigation found that the “same restrictions that preceded the Xinjiang crackdown on Uyghur Muslims are now appearing in Hui-dominated regions.” NPR learned that since 2018, “Hui mosques have been forcibly renovated or shuttered, schools demolished, and religious community leaders imprisoned. Hui who have traveled internationally are increasingly detained or sent to reeducation facilities in Xinjiang.” The magazine Bitter Winter reported that on May 27, 2023, thousands of protesting Hui Muslims gathered and clashed with hundreds of riot police at Najiaying Mosque in Yunnan. Reinforcement police and military came the next day to complete the job; they arrested some of the protesters and shut down the area’s internet.
<P>	自2018年起，回族穆斯林因抗議中共「宗教中國化」政策下，拆除清真寺圓頂、宣禮塔及阿拉伯文字裝飾之舉，展開罕見之抗議活動。2018年，國際媒體報導數百名回族穆斯林於威州舉行靜坐，以阻止其已獲許可之新建大清真寺遭到毀壞。2019年，美國國家公共廣播電台（NPR）調查發現，「先前新疆對維吾爾穆斯林進行鎮壓前之限制措施，現正出現在回族主導之地區」。NPR得知，自2018年以來，「回族清真寺遭強制翻修或關閉，學校被拆除，宗教社群領袖亦遭囚禁。曾有國際旅行經歷之回族，亦日益面臨遭拘留或送往新疆再教育設施之命運」。據《苦冬》（Bitter Winter）雜誌報導，2023年5月27日，數千名抗議之回族穆斯林於雲南那甲營清真寺集結，並與數百名防暴警察發生衝突。次日，增援之警力與軍隊抵達以完成任務；渠等逮捕了部分抗議者，並切斷該地區網路。
<P>	Hui Muslims have indicated in interviews with the international media that they are aware of the massive repression against the Uyghurs and Tibetan Buddhists and expressed their fears that the CCP could also target them. They are among the growing number of communities in China who have lost trust in Beijing due to its denial of fundamental freedoms.
<P>	回族穆斯林於接受國際媒體採訪時表示，渠等深知中共對維吾爾人及藏傳佛教徒之大規模壓迫，並表達對中共亦將其列為打壓目標之恐懼。隨著北京拒絕保障基本自由，渠等已成為中國境內對政府失去信任之社群中，日益壯大的一員。
<P>	The United States cannot know whether Hui Muslims support independence until they have the opportunity to determine their political future in a free and fair referendum. They are a minority in Ningxia, but a vote for independence could prevail there as a vote in 1991 for Ukrainian independence once did in Crimea, then a Russian ethnic majority region in Ukraine. The United States should expect Ningxia to declare sovereign independence and be prepared to evaluate, in particular, whether it could be a viable independent republic were it to be landlocked within a newly reconfigured greater China, as well as the other US guidelines.
<P>	在缺乏自由公正之公民投票前，美國無法得知回族穆斯林是否支持獨立。儘管其於寧夏僅屬少數族裔，然獨立之投票結果仍可能勝出，正如1991年烏克蘭獨立投票時，當時屬俄羅斯族裔多數之克里米亞亦曾投下贊成票。美國應預期寧夏宣布主權獨立，並準備好依據美國指導方針進行評估，特別是探討若寧夏被困於重新劃分後之「大中國」內陸腹地，其作為獨立共和國之可行性。

  ### Guangdong and Fujian/广东&福建
<P>	Guangdong Province borders both Hong Kong and Macau and, like Hong Kong, speaks Cantonese and is an economic powerhouse. Some commentators suggest it should break away from China and join Hong Kong in a single sovereign republic or opt for independence on its own. Both suggestions would be ill-advised. Guangdong’s people share much with Hong Kong. In fact, Hong Kong’s most prominent champion of free speech and democracy is the aforementioned Jimmy Lai, who escaped from Guangdong at age 12 to seek freedom in Hong Kong. However, Guangdong would overwhelm Hong Kong, given the former’s large population of 127 million and lack of democratic experience. For these reasons, unification would not likely serve Hong Kong’s interests.
<P>	廣東省與香港及澳門接壤，且與香港相似，皆以粵語為主要語言，並為經濟強權。部分評論家建議廣東應脫離中國，與香港合併為單一主權共和國，或自行尋求獨立。然此兩項建議均非明智之舉。廣東人民與香港有諸多共同之處；事實上，香港最著名之言論自由與民主倡導者，即前述之黎智英，渠於12歲時即逃離廣東，赴港尋求自由。然而，鑑於廣東擁有1.27億之龐大人口且缺乏民主經驗，其規模將令香港難以負荷。基於此等理由，兩者合併恐難符合香港之利益。
<P>	If Guangdong or any other province without autonomous status declared independence, it could embolden other areas and provinces to do so as well, fracturing China. For example, Fujian Province, with a population of 42 million and a border on the Taiwan Strait, might also seek unification with the Republic of China (Taiwan, population 24 million) or independent sovereignty.
<P>	若廣東或任何其他無自治地位之省份宣布獨立，恐將鼓舞其他地區與省份效法，進而導致中國分裂。例如，福建省擁有四千二百萬人口且面臨台灣海峽，亦可能尋求與中華民國（台灣，人口二千四百萬）合併，或追求主權獨立。
<P>	In short, the secession of any Chinese province that lacks a legal status of autonomy would risk a violent response by China and set a precedent that could lead other provinces to race for the exits. Such scenarios would be certain to plunge greater Asia into destabilizing political and economic crises. 
<P>	簡言之，任何缺乏法定自治地位之中國省份若進行分裂，皆面臨遭中國暴力回應之風險，並將開創先例，導致其他省份紛紛效仿。此等情勢必將使大亞洲陷入動盪之政治與經濟危機。

  ###  Stabilizing Human Rights Measures稳定人权状况的措施
<P>	Under CCP rule, China has been and continues to be among the world’s harshest regimes. Its human rights abuses are egregious and widespread, and the US government considers its actions against the ethno-religious minorities in Xinjiang genocide. In many cases, these abuses warrant immediate remediation after a CCP collapse, and the United States should prioritize such efforts rather than postpone them until a new government is fully in place. This would both serve justice and mitigate frustration and suffering among the population that, left unaddressed, could lead to violent retribution.
<P>	在中共统治下，中国一直是世界上最严酷的政权之一。其侵犯人权的行为既严重又普遍；美国政府认定其针对新疆少数民族和宗教群体的行径构成种族灭绝。在许多情况下，这些侵权问题需要在中共政权垮台后立即着手解决；美国应将此类工作置于优先地位，而不应等到新政府完全确立后再行处理。这样做既能伸张正义，又能缓解民众的挫败感与苦难——若这些问题得不到解决，可能会引发暴力报复。
<P>	An extensive list of China’s abuses appears in reports by the US State Department on human rights and religious freedom, the US Commission on International Religious Freedom, and the Executive and Congressional Commission on China and in the accounts of a large array of diaspora and international human rights groups. A few examples of immediate action that a new Chinese government should take to stop egregious human rights abuses follow:
<P>	美國國務院人權與宗教自由報告、美國國際宗教自由委員會、行政與國會中國委員會，以及眾多海外僑民與國際人權組織之紀錄，皆詳列了中國濫權之廣泛清單。以下列舉新任中國政府為終止嚴重人權侵害應立即採取之行動範例：
<P>	• Empty and shut all detention centers where people are held without due process or fair trial. Demolish Xinjiang’s large detention complex for Uyghurs and other Turkic Muslims, which has held over a million persons to force religious, ethnic, and political assimilation.
<P>	清空並關閉所有未經正當程序或公正審判即拘留民眾之拘留中心。拆除新疆針對維吾爾人及其他突厥語系穆斯林所設之大型拘留設施；該處曾關押逾百萬人，旨在強迫其進行宗教、族裔與政治同化。
<P>	• Throughout China, free the thousands of political and religious detainees and prisoners, including those in unofficial detention places, or “black jails,” and psychiatric institutes.
<P>	於中國全境釋放數以千計之政治與宗教被拘留者及囚犯，包含被關押於非官方拘留場所（即「黑牢」）及精神科醫療機構之人員。
<P>	• Stop forced sterilizations and forced abortions of Uyghur and Turkic women in Xinjiang and of any other victims of such practices in China.
<P>	停止對新疆維吾爾及突厥女性，以及中國境內任何其他此類實踐受害者，實施強制絕育與強制墮胎。
<P>	• In both Xinjiang and Tibet, release the combined estimate of 2 million children whom the CCP has forced into state boarding schools and reunite them with their families.
<P>	於新疆及西藏地區，釋放估計共計兩百萬名被中共強迫入讀國家寄宿制學校之兒童，並使其與家人團聚。
<P>	• Shut down all forced labor camps adjacent to prisons and detention centers throughout China. Secure the freedom of all Uyghurs and others pressed into forced labor.
<P>	關閉中國全境緊鄰監獄與拘留中心之所有強迫勞動營。確保所有遭強迫勞動之維吾爾人及其他族群之自由。
<P>	• In all hospitals and medical centers in China, suspend organ transplant surgeries until, for each case, independent regulators establish that the source of the organ was a volunteer donor under international definition.
<P>	於中國所有醫院與醫療中心，暫停器官移植手術，直至獨立監管機構能針對每宗案例，確認其器官來源係符合國際定義之志願捐贈者為止。
<P>	• Reopen mosques, churches, temples, and monasteries that have been shut or repurposed.
<P>	重新開放所有遭關閉或變更用途之清真寺、教堂、寺廟及寺院。
<P>	•Allow houses of worship and religious organizations to operate independently from patriotic associations and other state and party organizations.
<P>	容許宗教場所與宗教組織獨立運作，不受愛國協會或其他國家與黨務組織干預。
<P>	•Rescind the ban on Falun Gong and shut down the bureaucratic infrastructure that President Jiang Zemin first created specifically to “eliminate” this spiritual group.
<P>	解斥對法輪功之禁令，並裁撤當年江澤民總書記為「取締」該信仰團體而特設之官僚機構。
<P>	• Rescind bans on the underground Catholic and Protestant Churches, as well as Church of the Almighty God and any other peaceful religious group that can exist now only underground.
<P>	廢除對地下天主教與新教教會，以及全能神教會與任何目前僅能於地下生存之和平宗教團體之禁令。
<P>	•End all programs of forced ethnic and religious assimilation and Sinicization throughout the country, including desecration and destruction of temples, churches, and mosques; interference in the selection of religious leaders; bans on teaching and publishing in minority languages; demographic manipulation; restrictions on access to religious literature; requirements to preach and teach official political ideology; subordination of religious groups to governmental and political oversight on internal religious affairs; and numerous other CCP control measures.
<P>	終止全國範圍內所有強迫族裔與宗教同化及「中國化」計畫，其範圍包括：褻瀆與拆毀寺廟、教堂及清真寺；干預宗教領袖選拔；禁止以少數民族語言進行教學與出版；人口結構操弄；限制接觸宗教文獻；要求宣揚與教授官方政治意識形態；使宗教團體在宗教事務上從屬於政府與政治監督；以及其他眾多中共之控制措施。
<P>	•Allow the return of refugees and exiles.
<P>	容許難民與流亡者返鄉。
<P>	•End CCP spying and influence operations throughout China. These pervasive measures to exert control and intimidation poison human relations by destroying trust and heightening suspicion and anxiety. They are discussed further below.
<P>	終止中共於中國全境之間諜活動與影響力行動。此等旨在施加控制與恐嚇之普遍措施，因破壞信任並加劇猜忌與焦慮，實則毒害了人際關係。此項議題將於下文詳述。
<P>	A priority for consolidating democracy and encouraging social cooperation during the political transition will be to address two areas of CCP oppression that impact China as a whole. The first entails dismantling the party’s extensive system of surveillance of the private sector, and the second requires ending the party’s pervasive ideological indoctrination and domination by promoting free speech and the free flow of information. The CCP has most onerously applies these repressive measures in the autonomous areas, but they affect all in China and are a principal means of coercion throughout the country.
<P>	於政治轉型期間，鞏固民主與促進社會合作之首要任務，應著手處理兩項影響中國整體之中共壓迫領域。其一，係拆解中共對私營部門之廣泛監控體系；其二，則需透過倡導言論自由與資訊自由流動，終止中共無孔不入之意識形態灌輸與統治。儘管中共於自治區內對此類壓迫措施之執行最為嚴苛，然此等手段影響中國全境之所有人民，亦係中共於全國境內行使脅迫之主要手段。
<P>	Millions of people in China object to CCP policies and practices apart from the ethnic minority populations. Two Chinese dissidents, Lu Yuyu and Li Tingyu, documented some 70,000 collective protests in China between 2013 and 2016, until their arrests and eventual flight from the country. Most of those protests involved labor and economic grievances and were local in scale. They show the importance of stabilizing the economic sector, an issue addressed in chapter 3.
<P>	少數民族人口外，中國亦有數百萬民眾反對中共之政策與實踐。兩位中國異議人士陸昱昱與李婷雨，於被捕並最終流亡海外前，記錄了2013年至2016年間中國境內約七萬起集體抗議活動。該等抗議多涉及勞工與經濟訴求，且多屬地方規模。此現象顯示穩定經濟部門之重要性，相關議題將於第三章論述。
<P>	In addition to large political protests against CCP encroachment in Hong Kong in 2019, other such protests indicating the mounting frustrations of mainland Chinese against Xi Jinping and the CCP occurred against the zero-COVID policy. They represented the largest street demonstrations in China since the Tiananmen Square protests of 1989. In October 2022, police dragged away and disappeared into the penal system Peng Lifa, known as the bridge man, after he unfurled banners on a Beijing overpass that declared “Be citizens, not slaves,” “We want dignity not lies,” and other freedom slogans. He undoubtedly spoke for many and in 2023 was among Time’s most 100 influential people in the world. The massive street vigils and White Paper Protests that brought down the zero-COVID policy a month later drew inspiration from him. Afterward, several protests repeating Peng’s slogans briefly popped up, and early in 2023, Chinese seniors protested in Wuhan and other cities about cuts in health benefits. Freedom House documented 937 protests in the third quarter of 2024, an increase over the same period of the previous year. And in the first quarter of 2025, various news outlets reported a violent protest against police over an alleged cover up of the circumstances of a boy’s death in Shaanxi province.
<P>	除2019年針對中共侵蝕香港權益之大規模政治抗議外，針對「清零」政策之抗議，亦反映出中國大陸民眾對習近平與中共日益增長之挫折感。該等抗議係自1989年天安門事件以來，中國規模最大之街頭示威。2022年10月，一名被稱為「橋頭男」的彭立發於北京天橋展開橫幅，寫有「要做公民，不做奴隸」、「我們要尊嚴，不要謊言」及其他自由口號；隨後遭警方拖走並消失於監獄系統。渠無疑代表了眾人之聲，並於2023年入選《時代》雜誌全球百大影響力人物。一個月後，促使「清零」政策崩解之大規模街頭守夜與「白紙抗議」，皆受其啟發。此後，數場重複彭立發口號之抗議曾短暫出現；2023年初，中國長者亦於武漢及其他城市，就醫療福利削減問題展開抗議。根據自由之家（Freedom House）紀錄，2024年第三季共計發生937起抗議活動，較去年同期有所增加。此外，於2025年第一季，多家媒體報導陝西省發生一場針對警方的暴力抗議，起因為警方涉嫌掩蓋一名男孩死亡之真相。
<P>	These protests indicate both deep-seated grievances and continued yearning for rights and freedom among ordinary Chinese people. The CCP has punished and intimidated many of them even more as a result. Some will feel temptation to turn to street justice if they do not perceive prompt positive change after the regime falls.
<P>	此等抗議既顯示出中國平民心中根深蒂固之不滿，亦反映其對權利與自由之持續渴求。中共隨即對其中眾多成員施加更嚴厲之懲罰與恐嚇。若政權更迭後，民眾未能察覺即時且積極之變革，部分人士恐將受誘惑而轉向街頭私刑。
<P>	In demonstrable defiance of CCP ideology, religious groups have seen exponential growth in recent decades. By 1999, there were more Falun Gong practitioners and, by 2018, more Christians—as many as 100 million each—than the 90 million CCP members.263 These examples indicate that, within China’s Han majority population, there is significant dissatisfaction, rejection, and even resentment against the CCP. The US government needs to consider special outreach to them as well.
<P>	宗教團體於近數十年呈指數級增長，顯然是對中共意識形態之公開挑戰。至1999年，法輪功修煉者人數已超越中共黨員；及至2018年，基督徒人數亦達一億人，與中共九千萬名黨員相當。此等實例顯示，在中國漢族多數人口之中，對中共存在著顯著之不滿、排斥，甚至憤恨。美國政府亦需考慮針對此類群體進行特殊之對外聯繫。

  ###  Increasing Information Access/扩大信息获取渠道
<P>	The United States can address such aggrieved groups by providing reliable and accurate information to them, exposing CCP disinformation and human rights atrocities, and reporting real-time events during China’s political transition. It should enable respected voices, such as various Chinese religious leaders, human rights lawyers, and other heroic dissidents, both inside China and abroad, to speak freely through social and other media to reassure the population, restore calm, and reach particular communities. US broadcasting on Radio Free Asia can be brought back for this purpose. Private short-wave broadcasting, such as Falun Gong’s Sounds of Hope radio, is another resource. China’s renowned blind dissident Chen Guangcheng, now in exile in the United States, told me in an email that supporters had smuggled a short-wave radio to him in prison, which was “very helpful” to him.
<P>	美國可透過向上述受壓迫群體提供可靠且準確之資訊、揭露中共之虛假訊息與人權暴行，並於中國政治轉型期間報導即時事件，藉此回應其訴求。美國應賦予各界具公信力之聲音——如中國境內外之宗教領袖、人權律師及其他英雄式異議人士——得以透過社群媒體或其他媒介自由發言之權利，以安撫民心、恢復社會秩序，並觸及特定社群。為此目的，可恢復「自由亞洲電台」（Radio Free Asia）之廣播功能。私人短波廣播亦為另一資源，例如法輪功之「希望之聲」電台。現流亡美國之中國著名盲人異議人士陳光誠曾於電子郵件中告知，支持者曾將短波收音機走私至其獄中，對其「極具幫助」。
<P>	A new Chinese government needs to dismantle the so-called Great Firewall of China, which the CCP uses to censor and block internet access, and the US government should recruit cyber experts to make that happen as soon as possible. Virtual private networks (VPNs) can circumvent the wall, as the world saw during the White Paper Protests, and the United States should make them widely available. It should also make apps such as X (formerly Twitter), WhatsApp, Facebook, and Instagram freely available; reports in early 2025 state that, after or dered by the Communist authorities, Apple removed WhatsApp and Threads (an X competitor) from the App Store in China. Falun Gong has Ultra Surf and other technology that can reach Chinese audiences on a limited basis, and the United States should work to scale it up.
<P>	新任中國政府亟需拆解所謂之「防火長城」——中共藉此進行網路審查與封鎖；美國政府應招募網路專家，務求盡速達成此目標。誠如世界於「白紙抗議」期間所見，虛擬私人網路（VPN）可規避封鎖，美國應確保其廣泛可用。此外，美國亦應使 X（原 Twitter）、WhatsApp、Facebook 及 Instagram 等應用程式得以自由使用；據2025年初之報導，在共產當局命令下，蘋果公司已於中國 App Store 移除 WhatsApp 與 Threads（X 之競爭對手）。法輪功擁有无界等技術，雖目前僅能有限度觸及中國受眾，但美國應致力於擴大其規模。
<P>	The United States should also employ social media, in addition to broadcasts, to communicate official US news and commentary. In 2023, Li Ying, a 30-year-old Chinese artist in Italy, was shocked to find so many Chinese users on Twitter, which the CCP has blocked in China, making it accessible only through VPNs. In late 2022, it was Li’s Twitter feed that informed the world of the White Paper Protests in real time and provided information on police locations to the protesters inside China. The Washington Post reported that Li’s Twitter account became “the single most important clearinghouse for information about the protests.” On March 23, 2023, it reported on another individual who successfully sent information into China and throughout the diaspora using TikTok: “Kim Wong, a longtime journalist in Hong Kong who now lives in the Boston area, has attracted a giant online audience with his Mandarin-language videos criticizing the Chinese government—including, he says, a substantial number of people inside China who use software tools to access information the Chinese Communist Party doesn’t want them to see.”
<P>	除廣播外，美國亦應運用社群媒體以傳遞官方新聞與評論。2023年，現居義大利之三十歲中國藝術家李颖驚訝地發現，在遭中共封鎖、僅能透過 VPN 存取之 Twitter 上，竟有如此多中國用戶。2022年底，正是透過李颖之社群動態，世界方能即時得知「白紙抗議」之情勢，並為中國境內之抗議者提供警方位置之資訊。《華盛頓郵報》報導指出，李颖推特帳號已成為「關於該場抗議活動最重要之資訊匯集處」。2023年3月23日，該報亦報導了另一名利用 TikTok 成功將資訊傳入中國及海外僑民社群之個人：「現居波士頓地區之資深香港記者 Kim Wong，透過其批評中國政府之漢語影片，吸引了龐大之網路受眾——據其稱，其中包含大量身處中國境內、利用軟體工具獲取中共不願其見之資訊之民眾。」
 
  ###  Reforming Education/教育改革
<P>	The United States should work quickly to help a new Chinese government reform education as well. The CCP has renewed efforts to Sinicize the education system, starting in first grade. In 2022, the party placed cells within schools on the mainland even though the party had controlled them for seven decades. The cells will cultivate “love for the party,” run the schools, be “strong fighting fortresses” of CCP ideology, and “deeply integrate the . . . inheritance of red genes into school education.” Meanwhile, Chinese textbooks indoctrinate students in Xi’s thought. For example, a textbook passage states, “Xi Jinping has emphasized that our state’s ideology and social system are fundamentally incompatible with the West. Xi has said ‘This determines that our struggle and contest with Western countries is irreconcilable, so it will inevitably be long, complicated, and sometimes even very sharp.’ ” 
<P>	美國應迅速採取行動，協助新任中國政府推動教育改革。中共正重新致力於將教育體系「中國化」，且此舉已延伸至小學一年級。2022年，儘管中共已對中國大陸學校實施控制長達七十年，其仍於校園內增設黨支部。此類黨支部旨在培養「對黨之熱愛」、掌管學校運作，並作為中共意識形態之「堅強戰鬥堡壘」，力求將「……紅色基因之傳承深度融入學校教育」。與此同時，中國教科書亦持續向學生灌輸習近平思想。例如，某教科書段落載明：「習近平強調，我國之意識形態與社會制度與西方根本不相容。習指出：『這決定了我們與西方國家之鬥爭與競爭是不可調和的，因此必然是長期、複雜，且有時甚至非常尖銳的。』」

  ###  Eliminating Oppressive Surveillance/消除壓迫性監控
<P>	The CCP has also relied on a vast network of technological surveillance and spies to control the Chinese population and influence their thinking. This system, in varying degrees of implementation, cuts across all regions and social groups, and China should promptly dismantle it. The party has blanketed urban areas with cameras that track speech and movement. Under the Sinicization of religion policy, it has placed facial recognition cameras inside houses of worship and monasteries to track who enters. It has also linked these cameras to police stations and social credit score systems. If the Chinese people do not tear them down first, the United States should take the lead in a systematic effort to do so.
<P>	中共亦依賴龐大之技術監控與間諜網絡，以控制中國民眾並操弄其思想。此一體系依實施程度不同，橫跨所有地區與社會群體，中國應立即將其拆解。中共於城市區域佈滿攝影機，藉此追蹤言論與行動；在「宗教中國化」政策下，更於宗教場所及寺院內安裝人臉辨識攝影機，以監控出入人員。此外，該等攝影機亦與警察局及社會信用評分系統連動。若中國人民未先行拆除，美國應帶頭進行系統性之拆解行動。
<P>	The current Chinese government uses WeChat and other apps to censor and monitor users, even abroad. The Study the Great Nation app, for example, which the CCP requires certain professions to use, comes with a back door through which the government can view a cell phone’s data. Authorities in Xinjiang employed a comprehensive database to track the movements, mobile app usage, and even electricity and gasoline consumption of inhabitants in the region. They use a new smart religion app in some areas to track church attendance. The United States should widely publicize these back doors in China and work with a new Chinese government to disable them as soon as possible.
<P>	現行中國政府利用微信（WeChat）及其他應用程式，對用戶進行審查與監控，其範圍甚至延伸至海外。例如，中共要求特定職業使用之「學習強國」應用程式，即具備政府可藉以查閱手機數據之後門。新疆當局曾利用一項全方位資料庫，追蹤該地區居民之行動、應用程式使用紀錄，甚至電力與汽油消耗量。在部分地區，當局亦使用新型智慧宗教應用程式以監控教會出席情況。美國應廣泛揭露中國境內之此類後門，並與新任中國政府合作，務求盡速將其禁用。
<P>	The CCP’s various systems of recruiting private actors to spy and report on neighbors should promptly end as well. <P>	The government continued to use the “double-linked household” system in Xinjiang developed through many years of use in Tibet. This system divides towns and neighborhoods into units of 10 households each, with the households in each unit instructed to watch over each other and report on “security issues” and poverty problems to the government, thus turning average citizens into informers.
<P>	政府於新疆持續沿用「雙聯戶」制度，該制度係經多年於西藏之實踐而發展而成。此體系將城鎮與社區劃分為每單元十戶之單位，並指示該單元內之住戶相互監視，並向政府舉報「安全問題」與貧困狀況，從而將一般公民轉化為告密者。
<P>	In addition, the CCP pays individuals to report on each other. According to the State Department, in Tibet, “The maximum reward for information leading to the arrests of social media users deemed disloyal to the government increased to 300,000 renminbi ($42,800), six times the average per capita GDP.” They are to report relatives abroad or lose their government benefits. In Xinjiang, the government goes further, requiring Uyghur families to accept government “home stays,” in which the CCP sends strangers to live in their homes and monitor their observance of religion for signs of “extremism.” In his book, Nury Turkel describes this program, Chinese Becoming Family, which amounts to party spies masquerading as relatives. The CCP moves them into Uyghur homes “like a hostile army being billeted on a restive population in the old days of empire.” At a morning flag-raising ceremony, they forced the affected Uyghur families to chant, “We love our relatives. We welcome our relatives. We don’t need to ask our relatives any questions.”

  ### Bringing Oppressive Leaders to Justice/追究壓迫領導人之法律責任
<P>	It is important to restore the rights and freedoms of the Chinese people as soon as possible. In advance, the United States should work with new Chinese leaders to plan and publicly announce restorative justice measures as soon as the regime collapses. These should include Nuremberg-style tribunals and truth commissions to document and expose CCP atrocities and secret policies, directives, and deliberations; punish egregious perpetrators; and allow restitution. In post-Nazi Germany, post-apartheid South Africa, post-genocide Rwanda, and elsewhere, they have proven to be important in establishing a sense of justice and averting extralegal retributions. These investigations and proceedings will take time, even years, to carry out. In the short term, the steps outlined in this section can help alleviate suffering and bring optimism to the millions of victims of Chinese communism. This would go far in promoting stability within the aggrieved communities.
<P>	儘速恢復中國人民之權利與自由至關重要。美國應預先與新任中國領導人合作，擬定並於政權崩解之際，即時公開宣布修復式正義（restorative justice）措施。其內容應包含仿效紐倫堡審判之法庭，以及旨在記錄並揭露中共暴行、秘密政策、指令與決策過程之真相委員會；藉此懲處嚴重罪行之肇事者，並容許受害者獲得賠償。誠如納粹德國、種族隔離時期之南非、大屠殺後之盧安達及其他地區之經驗所示，此類機制對於確立正義感及避免法外報復，具有重要作用。此等調查與程序之執行，恐需耗費多年時日。然而，於短期而言，本節所述之步驟將有助於緩解中國共產主義數百萬受害者之痛苦，並帶來樂觀展望。此舉對於促進受壓迫社群內之穩定，將有極大裨益。
  
  ##  Conclusion/结论
<P>	The following is a summary of important US policy actions that would be applicable to both autonomous regions and the rest of China. They have precedents in American policy and aim to promote stability as well as fundamental rights and freedoms. 
<P>	以下摘要列舉適用於中國自治區及全境之重要美國政策行動。該等行動皆有美國政策之先例，旨在促進穩定，並捍衛根本權利與自由。
<P>	1. Be prepared with guidelines—such as the five principles the Bush administration issued on September 4, 1991— setting forth conditions for US support of new republics emerging from the dissolution of the PRC: self-determina tion consistent with democratic principles, recognition of existing borders, support for democracy and rule of law, preservation of human rights and rights of national minorities, and respect for international law and obligations. The US should widely disseminate the guidelines.
<P>	預先擬定準則——例如布希政府於1991年9月4日發布之五項原則——藉此設定美國支持由中華人民共和國解體後所產生之新共和國之條件：須符合民主原則之民族自決、承認既有邊界、支持民主與法治、維護人權及少數民族權利，並尊重國際法與國際義務。美國應廣泛傳播此類準則。
<P>	2. Make a preliminary determination whether it would serve American interests for a specific autonomous region to pursue independence. As noted in the Kurdistan example, there may be unresolvable, preexisting border disputes, determined violent resistance by a neighbor, or some other unsurmountable reason that would make independence unviable.
<P>	進行初步評估，研判特定自治區追求獨立是否符合美國利益。誠如庫德斯坦（Kurdistan）案例所示，可能存在無法解決之既有邊界爭議、鄰國之暴力抵抗，或任何其他導致獨立方案不可行之障礙。
<P>	3. Expect all of China’s autonomous regions to exercise the right to self-determination. The presence of a Han majority in a minority area or strong CCP control over many years in a particular region does not preclude a vote for an independent democratic republic, as the collapse of the Soviet empire showed.
<P>	預期中國所有自治區皆將行使自決權。誠如蘇聯帝國崩解所證，即便少數民族地區存在漢族多數人口，或特定地區長期受中共強勢控制，皆不排除該地投票建立獨立民主共和國之可能性。
<P>	4. Use skilled US diplomacy to renegotiate more robust autonomy guarantees for any autonomous regions that decide to remain in China or have no realistic alternative.
<P>	運用精湛之外交手段，為任何決定留在中國內或缺乏現實替代方案之自治區，重新談判並爭取更強而有力之自治保障。
<P>	5. Monitor the collapse of the CCP regime, which could follow the fast-paced, locally organized post–Cold War pattern or, as in South Sudan, cooperate with deliberate organization and supervision by an international team. In the latter case, be prepared to provide expert advice, logistical help, and validation of the outcome to ensure free and fair referen dums. The US and its allies should also be prepared to help organize and observe elections for new governments. 
<P>	監控中共政權之崩解過程。該過程可能遵循冷戰後快速且地方化組織之模式；亦可能如南蘇丹案例，於國際團隊之刻意組織與監督下進行合作。於後者情況下，美國應準備提供專家建議、後勤支援，並對結果進行驗證，以確保公投之自由與公正。此外，美國及其盟友亦應準備協助新政府之選舉組織與觀察工作。
<P>	6. Quickly recognize the new sovereign states and establish diplomatic relations with them. Prepare to help them gain China’s and the world’s acceptance. This will likely require complex US diplomacy to facilitate any mergers involving the new states, as occurred in German reunification, and in resolving border disputes, especially those concerning the China-India border.
<P>	迅速承認新主權國家，並與其建立外交關係。準備協助其獲得中國及國際社會之認可。此舉恐需運用複雜之外交手段，以促成涉及新興國家之合併（如德國統一案例），並解決邊界爭議，特別是涉及中印邊界之問題。
<P>	7. Recognize as legitimate leaders China and newly independent countries choose in free and fair elections and referendums, including former CCP members. Invite heads of state and parliamentary leaders for official visits to Washington within months after they take office to empower them and their democratic system, strengthen their country’s sovereignty, and facilitate bilateral relations.
<P>	承認由中國及新獨立國家透過自由、公正之選舉與公投所選出之合法領導人，其中亦應包含前中共成員。於領導人就職後數月內，應邀請其國家元首與議會領袖對華盛頓進行正式訪問，以賦予其權威並強化民主體制，鞏固國家主權，並促進雙邊關係。
<P>	8. Support the new republics’ entry as member states into the United Nations, the World Trade Organization, the Association of Southeast Asian Nations, and other key regional and international organizations to solidify their sovereignty. While full membership in NATO will not be available to these new democracies, regional defense alliances can and should embrace them.
<P>	支持新共和國加入聯合國、世界貿易組織（WTO）、東南亞國家協會（ASEAN）及其他關鍵區域與國際組織，以鞏固其主權地位。雖然這些新興民主國家無法取得北約（NATO）之正式成員資格，但區域性防禦聯盟可以、且應接納其成員。
<P>	9. Lead in ensuring priority human rights reforms in both the current autonomous regions and across China as soon as feasible. In addition to taking specific actions to end the human rights abuses and violations described in this chapter, as well as others that the United States may identify as priorities, begin implementing a plan to dismantle the CCP’s extensive surveillance system and ensure freedoms of religion and speech, in particular. These measures would honor America’s own fundamental foreign policy principles, alleviate resentment, and avert score settling against former oppressors that can lead to relentless cycles of violence and even civil war.
<P>	領導並確保在現有自治區及全中國境內，儘速優先推動人權改革。除採取具體行動以終止本章所述之侵犯人權行為（以及美國可能認定之其他優先事項）外，亦應開始執行拆解中共廣泛監控體系之計畫，並特別確保宗教與言論自由。此等措施將符合美國自身之基本外交政策原則，緩解憤恨情緒，並避免針對前壓迫者之私刑報復，以免陷入無休止之暴力循環，甚至引發內戰。
